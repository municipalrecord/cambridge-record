♻ How a page becomes verified, and stays that way

○ Unauditedwaiting for its first audit, which pays +3
◐ Audited ×1needs a blind second audit. You can't see the first auditor's verdicts until you file, and never your own page twice. +3, concurrence bonus +3 each.
● Verified ×2two independent auditors concur. Audits stay open forever: +3 to re-confirm, the full +10 standing bounty if you catch what everyone missed.
♻ Staleaudits pin the page's content hash. If the page changes, verification resets and full points return. Verification has a date, never a forever.

A discrepancy scores +0 on filing and +10 once a second auditor reproduces it (+10 if the page was already ● verified). Findings pay only when they're true. Reproducing someone else's flag pays +0. Disagreement between two audits is the audit of the audit: it's adjudicated in public, and whoever was right takes the finding points. (For now the site's maintainer adjudicates; a volunteer moderator tier will take this over as the program grows.)
