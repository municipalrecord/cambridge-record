Council requests & answers

What the council has asked the administration, and what it's still owed.

When the City Council wants something from the administration, a councillor files a Policy Order. If adopted, the request is logged as an Awaiting Report: an open question with a number. When the City Manager responds, the answer arrives on the City Manager's Agenda and the question closes. Every item in a chain shows its own timeline.

The City Manager reports all outstanding questions as a single bundled “Awaiting Report Status Update” line on the agenda: one item, no count. The clerk's own itemized list is above. A request is sometimes pruned at the start of a new term, on a vote of the council: 34 of the 99 carried on 18 December 2023 were gone by 8 January 2024. But it is not a clear-out and it does not always happen. The 2022 term started with requests from 2016, 2018 and 2019 still on the list, and item 19-66 survived both term changes. An old number means an old question, not a filing artefact.
