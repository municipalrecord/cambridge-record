The Docket, 1942–2015

Every action the City Council took from 1942 to 2015. 131,752 entries; 71,974 link to the city's scan of the document. Through 2003 this is the City Clerk's annual index volumes, which list actions the city never scanned, so the record here is wider than the scanned collection. From 2004 it is the Final Actions document the clerk publishes after every meeting.

The clerk's sections and numbering are kept as written. Which sections exist changes over the period: see the sections by year. The committee sections give way after 2003 to the item codes the clerk still numbers by today, which is why the later pages read differently. The charter does not change: Plan E was adopted by referendum in 1940, the first council and city manager took office in January 1942, and it was not amended until 2021, then amended by substitution in 2025.
