Missing minutes

Meetings that happened, whose minutes are not published on the city's portals: 626 as of 2026-09-10. Minutes may exist unpublished; each entry is a candidate for a public records request to the City Clerk or the board's department.

Keeping minutes isn't optional: charter §9-7 and Council Rule 28 require every body to keep agendas and minutes. This page is a running audit of those obligations.

What it does not count: a council committee publishes its minutes as a committee report accepted on the City Council floor rather than on its own portal page, so a hearing whose report reached the floor is not listed here. Nor are meetings that were cancelled, meetings held in the last 90 days (minutes are approved at a later meeting, so they are not yet due), or meetings whose minutes were posted as an ordinary attachment.

Standing gaps

  • Planning Board & BZA written public comment is never published on the portals (goes to staff email only). Records request
  • School Committee meetings before Feb 2021 are on legacy cpsd.us pages, not the portal
  • City Council, 2003 to 2015: 823 meetings set aside. The city listed every body in one table for that era, and committee meetings are counted under their own committee rather than here. Ceremonies and presentations have their own section below.
  • City Council, 2004 to 2015. The city's own minutes archive went offline and its links now lead nowhere; 219 files were recovered from the Internet Archive. Whether a given meeting's minutes were ever published is not answerable from any source we hold, so those dates are listed below without a reason rather than with a guessed one.
