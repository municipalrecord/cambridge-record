## Method
- **Readers:** Sonnet, blind (`READER_*.md`, `READ_PROMPT.md`). One change from 2023, in the plan before any reading: a transcript is cut into runs of at most 50 pages (lanes T, `READER_T.md`), each read once against the city's agenda outline, so a 200-page night is read once, not once per item lane. 287 runs over 11,811 pages; 414 reading lanes in all, 38 of them **double reads (seed 20221012)**.
- **Round 0** placed every stored roll before any grading: Opus placers read each night and wrote a ledger (`place/rows/`); 1,727 rolls placed, 398 added by hand, 760 held for cards; the lead settled the conflicts (`place/LEAD_SETTLED.md`) and the rows were applied (3,148 vote rows, 67 motion rows, 129 charter-right rows). Lane B carried the 2023 to 2025 page fixes to these years behind a guard; Lane D fetched the committee minutes, Calendars and specials.
- **From round 22 (C69 A):** a full fresh read of all 4,380 rows on f22 with no grade carried, then carry-forward only from round 22 on (`carry.py`). From round 25, graders ran as lean processes (`lean_lane.sh`, Opus, medium effort, Dan's C72), and from round 28 a detached scheduler kept 16 running (`rN/sched.sh`).
- **Graders:** Opus (`GRADER_*.md`, one note per round, `GRADER_R1.md` to `GRADER_R18.md`; `RULES.md` and `SOURCES.md` read whole). The lead read misses against the source and wrote re-classes with reasons (`rN/lead_reclass.json`).
- **Two scripted checks** run on every build from round 9: `kinds.py` (fault kinds rounds 1 to 8 found, with each false flag allowed by a ruling in `kinds_allow.json`) and `kinds_r10.py` (five more kinds). **The guard** (`guard.py`, `GUARD.md`) freezes the 940 rows graded exact on f8 and stops a build that gives any of them a new flag.
- **Carry-forward, card C61 B** (Dan, 23 October): a row counts as graded on a new build when the latest round graded it exact and every page a reader meets it on is unchanged since, by the guard's fingerprint (`carry.py`). **Card C64 A** (24 October) let unchanged benign rows carry too (`--pass-benign`). Every other row is read in full.
- **Tally rules** (lead, 23 October, in each `tallyN.py`): an unnamed second amendment is missing wherever graded benign; a suspension shown in the meeting page's council business block is benign (known-21-2); a letter that names no item is benign (letter-topic-only).
- **The ledger** (`final/ledger_final.py`, `final/ledger.json`): each row's grade from the latest round that graded it, with the lead re-classes and tally rules; it agrees with `r18/tally18.json` on every one of the 4,380 rows.
