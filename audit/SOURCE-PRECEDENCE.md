# Which source wins

The city's own records disagree with each other. That is permanent and not a
defect to be fixed — it is the material. What was missing was a ruling, so
every audit rediscovered the same disagreements and reported them as news.

This is the ruling. **For vote tallies and dispositions, higher wins:**

| # | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `council minutes` | The approved record. The body voted to accept it. |
| 2 | `final actions sheet` | The clerk's same-day record. Names individual voters and differentiates real dissent. |
| 3 | `city portal` | The city's feed. Timely, not authoritative. |
| 4 | Engrossed / adopted order attestation | Legally operative for the ACT, unreliable for the TALLY. |

## Why the attested order ranks last for tallies

It looks the most official and is the most misleading. `CMA 2026-98`'s adopted
loan order reads "Adopted by a yea and nay vote: Yeas 9; Nays 0; Absent 0",
attested by the City Clerk. The actions sheet for the same June 1 vote records
8-1-0 with Al-Zubi voting no.

The actions sheet is right. On that date only 2 of 58 items carried any no
vote, so the sheet is recording differentiated votes rather than stamping a
template. "Yeas 9; Nays 0" is engrossment boilerplate printed regardless of
the count.

Trusting the attested order here would have erased a councillor's recorded
dissent — a worse error than the one it appeared to fix.

The order still governs **what the council did** (adopted, referred, tabled)
and whether an item is law. It just does not govern **who voted how**.

## What this obliges

- The generator resolves conflicts by this table, silently.
- Audits report only what this table CANNOT settle. A disagreement the rule
  resolves is not a finding and must not be raised as one.
- Changing the ranking is a one-line edit here. Everything downstream reads
  it rather than re-deciding.

## Known unresolvable

No council minutes exist after **2026-05-04** (the clerk has published none).
Until they do, every 2026 vote rests on rank 2 with nothing above it to check
against. That is a gap in the city's publishing, not in ours.
