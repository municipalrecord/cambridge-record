Every other body on the record

Cambridge has no single complete list of its own boards. The city publishes a directory; the Municipal Code establishes bodies the directory omits; and the council votes appointments to bodies that appear in neither. These are the three reconciled, so a body cannot go missing just because one source forgot it.
