Roster history

Where this comes from

Membership is drawn from the roll calls in the committee's own minutes: each term's roster is the set of members recorded voting. Who presided is read from the titles the minutes give them. Until 2026 the Mayor chaired the committee and it elected a Vice Chair from among its members; the city's new charter makes the Mayor an ordinary member, and in 2026 the committee elected its own Chair.

Thinner than the council's roster, which carries the date each seat was taken and left. Minutes before 2019 sit on the district's legacy pages, which this site has not yet collected, so terms earlier than 2018 are not shown here yet.
