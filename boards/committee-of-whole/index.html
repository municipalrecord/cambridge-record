Special Committee of the Whole on Charter Review

Formed at the council's request in POR 2024 #103 (August 5, 2024) to take up the Charter Review Committee's recommendations. It met five times between December 9, 2024 and March 28, 2025; the council adopted its motions in POR 2025 #35 (March 17) and again in POR 2025 #55 (April 14), then petitioned the General Court for a new charter in POR 2025 #57. That petition became the charter Cambridge voters approved on November 4, 2025. This committee has concluded its work.

The whole council sitting as a special committee — nine members, formed for the charter review and dissolved with it. Not one of the standing committees. Charge per the POR 2024 #103, as adopted August 5, 2024.

What it takes up

  • To discuss the Charter Review Committee's recommendations and plan the process moving forward;
  • To take votes in committee on proposed charter changes, including the method of selecting the Mayor, and report them to the City Council for adoption.
