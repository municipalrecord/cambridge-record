Public Safety Committee

A committee of five City Councillors. Three members are a quorum. Charge per the Rules of the City Council, adopted June 1, 2026.

What it takes up

  • To consider all matters affecting the public safety of Cambridge; and
  • To review matters relating to the performance, organization, and effectiveness of the Police and Fire Departments, Public Works Department, the Inspectional Services Department as it relates to public safety issues, and the Police Review Board.
