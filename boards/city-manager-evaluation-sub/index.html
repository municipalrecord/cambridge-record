City Manager Evaluation Subcommittee

An ad hoc subcommittee, not one of the standing committees, and not among the Mayor's 2026–27 committee assignments. One meeting is on the record — June 22, 2023, taking up the proposed annual performance review process for the City Manager.

A subcommittee of councillors, formed to work out how the council reviews the City Manager's performance. Charge per the Rules of the City Council — Government Operations, Rules & Claims.

What it takes up

  • To develop the process by which the City Council conducts the City Manager's annual performance review.
  • The subject sits inside a standing committee's charge: under the rules, matters relating to the hiring and evaluation of employees who report to the City Council belong to Government Operations, Rules & Claims — and the City Manager is one of the two officers the council appoints.
