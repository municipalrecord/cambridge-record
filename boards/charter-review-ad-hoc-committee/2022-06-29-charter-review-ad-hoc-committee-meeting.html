Charter Review Ad Hoc Committee · June 29, 2022

📄 📄 minutes

What the minutes record

  • Councillor Nolan called the meeting to order at 3:00 PM in the Ackermann Room. Its purpose, in the minutes’ words: “reviewing and selecting self-nominated residents and stakeholders to be on the Charter Review Committee.”
  • Present: Vice Mayor Mallon, Councillor Nolan, Councillor Toner and Mayor Siddiqui.
  • At 3:03 PM the committee moved into executive session, to discuss the reputation and character of applicants. The minutes print no roll call on that motion. The Mayor’s chief of staff and the Deputy City Clerk were also present.
  • In executive session, the minutes record, each member offered their top five candidates, and the members discussed reaching the final fifteen, naming alternates, and how to tell the candidates they had been selected. The minutes name no candidate.
  • The executive session adjourned at about 4:35 PM; the committee returned to open session with all members present at 4:38 PM.
  • At 4:40 PM Councillor Nolan moved to adjourn, and the motion passed unanimously. The minutes print no roll call.

The City Council adopted these minutes on May 12, 2025, 9 to 0, by the clerk’s certification on the minutes’ last page. The committee’s choice reached the council at its special meeting of July 20, 2022.
