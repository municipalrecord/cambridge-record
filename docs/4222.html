Solet, Members Gavin W. Kleespies, Paula A. Paris, Kyle Sheffield, Alternate COMMISSION Date: December 1, 2017 To: Louis A. DePasquale, City Manager From: Charles M. Sullivan, Executive Director MUS Re: Harvard Square Kiosk final landmark designation report and CHC recommendation Attached please find the Final Landmark Designation Report for the Harvard Square Kiosk. On October 5 the Cambridge Historical Commission voted unanimously to find that the kiosk meets the criteria in the ordinance for landmark designation and to instruct staff to forward the final report to the City Council with the Commission's positive recommendation for designation. If you have any questions, please let me know.
