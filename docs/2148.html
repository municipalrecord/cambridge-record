MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING Executed as of June 27 , 2016 To: Cambridge Planning Board; Ordinance Committee, Honorable City Council; This letter will serve as a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) and confirms the understandings with respect to the proposed development at 2551 Massachusetts Avenue (the "Project") that has been agreed to by 2551 Mass Ave LLC, developer of the Project, and the Neighborhood Review Committee ("NRC"), consisting of residents of the surrounding community as enumerated below, who signed the Stern, et al Petition. The design shall be as shown in the Exhibits A which is hereby incorporated by reference in this MOU. All future plans, construction, and related documents filed with the city related to this project shall be consistent with this MOU or require approval of the NRC. In conformance with Exhibit A and further in accordance with the agreement of 2551 Mass Ave LLC and the NRC, the design is to include the following major features: ·1. Unit count of 4 2. Concealed garage parking for one car per unit at grade 3. No ground floor retail 4. Three stories not to exceed the Residence 8 zoning requirement of 5. setbacks as shown in Exhibit A . 6. Lightwells (originally shown) at Mass Ave frontage will be replaced with planted shrubbery beds to be maintained by the Developer or the Condo Association. 7. Curbside planting with trees plantings to be consistent with best City of Cambridge standards. 8. Mass Ave and Richard Ave metal fencing to be agreed upon by both parties to this agreement. 9. Fencing between 2551 and 7 Richard Ave to be 6 foot or more, wood/pvc structure to be agreed upon by both parties. 10. materials are described in Exhibit A. 11. Window type and style to be noted in Exhibit A. 12. The Developer agrees to retain a color consultant to assist with options for the exterior colors of the building suggested by the Cambridge Historical Commission and to abide by reasonable recommendations made. 1
