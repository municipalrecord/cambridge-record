ATTACHMENTE-I February 1, 2017 Dear Mayor Simmons & Honorable City Council, I am writing to you on the eve of our second Ordinance Committee hearing regarding the Central Square Restoration Petition to address a few issues and hopefully create a clear path for potential ordination. As many of you know this process of envisioning Central Square has been a long one. Much of what the CSBA and Sater Family hope to achieve with this petition is backed by decades of study. We have worked with everyone including those whom in the past had seemed at odds with our goals and vision. To that last point I want to address a memo that has been produced by the Cambridge Residents Alliance regarding several key changes they would like to see. 1) They have suggested that the petition only grant a 4.0 FAR to the "BB" district of Central Square. This point is congruent with C2 and CDD's memo, and thus we absolutely agree that this is a change that should be made. 2) They have asked that the petition state that the height doesn't change but for the affordable housing. Our petition has nothing to do with height as that regulation has and will remain unchanged. Thus we do not think it should be included in our petition. Their proposal that was included in an earlier version of their statement has been stricken at our mutual agreement. 3) The CresA also now agrees with the parking recommendation of CDD and thus this is moot as we also agree with CDD. 4) The CresA would like bars limited to the side streets within the BB district. We agree. 5) The CresA would like to limit labs. We think this might be a worthwhile goal, but it is not included in our petition and per the CDD recommendation we think this issue aught to be brought up separately. 6) The CresA would like the petition to include lighting as a design guideline for rooftop spaces. This comports with both CDD and Planning Board recommendations and we also happily agree. In summation, we seem to all agree on nearly every aspect of the petition as written with suggested amendments. The only remaining point of contention left has to do with Formula Business Regulation. With all due respect to our Solicitor, I implore this council to not go lightly into that dark night on this issue. We are not zoning for ownership but aesthetic. We do not violate the Dormant Commerce Clause, nor do we offend uniformity. I have prepared a memo addressing her concerns and I urge everyone to at least give it some thought (coming to an email near you). There are communities that have had this type of ordinance upheld in court. it is possible. Please do not forget that one of the main goals of this petition was to address the long overdue problems caused by the current Fast Food Cap. If this cap remains and the provisions therein Central Square will always have vacancies holding out for higher rents to the detriment of all. The narrative spun at the license commission will always be an eye roller and we will be perpetuating a anachronistic practice that further handicaps Central Square. Give this type of regulation a chance and let Central Square be an example to the rest of the City. We all agree Central Square needs help. We all agree that the financial window is closing rapidly for local owners to be able to fund worthwhile projects that we all want. We all agree that if we can't find a way work CC: Everyone Regards, Patrick W. Barrett lIE

AITACHMENT E-2 Cambridge Ordinance Committee Re: Central Square Restoration Petition Requested Amendments Date: February 2, 2017 Dear Co-Chairs, We humbly submit the following widely agreed upon amendments to the Central Square Restoration Petition. We ask that this be entered into the record and considered by this committee to be included in any recommendation to the Council. • Strike 20.304.3 (4) This provision extended a potential FAR limit to 4.0 to areas outside of the BB district. We acknowledge the concern of the neighborhood and therefor request this line be stricken, limiting a 4.0 FAR be limited the BB Business District only. • Amend 20.304 (6) to read: "6. FAR Exemption for Rooftop Spaces The intent of this section is to allow public and private usage of rooftop spaces that would otherwise impinge on gross floor area calculations within a structure that would enhance the surrounding community. Notwithstanding any other provisions of this Ordinance, the Gross Floor Area of'open-air spaces on the roofs of buildings, such as roof gardens, terraces, walkways. including open or enclosed egresses, covered Staircase head-houses, and observation spaces shall be exempted from Gross Floor Area and FAR limitations upon the granting of a special permit by the Planning Board. In granting any such special permit that would allow active usage of rooltop spaces for reasons other than safety, security, building maintenance and/or building operations, the Planning Board shall include in their review and consideration the extent to which any active use of rooftop spaces will negatively affect residents within the Centrai Square Overlay District and in abutting Base Zoning Districts and inay prohibit or limit such active use and place further requirements and conditions on the design and operational aspects of spaces exempted pursuant to this Section, including (i) by limiting hours of'operation, lighting, noise, the range of activities permitted, visual and privacy impacts of the anticipated use on residents, signage; and (ii) by requiring other measures to ensure that the use of the space is consistent with the intent of this Section and in harmony with the purposes of this Ordinance." • Amend 20.304.5 (3) Restrieted Uses to reflect its limitation to the BB Business District Only. • Amend 20.304.6 Waiver of Parking and Loading Requirements to refleci the recommended language provided by the Cambridge Community Develop Department and recommendation of the Planning Board. • We ask that the definition and language pertaining to Formula Businesses be stricken, and that we preserve a limitation of Bank frontage and removal of the Fast Food Cap. • We ask that a working group be assembled to continue the work of crafting a viable Formula Business Ordinance with a stated timeline of completion no later than June 1, 2017. Respectfully, Patrick W. Barrett II
