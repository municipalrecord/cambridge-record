CITY OF CAMBRIDGE Traffic, Parking and Transportation 344 Broadway Cambridge, Massachusetts 02139 SIVICO REGIMIN www.cambridgema.gov/traffic Phone: [phone removed] Joseph E. Barr, Director Fax: [phone removed] Stephanie McAuliffe, Assistant Director for Parking Management Brooke McKenna, Assistant Director for Street Management January 28, 2019 Leo Roy, Commissioner Department of Conservation and Recreation 251 Causeway Street, Ninth Floor Boston, MA 02114 RE: Cambridge City Council Request for Improved Signs/Markings on Alewife Brook Parkway Dear Commissioner Roy: At its meeting on October 29, 2018, the Cambridge City Council passed the attached Policy Order, requesting that we work with the Department of Conservation and Recreation and the State Police to address the concerns expressed about vehicles blocking the exit from the Fresh Pond Mall onto Alewife Brook Parkway, particularly during times when the Parkway is congested. Specifically, the order requests the installation of "Don't Block the Box" signs and/or pavement markings to attempt to address this issue. As Alewife Brook Parkway is under the jurisdiction of your agency, 1 am formally passing along this request to you and your staff, so that you can investigate the situation and determine what interventions are appropriate to address this request. As the order notes, the City Council has requested that we report back to them on the outcome of this request, so please let me know when you have evaluated this issue and have a response that can be shared with the City Council. If there is any assistance that the City of Cambridge can provide in evaluating and/or addressing this situation, please don't hesitate to contact me at [phone removed] or jbarr@cambridgema.gov. Thank you very much for your consideration of this request. Very truly yours, Joseph E. Barr Director cc: J. Parenti, Department of Conservation and Recreation Attachment (1)
