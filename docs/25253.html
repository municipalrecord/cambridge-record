CAMBRIDGE CITY COUNCIL Patricia Nolan City Councillor Re: Filing Reconsideration For APP 2025 #19 Dear Clerk LeBlanc, It is my intent to move reconsideration of the vote taken on May 12, 2025, adopting Application and Petitions 2025 #19 (APP 2025 #19), an application requesting permission for a curb cut at the premises numbers 49 Alpine Street ("That the Commissioner of Public Works is hereby authorized to grant a permit for the installation of a curb cut at the premises numbered 49 Alpine Street, Cambridge, Massachusetts; said authorization is conditioned upon use of material specified by the Department of Public Works and upon permit requirements being met, including an ADA compliant design."). Thank you for your attention to this matter. Sincerely, sial Patricia Nolan Cambridge City Councillor CITY HALL, CAMBRIDGE, MASSACHUSETTS 02139 Email: pnolan@cambridgema.gov
