CMA 2026-258 IN CITY COUNCIL September 14, 2026 To the Honorable, the City Council: I am hereby requesting the appropriation of the Federal Fiscal Year 2026 Metropolitan Area Planning Council (MAPC) Community Connections Grant, in the amount of $308,364.80 to the Grant Fund Transportation Department Extraordinary Expenditures account. Fund will support the replacement of nine (9) Bluebikes stations in the City of Cambridge, totaling one- hundred-sixty-seven (167) station docks. The project includes purchase and installation of all station equipment. Very truly yours, Yi-An Huang City Manager
