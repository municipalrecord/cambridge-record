APP 2017 #67 · Agenda item attachment · Oct 2 2017

An petition was received from MIT List Visual Arts Center requesting permission for a temporary banner across 20 Ames Street to promote their upcoming exhibition Heimo Zobernig: chess painting. The temporary banners will be hung from October to July 2018

MIT List Visual Arts Center September 25, 2017 Cambridge City Council 795 Massachusetts Avenue Cambridge, MA 02139 To Whom It May Concern: I am writing from the MIT List Visual Arts Center, the contemporary museum at MIT, to indicate interest in displaying banners on Ames Street, where this List Center is located, in order to promote our upcoming exhibition Heimo Zobernig: chess painting. The design of the banners is in keeping with the List Center's logo and design standards. Every other banner has our logo, our name, and the exhibition title in blue, black, and white. The remaining banners have a checkerboard pattern with our name in blue at the bottom. This design ties into the title of the exhibition "chess painting." Enclosed please find detailed mock ups of the banners including a rendering of them on Ames Street. We thank you for your consideration. Sincerely yours, Betsy Willett 20 Arnes Street, Bldg. E15-109 Combridge, MA 02139 Phone [phone removed] Fax 617258 7265 listart.mit.edu

Attachments shall be made with proper hardware and shall only be attached with non-corrosive metal clamps or brackets. 2. Banners attached to poles shall not exced a total of 1152 square inches, approximately 18 inches x 48 inches, 24 inches x 36 inches, or 24 inches x 48. The actual size is subject to required clearances. 3. Banners cannot be attached to wood utility poles. 4. Banners shall have a minimum clearance of: a. 16 feet over a roadway b. 12 feet over a sidewalk 5. Banners shall not interfere with or obstruct, or otherwise block any roadway signs, lighting, or traffic signals. 6. The City reserves the right to remove, or order to be removed any banner deemed unsafe, not properly installed, or creating a hazard and the applicant may be subject to costs. Planter 7.
