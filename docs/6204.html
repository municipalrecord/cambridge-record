1 ELECTRICAL DEPARTMENT INTEROFFICE MEMO To Louis A. DePasquale, City Manager From: Stephen J Lenkauskas, City Electrician Date: March 14, 2019 Sub: City Council Policy Order 18-86 of July 30, 2018 In June 2014 the City began an effort to reduce energy consumption regarding outdoor lighting. The first phase of this project was to convert the approximately 5000 city owned roadway street lights to LED using 4000K LED fixtures. Included in this phase of the project was the installation of a city operated adaptive lighting control system. This system enables the city to apply schedules and control the intensity of the light by dimming the fixtures at a pre-determined time. This system with its flexibility also accommodates further installations to include parks, pathways and other control functions regarding outdoor lighting. The second phase of the project was to covert approximately 2500 of the city’s decorative roadway fixtures, parks, pathways and pedestrian scale fixtures to LED. This phase of the project utilized a combination of 4000K and 3000K fixtures. Decorative roadway fixtures were maintained at 4000K to stay consistent with phase one of the project. All parks, pathways and pedestrian scale fixtures are 3000K fixtures. Current fixture specifications require provisions to shield a fixture from emitting light in a particular direction. These shields have been installed on approximately 100 fixtures to date and continue to be installed when request are made and after an evaluation of the area in question has been conducted. The City currently operates 31 different schedules, the following is a description of the schedules that effect 4000K fixtures and have been applied throughout the city. Please take note that all schedules start at sunset and activate the fixture at or lower than 70% of the maximum intensity of the particular fixture. Schedules 8-31 cover control of on/off and dimming for fixtures in the 3000K range. Schedule 1 - On at Sunset 70% - 10PM Setback Dimmed to 35%, 3920 Fixtures. Schedule 2 - On at Sunset 30% - No Setback, 364 Fixtures. Schedule 3 - On at Sunset 70% - Midnight Setback Dimmed to 35%, 916 Fixtures Schedule 4 - On at Sunset 70% - 10PM Setback Dimmed to 35%, 175 Fixtures. (note: Schedule 4 reduces an 80 LED to perform as a 40 LED) Schedule 5 - On at Sunset 50% - 10PM Setback Dimmed to 25%, 25 Fixtures.
