An application was received from Brinn Sanders, requesting permission for a curb cut at the premises numbered 177 Hancock Street; said petition has received approval from Inspectional Services, Traffic, Parking and Transportation, Historical Commission and Public Works. Response has been received from the neighborhood association

1/8/25 To: Inspection Services Department - Cambridge MA Cc: Cambridge City Council I am the owner of 177 Hancock St, Cambridge, MA. I purchased the home in August of 2024 with the intention of completing a historic renovation of the home to bring it back to its full potential. I have been working with Kelly Boucher Architecture on the project. We recently went through zoning processes with Mid Cambridge Historic and have a planned case for 1/9/25 with Cambridge Zoning. Through this process we were notified that we are able to add a driveway/parking pad by right given the age of our building and the fact that it is a 2-family that predates the zoning rule of a 5’ setback. We have the necessary 10’ (actually 10’9”) required to add the parking pad to the Northeast side of the house. In mid-December we started the process of submitting our Curb Cut Application. Under advisement from the Cambridge Assessor's Office and Inspection Services Department we provided notice to all abutters (64 units) of 177 Hancock St on 12/17/24 via certified mail. As of 1/7 we have received a ~10% response rate, including 3 approvals, 1 letter of support from an owner who recently sold but was still listed as an official abutter, and 2 disapprovals. Prior to this official notice we had been in communication with the direct abbutters (185 Hancock, 176 Hancock & 390 Broadway) about our plans for our overall project and offered multiple opportunities to engage on developing a plan that would be agreeable to all, we were not taken up on our offer to meet. The reason we are seeking this curb cut is to be able to access the driveway that we are able to add by right per city zoning. This curb cut would also be in keeping with precedent for parking at other single-family & 2-family residences in the greater mid-Cambridge neighborhood, including multiple houses on Hancock St. The addition of curb cuts also recently were added at a development project just down the street at 134-136 Hancock St within the last 24 mos. Overall our curb cut will add 1 net additional parking space to the neighborhood. I look forward to your response and remain open to answer any questions or comments. Thank you for your consideration. Brinn Sanders LETTER PROVIDED WITH ABBUTER FORM:
