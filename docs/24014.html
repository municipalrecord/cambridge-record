OFFICE OF THE CITY CLERK CITY OF CAMBRIDGE [phone removed] FAX: [phone removed] DIANE P. LEBLANC PAULA M. CRANE CITY CLERK DEPUTY CITY CLERK April 2, 2025 Dear Honorable Mayor Simmons, Vice Mayor McGovern, and Councillors, The City Council agenda for March 31, 2025, listed the Minutes of the City Council - Special Meeting/Executive Session-February 10, 2025. In error, the minutes for February 3, 2025, were included in the agenda packet twice, and the minutes from February 10, were not included. The Minutes of the City Council - Special Meeting/Executive Session-February 10, 2025 will be on the April 7, 2025 agenda and should be accepted and placed on file. To ensure that the City Council record is accurate, I request that you rescind the vote taken on March 31, 2025, to accept the minutes from February 10, 2025, as amended and place them on file. Best, Diane LeBlanc City Clerk CITY HALL, 795 MASSACHUSETTS AVENUE, CAMBRIDGE, MASSACHUSETTS 02139
