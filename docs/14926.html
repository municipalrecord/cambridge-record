CMA 2023 #128 · Agenda item attachment · May 8 2023

A communication transmitted from Yi-An Huang, City Manager, relative to a recommendation that the City Council approve an Order to take by “friendly” eminent domain proceeding parcels of land located at 319 Webster Avenue, Cambridge (as shown on Cambridge Assessors Map 81, Parcel 21), 333 Webster Avenue, Cambridge (as shown on Cambridge Assessors Map 81, Parcel 23), and 455 Columbia Street (as shown on Cambridge Assessors Map 81, Parcel 16) (collectively “Cambridge Parcels”), and approve an Order authorizing the purchase of a parcel of land located at 94 Webster Avenue, Somerville (as shown on Somerville Assessors Map 96, Parcel B-7) (“Somerville Parcel”) (collectively, the “Premises”), all of which are owned by Webster Avenue Ventures LLC (“WV”)

1 City of Cambridge Agenda Item 5d IN CITY COUNCIL May 8, 2023 ORDER AUTHORIZING PURCHASE WHEREAS: By Section 30 of Chapter 43 and Section 14 of Chapter 40 of the General Laws, all as amended, it is provided that the Cambridge City Council may purchase in the name of the City for any municipal purpose any land within or without the limits of the City not already appropriated for public use; and WHEREAS: The City Manager of Cambridge and City Council, in order to provide for municipal operations and other general municipal uses, have approved and authorized that the City should under the provisions of the aforesaid Chapters of the General Laws purchase and hold for a municipal service facility, office use and all accessory uses thereto or any other municipal purposes the parcel of land located at 94 Webster Ave, Cambridge and Somerville, County of Middlesex, Massachusetts, currently owned of record by Judith A. Scollon, Trustee of A&B Realty Trust udt Dated April 29, 1983, and recorded at Book 22916, Page 390, and filed as Document 638757 as may be amended of record (to be conveyed to Webster Avenue Ventures LLC), as hereinafter bounded and described and any buildings and trees thereon; and WHEREAS: An appropriation duly made by the City Council on May 8, 2023, is sufficient to cover the estimated purchase expenses of acquisition as required by the aforesaid Sections of Chapters 40 and 43; WHEREAS: by virtue of and in pursuance of the authority conferred by said Chapters of the General Laws and by every other power and authority it thereto enabling, the City of Cambridge by its City Council does hereby purchase the land located at 94 Webster Avenue, Somerville, County of Middlesex, Massachusetts and hereinafter described, and all privileges and appurtenances thereto belonging as well as all trees and all structures thereon, excepting any easement of record, for a municipal service facility, office use, and all accessory uses thereto or any other municipal purposes and for all purposes and uses accessory thereto.
