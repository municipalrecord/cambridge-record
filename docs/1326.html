1. Ground level, pedestrian friendly retail and other non-residential uses for all new construction • Implement zoning sub-district identical to the Northern Mass Av Sub- district from Roseland down to Chauncy/Everett • Ground level non-residential is required for 75% of frontage • No ground level labs, manufacturing, assembly, etc. • Banks limited to 25’ wide • Increase required non-res depth to 40’ (before parking can begin) • Incentives for more gracious retail space
Page 19 of 29
2. Give better design guidance so we get buildings that fit in with the area • Expand the purpose paragraph to specifically recommend or require that buildings be Compatible in Context • Update or replace the Urban Design Guidelines Handbook to show real buildings in the area that successfully do this (not just Victorian) • Goal is to save developers and us time, money and aggravation
Page 20 of 29
3. Re-widen the sidewalks over the next 20 years as the avenue gets redeveloped • All new buildings are currently required to have 5’ front setback, or they can match the non-setback of an adjacent building • We propose to eliminate the exception. This is the only way to re- widen the sidewalks. • Note: This will create a jigsaw effect in some places for a period of time. That is not a bad thing.
Page 21 of 29
4. Avoid new buildings on Mass Av towering over existing homes behind them • We need appropriate rear set backs behind new construction • Residential B rear setback is 25’ plus 1’ additional for every 4’ the lot depth exceeds 100’ up to a total of 35’ • Business A-2 setback requirement is the greater of 20’ or 2/3 the height of the rear wall of the new building. • Rule should be the greater of the two zoning requirements when a parcel or parcels spanning more than one zone is treated as a single development site
Page 22 of 29
5. Provide parking for our merchants and protect side streets from more compaction • Our inner streets are already congested due to apartment buildings, multi- family buildings and institutions like Lesley, therefore: • Require NO LOSS OF EXISTING PARKING. • Require new residential buildings to have one parking space per dwelling unit as per current zoning. • Use development bonuses, variances and special permits to incentivize developers to build more than the 1:1 so that any surplus can be rented to local merchants, service providers and others. • How about a discreet, enclosed parking garage! (with charging stations)
Page 23 of 29
6. No Stilt Buildings! • Clarify that NO ground level parking (i.e. not just structured) directly facing Mass Av is a permitted use.
Page 24 of 29
7. Retain variance requirement for F&B uses • MAPOCO is zoned Business A-2 now, so Fast Order Food requires a variance. • If we adopt the North Mass Av sub-district language, which permits Fast Order Food as a required ground floor use, we want to carve out MAPOCO so we keep our status quo. • This is not snobbery – issues include noise, smells, garbage plan, litter, in & out traffic patterns, adjacency.
Page 25 of 29
8. Encourage more Cambridge Local First type businesses (vs. national chain outlets). • Right now the by-right threshold for leased premises is 5,000 sq in total (i.e. including basement and second floor space accessed through the premises). • Many independent shops can’t afford large premises and high rents. • We want to encourage more stores and more creative use of second stories and basements, so we propose 2,000 sf by-right counting only the ground level. In other words, a store could have an entrance at grade and an 8,000 sf second story or basement (like Abbey). • 25’ wide storefront x 60’ typical depth = 1500 sf

9. Ensure that Identified Historical Structures are not canyoned by new construction. • Require minimum 10’ setbacks on new construction adjacent to Identified Historical Structures • Require corner transitions on abutting properties to preserve the visual impact of historical buildings • Require appropriate massing set backs
Page 28 of 29
10. Preserve the scale of the neighborhood Encourage gracious retail spaces • Delete paragraph that allows for 50’ height building, and replace the language to say that overall building height can increase above the 45’ allowed in Business A-2 when the first floor to second floor elevation exceeds 15’ but only up to a maximum of 50’ total building height.
