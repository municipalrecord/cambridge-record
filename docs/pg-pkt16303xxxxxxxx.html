That the Cambridge City Council go on record congratulating Transition House on the occasion of its 50th anniversary and extending its deepest gratitude for five decades of leadership and service to survivors of domestic violence and their families.

RES 2026-97 FIRST IN COUNCIL June 8, 2026 City of Cambridge MAYOR SIDDIQUI WHEREAS: Transition House is celebrating 50 years of providing safety, support, advocacy, and hope to survivors of domestic violence and their families in Cambridge and throughout the region; and WHEREAS: Founded in Cambridge in 1976, Transition House was the first domestic violence shelter on the East Coast; and WHEREAS: Transition House has provided emergency shelter, housing assistance, advocacy, prevention programs, and supportive services that have empowered thousands of survivors and their children to build lives free from violence; and WHEREAS: Transition House has remained steadfast in its commitment to social justice, dignity, and safety, while continuing to evolve its programs to meet the changing needs of survivors and the broader community; and WHEREAS: The dedicated staff, volunteers, board members, advocates, and supporters of Transition House have demonstrated extraordinary compassion, leadership, and perseverance in the service of others; and WHEREAS: Transition House has played a vital role in Cambridge's network of care, partnering with residents, community organizations, and public agencies to advance awareness, prevention, and healing, and pioneered a movement that transformed how communities respond to domestic violence; and WHEREAS: The City of Cambridge is proud to recognize the profound and lasting impact Transition House has had on generations of individuals and families seeking safety, stability, and opportunity; now therefore be it RESOLVED: That the Cambridge City Council go on record congratulating Transition House on the occasion of its 50th anniversary and extending its deepest gratitude for five decades of leadership and service to survivors of domestic violence and their families; and be it further RESOLVED: That the City Clerk be and hereby is requested to forward a suitably engrossed copy of this resolution to Sarah Gyorog, the Executive Director of Transition House on behalf of the entire City Council.
