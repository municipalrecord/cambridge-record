Engineering Controls and Work Practices "To the extent feasible, engineering controls, administrative controls, and work practices shall be used to ensure that workers are not exposed to noise at or above 85 dBA as an 8-hr TWA. The use of administrative controls shall not result in exposing more workers to noise." WOSH CDC WORKPLACE SAFETY AND HEALTH Hearing Protectors • In the absence of Engineering Noise Control, Hearing Protectors are required • when exposure equals or exceeds 85 dBA as an 8-hr TWA • and protectors must be derated based upon the type of protector (25% muffs, 50% slow- recovery foam plugs, 75% other earplugs). • or ratings based upon ANSI S12.6 Method B. HOW LOUD IS THAT LEAF BLOWER IN THE WINDOW? • From Consumer Search.com • Experts say leaf blower noise levels should not measure over 65 decibels at 50 feet away from the machine, and in fact, many municipalities have outlawed gas-powered blowers with higher decibel levels. In any case, experts recommend using ear protection for models over 65 dBA. Manufacturers are struggling to make gas blowers that fall under 65 decibels, but with limited success NOSH CDC WORKPLACE SAFETY AND HEALTH HOW LOUD IS THAT LEAF BLOWER IN THE WINDOW? • Current state of noise limits for leaf blowers • From Consumer Search.com • Leaf blower/vacs are more controversial than you might think. More than half of our Web searches for "leaf blowers" turned up sites that call for their ban. Due to noise concerns, many counties have already limited the hours residents can use leaf blowers, and several have outlawed gas-powered models altogether. • Opponents say the noise level of gas-powered blowers is unbearable ? Not just irritating, but able to inflict permanent hearing damage. WOSH CDC WORKPLACE SAFETY AND HEALTH
