A communication transmitted from Louis A. DePasquale, City Manager, relative to a request for approval of an application for a Jitney License to USA Guided Tours Boston, LLC

City of Cambridge Agenda Item Number IN CITY COUNCIL July 29, 2019 WHEREAS: A petition was received by the License Commission dated May 15, 2019 by USA Guided Tours Boston, LLC, owned by Claudio Herrera, [address removed], 5th Floor, Suite 5054, Boston, MA 02116, requesting a jitney license for the operation of sightseeing tours between Boston and Lexington with a stop in Harvard Square, for the carriage of passengers for hire over certain public ways in the City of Cambridge; and WHEREAS: The said sightseeing tours would operate a Thirty-four (34) passenger tour bus Monday- Sunday between the hours of 10:30 am and 11:30 am, with the vehicle being stored in Woburn; and WHEREAS: In accordance with the provisions of Section 5.20.360(B) of Chapter 5.20 of the Municipal Code of Cambridge, a public hearing was held on June 26, 2019 with said License Commission and their recommendation for approval was transmitted by the City Manager to the City Council at the July 29, 2019 City Council meeting; now therefore be it

ORDERED: That a license is granted to USA Guided Tours Boston, LLC, owned by Claudio Herrera, 75 Arlington Street, 5th Floor, Suite 5054, Boston, MA 02116 under the provisions of Chapter 159A of the Massachusetts General Laws for a jitney license over the following public ways in the City of Cambridge: Entering Cambridge (To Drop -off passengers in Harvard Square): At approximately 10:15 am, from Boston (over the Harvard Bridge), the bus will enter Cambridge, travel westbound on Massachusetts Avenue to Harvard Square, and around 10:30 am drop-off passengers at the existing Tour Bus Drop-Off/Pick-Up location on the south side of Mt. Auburn Street, between Holyoke Street and Plympton Street. After Drop-off (to store bus): After dropping-off passengers, because USA Guided Tours has no location to park the tour bus for approximately an hour in Harvard Square, they propose to travel back to Boston with the empty bus from Massachusetts Avenue eastbound, over the Harvard Bridge, to Boston. Entering Cambridge (To Pick-Up passengers in Harvard Square and traveling to Lexington and Concord): At approximately 11:15 am, returning from Boston with the empty bus (over the Harvard Bridge), the bus will enter Cambridge, travel westbound on Massachusetts Avenue to Harvard Square, and pick-up the passengers at approximately 11:30 am at the Mt. Auburn Tour Bus Drop-Off/Pick-Up zone where they had earlier dropped them off. After picking up the passengers at approximately 11:30 am, the bus will turn left onto Massachusetts Avenue, to Garden Street, to Concord Avenue to Alewife Brook Parkway, to Route 2 westbound, to Lexington and Concord. From Lexington and Concord, to return to Boston, USA Guided Tours indicated that they will not travel though Cambridge to travel back to Boston; and be it further

ORDERED: That the license is granted with the following conditions: 1. The Mt. Auburn Tour Bus Drop-Off/Pick-Up location is occasionally reported to Traffic, Parking & Transportation (TP&T) as full. Nonetheless, TP&T believes that a probationary period for USA Guided Tours can be permitted. TP&T recommends a 6 month trial, followed by a check-in and report by USA Guided Tours to TP&T on how the route and Mt. Auburn Street Tour Bus Drop-Off/Pick-Up location is working, including any traffic citations, incidents, complaints, number of passengers, etc. 2. TP&T believes that at any time if problems arise, USA Guided Tours shall meet with the TP&T and other City departments to resolve the issue. 3. TP&T maintains the right to change or cancel the route at any time if it is determined that it is causing adverse impacts to traffic in Cambridge. 4. It is important that USA Guided Tours not illegally park in Harvard Square after Dropping-Off passengers at the Mt. Auburn Street Tour Bus Drop- Off/Pick-up location. 5. No circling around Harvard Square with an empty bus will be permitted directly after dropping-off passengers, except to directly travel back to Boston or a parking location approved by TP&T in the future. 6. After dropping-off passengers at the Mt. Auburn Tour Bus Drop-Off/Pick-Up Zone, USA Guided Tours must return to Boston as described above, however, TP&T recommends that USA Guided Tours continually seek a legal parking location on private property in the vicinity of Harvard Square (such as Allston) to minimize the need for empty bus trips back and forth daily thru Cambridge. If a parking location is secured, USA Guided Tours should inform TP&T and the License Commission and amend their jitney route. 7. No double parking or stopping in a bicycle lane or travel lane or blocking traffic on Mt. Auburn Street or on any Cambridge street will be allowed. 8. All loading and unloading of passengers must occur within the designated Tour Bus Drop-Off/Pick-Up location on Mt. Auburn Street. 9. No illegal U-turns shall be done. Changing of directions should be conducted as indicated on the routing plan. 10. This Department should approve any routing or stop changes to the service; and be it further ORDERED: That the City Council reserves the right to revoke the license granted in the event of the breach of any conditions outlined above.
