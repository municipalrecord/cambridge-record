CITY OF CAMBRIDGE 4 Capital Budget Loan Authorization Bond Schedule Bond Issuance Debt Service Payments & Spending The City’s capital budget is a plan for future capital program spending. Loan orders for projects in the capital budget require City Council approval in the form of a loan authorization. A bond schedule is organized based on the cash flow needs of each individual project, ensuring the City only issues new debt when it is needed. For the life of the bond, the City is obligated to pay principal and interest payments, or debt service. Once bonds are sold, 95% of the bond issuance should be spent within 2 years. Bond issuances create debt, which can be structured on 5-, 10-, or 20-year terms, in exchange for the funds for the project. Bonding for capital projects is a multi-step process that involves authorizing a loan, issuing the bond, paying down debt, and spending
