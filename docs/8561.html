Attn: Cambridge City Council 795 Massachusetts Avenue Cambridge, MA 02139 The Honorable, Cambridge City Council, The Central Square Business Improvement District is bringing together businesses to safely and warmly welcome back visitors to Central Square. Under one unified voice and message BID aims to create an atmosphere of positivity and safety in the face of unprecedented challenges. Through bold color, helpful iconography, and a warm hello, this campaign seeks to alert visitors that Central Square is ready to welcome them back safely and is unified in the pursuit of adapting to our current situation in ways that will preserve the vibrancy of our community and keep Central essential. Color is the unifying language of our campaign. It acts as a consistent, visual signifier of positivity, warmth and vibrancy during these unprecedented, difficult times. We chose our shade of Positive Pink for its bold, but friendly demeanor. Its application across Central Square will send a message to visitors and members of Central Square that businesses are ready to welcome them back, and that they are unified in their commitment to keeping Central Square vibrant, safe, and essential to the community. Our application states the banners would have a flight period of mid-June through the end of September, please be in contact with any questions or concerns. In addition to all the application materials we have also attached the a summary of our Project Neighbor, Hello Again campaign so you can see the banners are just one part of a larger communication plan. Thank you in advance for your support. My Best, pular loutine. Michael Monestime Executive Director, CSBID Central Square Business Improvement District 620 Massachusetts Avenue, Suite 3 Cambridge, MA 02139 www.centralsg.org

ELEMENTS Kind Reminders to Act Safely A limited set of simple, bold icons whose selection mirrors the state mandates for safety. These are to be used in storefronts and as part of the communication campaign to act as friendly reminders of safe behaviors people can adopt while in Central Square. The focus of these icons is to remind people to that washing their hands, wearing a mask and remaining 6' away from others whenever possible will help keep themselves and others safe while accessing Central Square.

TAKING IT FORWARD Paint the Town Pink People use city furniture and infrastructure as a part of their daily experience. We wait for the bus, we sit in the sunshine, we eat lunch on the lawn on a nice day. This is not just desired but essential. To help people weave themselves back into the fabric of our community, we paint the town pink. It is our color of safety, inclusion, permission. Swathes of color (at 6'-0" socially distant increments) give people a graphic shorthand to understand where they can (and cannot be) while extending the canvas of our campaign throughout the district.

ELEMENTS Clarity Through Consistency With so much in flux, businesses are struggling to communicate their adapted offerings to the public. Many windows and doors are covered in an array of improvised, ad-hoc signage. By providing businesses with adaptable, but consistent signage and a do-it-yourself communication kit, we set the conditions for a more unified message, improving clarity for patrons and lending an air of legitimacy to stores' efforts.
