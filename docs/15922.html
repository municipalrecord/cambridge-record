Zondervan August 2, 2023 Dear Mayor Siddiqui and Councillors, The Charter Review Committee has continued its important work of reviewing our City’s governing document. The committee has continued to meet on a bi-weekly basis to review the Cambridge City Charter with the goal of modernizing its structure, increasing accountability of local government, and aligning the Charter with the values of our city, in particular equity and accessibility. We have continued to engage with the Cambridge community, hosting two public events since our last report, one in May at the Foundry and a second in July at Tasty Burger Central Square. Every household in Cambridge received a mailer describing our work and providing a link to more information. Our coUrbanize website has garnered a number of responses and we continue to incorporate all public feedback and ideas actively into committee discussions and timelines. Please find attached our approved meeting minutes since the last update to the city council, as an update on our work. Please do not hesitate to reach out to the committee staff if you have any questions or would like any more information on plans for outreach or our projected schedule. Sincerely, Kathleen Born, Chair On Behalf of the Cambridge Charter Review Committee
