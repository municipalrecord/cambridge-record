The Path Forward Terminate Infrastructure Restore Democracy Invest in Community Immediately end Cambridge’s participation in the ShotSpotter program and physically remove all always listening microphones from neighborhoods. Mandate a full City Council vote and public review process for the acquisition of any future surveillance technology, closing the federal grant loophole. Reallocate resources toward community-led, evidence-based apporaches to public safety rather thn unproven, unaccountable surveillance systems.
