APP 2017 #21 · Agenda item attachment · May 8 2017

An application was received from DIV 35 CPD, LLC, requesting permission to widen the curb cut on Steel Place at the entrance to the parking garage and relocating the curb cut from Cambridgepark Drive to Steel Place at the premises numbered 35 Cambridgepark Drive; said petition has received approval from Inspectional Services, Traffic, Parking and Transportation, Historical Commission and Public Works. No response has been received from the neighborhood association

ADAMS & RAFFERTY 297*-: PM 2:44 ATTORNEYS AT LAW A Professional Association * 675 Massachusetts Avenue Cambridge, MA 02139 James J. Rafferty, P.C. Telephone [phone removed] [email removed] Fax [phone removed] Cambridge City Council May 4, 2017 City Hall 795 Massachusetts Avenue Cambridge, MA 02139 Re: Curb Cut Application 35 Cambridgepark Drive Dear Mayor Simmons and Members of the City Council: I am writing to provide you with some additional information concerning the above- captioned curb cut application. The Special Permit that authorized the renovation and addition at this building was issued by the Planning Board on December 12, 2016. During the hearing process on the application, the Planning Board expressed the view that the existing curb cut on Cambridgepark Drive should be eliminated. That curb cut served as the vehicle entry for the garage at the rear of the site and for the loading area. Moreover, the Planning Board correctly noted that by removing the curb cut, the corner of the site could be enhanced with improved pedestrian circulation, expanded patio seating area, and additional green space, All of those objectives were achieved in the revised plan that was approved by the Planning Board (see attached). The exact location of the new curb cut into the garage from Steel Place underwent several months of review and discussion with DPW and the Traffic Department. An earlier proposed location would have necessitated the removal of a mature street tree on Steel Place. Removal of the tree is no longer necessary. The approval of this curb cut on Monday evening will allow the applicant to complete the building permit application process for this project and commence construction. Thank you for your attention and timely action to this important matter. James J. Rafferty JJR/pwe *not a partnership
