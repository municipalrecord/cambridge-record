An application was received from Central Square Business Improvement District requesting permission for 45 temporary banners along Inman Square, Central Square, Kendall Square and Cambridge Rindge and Latin School along Broadway. Celebrating the graduating class of 2020 from June 16, 2020 through August 16, 2020

Attn: Cambridge City Council 795 Massachusetts Avenue Cambridge, MA 02139 The Honorable, Cambridge City Council, The Central Square Business Improvement District is working in partnership with Cambridge Rindge and Latin to help celebrate the graduating class of 2020. Since our seniors didn't get to finish there last ¼ of school, prom or graduation -we would like to put up pole banners to celebrate them. Supporting documents are attached to this letter of support. Please be in touch with any questions or concerns.
