City of Cambridge Executive Department Yl-AN HUANG City Manager Douglas Rice/ Commissioner Department of Conservation and Recreation DCR Main Office 251 CausewaySt. Boston, Massachusetts 02114 March 3rd 2023 Re: Riverbend Park on Memoria! Drive. Dear Commissioner Rice, The City of Cambridge hereby requests that the Department of Conservation and Recreation (DCR) close Memorial Drive between Gerry's Landing Road and Western Avenue to vehicular traffic on both Saturday and Sunday through the spring, summer/ and fall of 2023. This request is being made as a result of a Cambridge City Counci! order that passed on Monday February 27th, 2023 asking that the City Manager work with state agencies to ensure the success of the proposed closures this year. This section of Memorial Drive, known as Riverbend Park, provides essential access to open space/ recreational opportunities/ and the Charles River for thousands of Cambridge residents each weekend. As you may be aware, community outreach completed over the past year has identified very strong support for Saturday closures in addition to the traditiona! Sunday closures. This outreach also identified concerns over the impacts the Saturday closures have on traffic in the adjacent Riverside neighborhood. It is the City's hope that we can work collaboratively with you to mitigate the traffic impacts associated with the Saturday closures on neighboring streets. While the City plans to look closely at traffic impacts and changes on City streets/ we look to partner with you to improve the functioning of the intersections of Memorial Drive and Western Ave in Cambridge, and Soldier's Fie!d Road and Western Ave in Boston. The weekend closures of Memorial Drive significantly alter the traffic pattern of vehicles passing through this area, and weekend-specific

traffic signal timing can address those changes. Through changes to signal timing and lane use markings at these intersections and on the Bridge, we hope to optimize the flow of traffic during the weekend closures. Improving the operation of these intersections will mitigate the impact of traffic backups on the Riverside neighborhood. In addition to mitigating the traffic impacts on the Riverside neighborhood, we plan to conduct a season long study to quantify the overall traffic impacts of the weekend closures on the neighborhoods adjacent to Riverbend Park. By collecting data this Spring and Summer/ we can alt share a better understanding of the traffic changes stemming from the closures, and by extension additional mitigations that could be implemented. Further, we look forward to working out the details of the closures with you to further mitigate the impacts on local streets. As always, we greatly appreciate your continuing partnership with the City in our efforts to ensure the closure of Riverbend Park to vehicular traffic and the creation of the additional open space opportunities along the Parkway will work well this year/ while at the same time working with us to ensure we do everything possible to accommodate the adjacent Riverside neighborhood directly impacted by the road closure. Thank you for your consideration, Yi-An Huang/ City Manager
