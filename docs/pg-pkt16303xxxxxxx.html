Congratulations to Simply Erinn's on their 24th Anniversary.

RES 2026-96·Council meeting Jun 8, 2026·3 pages
Page 1 of 3
RES 2026-96 FIRST IN COUNCIL June 8, 2026 City of Cambridge COUNCILLOR SIMMONS WHEREAS: Erinn Pearson has been a dedicated business owner and a lifelong resident of Cambridge, where she has spent decades forming tight relationships with her clients and the broader community; and WHEREAS: Erinn Pearson established Simply Erinn's Unisex Hair Salon in Cambridge in 2002, and for 24 years has provided professional, personalized hair care excellence to clients with all hair textures – offering every visit a personalized consultation, expert hair and scalp analysis, a custom treatment plan, and at-home care guidance, earning the salon its reputation as "The Secret to Extraordinary Hair"; and WHEREAS: Erinn Pearson, as a Licensed Master Barber since 1990 and a Licensed Cosmetologist since 1999, has brought a wealth of knowledge and continuous study to the salon culture at Simply Erinn's; and WHEREAS: Erinn Pearson has received numerous awards and accolades in recognition of her outstanding contributions to her craft and her community, including being named "Best Ethnic Salon" in the Improper Boston in 2009, being recognized by the Boston Globe for Domestic Violence Awareness efforts in 2009, being highlighted by the Baystate Banner as an outstanding business owner in 2015, named one of YWCA Cambridge's Outstanding Women in 2015, being chosen as a finalist for the "Thrive" award in 2015, being named the national winner of the Barbershop: The Next Cut Challenge – a $20,000 makeover sponsored by Warner Brothers – in 2016, and more recently being honored with the BICA Growth Builders Award in 2022, the Black Biz Ball Kuumba Award in 2023, and the GBOS Lifetime Achievement Award in 2024; and WHEREAS: Erinn Pearson is passionately committed to training the next generation of stylists and continuously works with newly-graduated stylists from local schools to mentor them in the professional standards and practices of the industry; and WHEREAS: Erinn Pearson remains deeply committed to all aspects of women's health, including her important work with the "Cut it Out" project, which offers a front-line defense against domestic violence, and through Simply Erinn's Unisex Hair Salon, which hosts monthly Community Conversations for Women of Color called "Sister to Sister" – led by professional healthcare practitioners of color; now therefore be it RESOLVED: That the City Council go on record congratulating Erinn Pearson on the 24th anniversary of Simply Erinn's Unisex Hair Salon and in recognizing her invaluable and enduring contributions to the Cambridge community; and be it further

RESOLVED: That the City Clerk be and hereby is requested to forward a suitably engrossed copy of this resolution to Erinn Pearson on behalf of the entire City Council. A true copy; ATTEST:- Paula M. Crane, Interim City Clerk
