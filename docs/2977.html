Through our Creative Marketplace Exhibition program, we curate and install exhibitions featuring local artists at businesses throughout the city to boost the visibility of the arts and create welcoming, enlivened office environments. Established in 1974, Cambridge Arts is one of the oldest and most dynamic art agencies in the country—a trusted partner to businesses such as the Mount Auburn Hospital, Boston Properties, Workbar Cambridge, Harding House and Unum Therapeutics. WHO THE PROGRAM SUPPORTS Creative Marketplace Exhibitions support the professional community in Cambridge by creating work environments that wake the senses and foster connections. Exhibitions also provide direct financial support to local artists and the annual Cambridge Arts Open Studios where each participating business is honored as a sponsor. HOW THE PROGRAM WORKS A team of experts at Cambridge Arts will collaborate closely with you to ensure that each Creative Marketplace Exhibition meets the exacting needs of your business, or organization. With your goals in mind, we will recommend a collection of artwork from our database of over 350 individual Cambridge artists. Their work represents a diverse array of media and subject matter, so there is sure to be a good fit for your mission, space, and style. Let us take the hassle out of creating an invigorating workspace for your colleagues and visitors. We have years of experience handling contracts with artists and professional installation of artwork. Your tax-deductible stipend underwrites these services and a loan of art for a year. Whether you work for a mom-and-pop, or a global corporation, we are committed to helping you achieve a Creative Marketplace.
