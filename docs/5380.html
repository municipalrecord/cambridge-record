Barr, Director DATE: September 19, 2018 RE: Awaiting Report 18-29: Fees for Block Parties This memo is in response to Policy Order #1 from the March 5, 2018 City Council Meeting (Awaiting Report 18-29) regarding the fees charged for block parties by various City departments. As requested in the original Policy Order, I have reviewed the current $25 fee that the Traffic, Parking, and Transportation Department charges for block parties. The issuance of a block party permit requires the following activities on the part of Department staff: • Review of the initial permit application, including verifying that a sufficient number of abutters to the block party location have indicated their support for the event in writing. • Review of the informational flyer for the block party, to ensure that the correct information is being communicated to local residents. • Placement and pick up of traffic control barrels to be used to safely shut down the street during the block party, requiring two trips to the location by Operations unit staff. Based on this review, I feel that the current $25 fee is set at a reasonable level, and in many cases does not recoup the total cost associated with reviewing and issuing the permit, and providing residents with the traffic control devices necessary to ensure public safety. It is also worth noting that we do not generally receive complaints about the cost associated with block party permits, and I believe that residents feel that they are receiving good value for the fee they pay, particularly since it includes delivery and pickup of the traffic control barrels. Based on this review, I do not believe that it is necessary or appropriate to change the fee charged for block party permits, and would not recommend making any modifications to our current fee structure. More information on our block party permits is available online: http://www.cambridgema.gov/traffic/Permits/blockparties.
