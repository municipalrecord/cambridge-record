City of Cambridge Agenda Item Number 18C IN CITY COUNCIL December 3, 2018 WHEREAS: The Cambridge Housing Authority and its affiliated entities Millers River Holding LLC and Millers River LLC (collectively the "CHA") seek to redevelop the Millers River Apartments at 15 Lambert Street in Cambridge, MA (the "Property"); and WHEREAS: The CHA and the City have been working very closely for the past six months or so to explore ways in which the CHA could raise the necessary funding for the CHA's project, which as planned will include the demolition and replacement of the building which houses the community room, which is important to Millers River residents (the "Project"); and WHEREAS: The City seeks to obtain a 10-foot wide easement of 4,600 square feet at the Millers River site from the CHA (the "Easement" in order to provide a key element of the planned construction of a public multi-use path adjacent to the Grand Junction Railway Line (the "Multi-Use Path"); and WHEREAS: The City and the CHA have worked collaboratively to develop a plan at the Miller's River Apartments site that would allow both parties to achieve both of these important community goals; and WHEREAS: The CHA has confirmed that it will accept a pro tanto damage award in the amount of One Million Four Hundred Twenty-Four Thousand Three Hundred Fifty Dollars and Seventy Cents ($1,424,350.70) (the "Damage Award") from the City for the taking by eminent domain by the City of the Easement and that the CHA including its affiliated entities will not seek additional monetary damages related to the taking of the Easement and will waive the statutory requirement that the City obtain and file an appraisal prior to the taking of the Easement; and WHEREAS: The City and the CHA have reached an agreement pursuant to which the City will provide grant funds to the CHA in the amount of Three Million, Five Hundred Seventy-Five Thousand Six Hundred Forty-Nine Dollars and Thirty Cents ($3,575,649.30) to assist in the funding of the Miller River redevelopment Project (the "Grant Funds"); and

WHEREAS: The Grant Funds shail be used exclusively for the purpose of facilitating the redevelopment and rehabilitation of the affordable housing units at Millers River, specifically, for the demolition of the building that currently houses the community room and the construction of fifteen units at Millers River that will be permanently maintained as affordable housing units as part of the Project; and WHEREAS: The City's granting of the Grant Funds to the CHA, together with the City's payment of the Damage Award for the taking of the Easement in order to construct and maintain the Multi-Use Path will enable the CHA to close the funding gap that currently would prevent the CHA from building out the Project as planned; and WHEREAS: The provision of these grant funds is a unique circumstance relating to the City's strong desire to support the provision of housing for low-income families in Cambridge while supporting the City's goal of providing for the development of the public, multi-use path adjacent to the Grand Junction Railway Line; NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDERED: That the amount of Three Million, Five Hundred Seventy-Five Thousand Six Hundred Forty-Nine Dollars and Thirty Cents ($3,575,649.30) be and hereby is appropriated from Free Cash to the Public Investment Fund Community Development Department Extraordinary Expenditures Account in order for the City to provide grant funds to the CHA to assist in the funding of the Miller River redevelopment Project as described above, In City Council December 3, 2018.
