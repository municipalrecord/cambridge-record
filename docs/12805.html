CASTIVES DONATARTE OFFICE OF THE CITY CLERK CITY OF CAMBRIDGE [phone removed] FAX: [phone removed] DIANE P. LEBLANC PAULA M. CRANE CITY CLERK DEPUTY CITY CLERK October 20, 2022 Dear Honorable Mayor Siddiqui and Councillors, As you know, the City Clerk's office receives numerous communications from the public for inclusion on the City Council agenda. Currently these communications are sent to the primary City Clerk email address, City Clerk@cambridgema.gov that is also used for requesting vital records and making inquires on a variety of different topics. With the volume of emails we receive, it can be challenging to separate out the communications that need to get on the City Council agenda. I ask you to consider establishing a dedicated email, CouncilCommunications@cambridgema.gov, that the public would use to submit communications for the Council agenda. This would help immensely with workflow in our office and also help to ensure that no communication is missed. Thank you for your consideration. Diane LeBlanc City Clerk CITY HALL, 795 MASSACHUSETTS AVENUE, CAMBRIDGE, MASSACHUSETTS 02139
