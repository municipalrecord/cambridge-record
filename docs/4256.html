Bard, Jr. Date: December 18, 2017 Re: Appointment of Special Police Officers for Detail Assignments Please find attached, proposed special legislation regarding the appointment of retired Cambridge Police Department officers as special police officers within the City of Cambridge for paid detail assignments which I respectfully request be submitted to the City Council and the state legislature for approval. As you can see from the attached documentation, there are rigorous requirements for the appointment of special police officers under the proposed legislation. We recognize that policing in Cambridge is a unique responsibility that carries with it a community orientated focus and requires specialized training that is geared specifically towards serving a diverse group of citizens, daily workforce and visitors to the City. Currently, every sworn Cambridge police officer is trained in specific methods of community engagement; transparency and legitimacy; and enforcement, de-escalation, crisis intervention, and use of force techniques that are wholly specific to the needs and values of this City. We expect nothing less of the police officers that staff the City’s detail assignments on a daily basis. However, given the sheer volume of projects currently underway in the City, dozens of paid detail assignments are staffed every day by officers from outside police departments or, in many cases, left unstaffed, posing an unnecessary safety risk for the City. We believe that it is critically important for the City to supplement the staffing of detail assignments with retired Cambridge police officers to not only cover the large number of unstaffed detail assignments, but also to staff as many detail assignments with police officers who are trained and experienced in the unique style of policing that the City expects and deserves. We are engaged in discussions with both the Cambridge Patrol Officers and the Cambridge Superior Officers unions to ensure the rights of our current police officers are maintained. To that end, every special police officer that would be assigned under the proposed legislation would have prior training on Cambridge specific policies and procedures, including topics such as community orientated policing; transparency and legitimacy; use of force techniques, specifically including de-escalation, crisis intervention and departmental firearms training. Every special police officer will also have years, if not decades, of experience servicing the needs of the Cambridge citizenry and will know how this City expects police officers to appropriately protect and engage with the public. As always, we look forward to identifying ways in which the Department can better serve the community. I look forward to discussing this matter with you and the City Council in order to answer any questions you and others may have. Sincerely, Branville G. Bard, Jr. Police Commissioner
