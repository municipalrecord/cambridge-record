COVID-19 Variants • Total number of cases known to be infected with B1.1.7 (U.K.) COVID-19 variant of the virus is at 34 in the Commonwealth, as of February 21. 12 • CPHD announced on Feb. 18 that the first case of the B1.351 COVID-19 variant (first detected in South Africa) reported by the Commonwealth on Feb. 16 is a resident of Cambridge. • Remain more vigilant than ever with mask wearing, social distancing and hand hygiene in order to prevent transmission of variant (as with COVID-19). • Get population sufficiently vaccinated.
