To: Yi-An Huang, City Manager From: Taha Jennings, Chair of Community Preservation Act Committee Date: September 4, 2025 Re: Allocation and Appropriation of FY2026 CPA Funds The purpose of this memo is to summarize the process of the Community Preservation Act Committee (CPAC) and to convey the Committee’s recommendations for FY2026 CPA funding, voted on by the CPAC on September 12, 2025. The Committee met four times: June 4, 2025 (Organizing Meeting), July 28, 2025 (Project Recommendation Hearing), August 20, 2025 (Allocation Percentages Hearing), and September 3, 2025 (Project and Allocation Vote Meeting). The Committee’s recommendations are based on public comments and City staff presentations received at the July 28, 2025 and August 20,2025 hearings, public comment submitted while the record remained open, and CPAC discussion at the September 3, 2025 meeting. The Committee received comments as follows: • 9 public speakers at the July 28, 2025 hearing; • 5 public speakers at the August 20, 2025 hearing; • 26 communications received via electronic submission or telephone; and • Emailed petitions from 6 affordable housing developments with a total of 250 signatures. The Committee received 30 comments related to CPA projects: • 1 comment related to affordable housing; • 5 comments supported historic preservation projects; and • 24 comments supported open space projects. The Committee received 216 comments on the allocation percentages: • 9 individual comments supported an 80% allocation to affordable housing; and • 250 petition signatures supported an 80% allocation to affordable housing. The attached FY26 Project Recommendations Book provides additional detail on the Committee’s process, funding, and recommended projects for FY26. Accordingly, on September 3, 2025, the CPAC voted on the following recommendations to the City Council, through the City Manager. The CPAC voted unanimously for an allocation of 80% for Affordable Housing, 10% for Historic Preservation projects and 10% for Open Space projects. The Committee then voted to recommend that CPA funds in the amount of $22,500,000 be allocated and appropriated to the Affordable Housing Trust, as well as to certain historic preservation and open space projects. In addition, the committee voted to recommend appropriating $113,000 from the Historic Preservation Reserve Fund and $331,000 from the Open Space Reserve Fund to projects. All recommendations were voted unanimously. The Committee votes were as follows:
