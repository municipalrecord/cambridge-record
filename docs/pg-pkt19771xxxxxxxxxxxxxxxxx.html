(A) ICE Detainer Requests and Administrative Warrants • Between February 2026 and August 2026, the Cambridge Police Department received zero (0) ICE detainer requests. • Zero (0) administrative warrants were lodged with the department. (B) Individuals Detained as a Result of ICE Requests or Warrants • No individuals were detained as a result of an ICE detainer or administrative warrant during this period. (C) Individuals Transferred to ICE Custody • No individuals were transferred to ICE custody during this period. (D) Federal Reimbursements Related to ICE Detainers or Warrants • No reimbursements were received from the federal government pursuant to any ICE detainer or administrative warrant during this period. (E) Investigations Involving Cooperation with ICE • Between February 2026 and August 2026, there were zero (0) investigations in which the Cambridge Police Department cooperated with or provided information to ICE.
