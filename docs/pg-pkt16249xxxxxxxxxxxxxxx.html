RES 2026-91 FIRST IN COUNCIL June 1, 2026 City of Cambridge COUNCILLOR SIMMONS WHEREAS: The Cambridge City Council is pleased to join the Cambridge Education Association and Cambridge Public Schools in congratulating the educators, administrators, staff members, and school community professionals who are being honored upon their retirement in 2026; and WHEREAS: The work of public education depends upon the dedication of many people serving in many different roles, including classroom educators, school administrators, support staff, specialists, building-based staff, and central office personnel, all of whom contribute to the daily life, success, and wellbeing of Cambridge students and families; and WHEREAS: Through years of service to Cambridge Public Schools, these retirees have helped teach, guide, support, encourage, and care for generations of young people, while also strengthening the school communities, programs, and departments in which they served; WHEREAS: The Cambridge Education Association and Cambridge Public Schools will honor these retirees at a Retirement Celebration on Tuesday, June 23, 2026, at First Street Market in East Cambridge; now therefore be it RESOLVED: That the Cambridge City Council go on record congratulating the 2026 retirees of Cambridge Public Schools, thanking them for their many years of dedicated service, and extending best wishes to each of them for a happy, healthy, and fulfilling retirement; and be it further RESOLVED: That the City Clerk be and hereby is requested to prepare a suitably engrossed copy of this resolution for presentation at the Retirement Celebration on behalf of the entire Cambridge City Council. A true copy; ATTEST:- Paula M. Crane, Interim City Clerk
