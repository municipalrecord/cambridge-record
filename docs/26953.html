Bk: 83853 Pg: 381 BZA-290238 Case No. Location: 10 Blanchard Road, Cambridge MA Petitioner: Kerry and Prithviraj Tanwar - c/o Maggie Booz, Architect On November 7, 2024, Petitioner's architect Maggie Booz appeared before the Board of Zoning Appeal requesting a special permit in order to reduce the single 20-foot-wide curb cut to 10 feet and to add a second 10-foot-wide curb cut to access Blanchard Road, where the property lacked the frontage for a second curb cut. The Petitioner requested relief under Article 6, Sections 6.43.3.c and 6.43.5.c and Article 10, Section 10.40 of the Cambridge Zoning Ordinance (Ordinance"). The Petitioner submitted materials in support of the application, including information about the project, plans, and photographs. Ms. Booz stated that the Petitioners were requesting permission to install a second curb cut at the property. She stated that the second cut would make egressing onto the busy Blanchard Road safer given the high volume of vehicular and pedestrian traffic. She stated that the property needed to have 100 feet of frontage for a second cut but that the Petitioners only had 84 feet, requiring a special permit. The Chair opened public comment and asked if anyone wished to be heard on the matter. One neighbor spoke in support of the proposal. The Chair also noted letters of support from neighbors. After discussion, the Chair moved that the Board make the following findings based upon the application materials submitted and all evidence before the Board and that based upon the findings the Board grant the requested relief as described in the Petitioner's submitted materials and the evidence before the Board: that the Board find that it appeared the requirements of the Ordinance could and would be met; that the Board find that traffic generated or patterns of access or egress would not cause congestion; that the Board find that the continued operation of or the development of adjacent uses as permitted in the Ordinance would not be adversely affected; that the Board find that no nuisance or hazard would be created to the detriment of health, safety, or welfare; that the Board find that for no other reasons would the proposed use impair the integrity of the district or adjoining district; that the Board find that the application met the criteria of Article 10, Section 10.43 required to grant the Special Permit; and that the Board incorporate the supporting statements and dimensional form submitted as part of the application. The Chair further moved that based upon all the information presented the Board grant the requested relief as described in the Petitioner's submitted materials and the evidence before the Board on the following conditions: 1. that the work comply with the drawings submitted, titled "Tanwar Residence, 10 Blanchard Road," prepared by Smart Architecture, dated September 19, 2024, as initialed by the Chair, and 2. that the new paving be permeable.
