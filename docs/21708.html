314 Main Street Gambrill Center Cambridga, MA 02142 IT October 22, 2024 To the Honorable City Councilors of Cambridge City Council City Hall 795 Massachusetts Avenue EUSEUM Cambridge, MA 02139 Dear Council Members: I am writing to respectfully request your permission to change 25% of the existing MIT Museum banners to reflect a recent exhibition change. We currently have four different banners hanging, each one featuring a different ongoing exhibition: A1: Mind the Gap, Gene Cultures, Essential MIT, and In Concert. Our In Concert exhibition recently closed and was replaced with Cosmograph. This request does not include any change of pole number, only the replacement of existing In Concert banners with Cosmograph ones.
