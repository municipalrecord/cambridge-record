A Neighborhood Organization for the Betterment of East Cambridge East End House 105 Spring Street, Cambridge MA 02141 [email removed] October 6, 2023 Cambridge City Council Cambridge City Hall 795 Massachusetts Avenue, 2nd Floor Cambridge, MA 02139 RE: Curb Cut Request at 63 Sixth St Dear Mayor Siddiqui, Vice Mayor Mallon, Councillors Azeem, Carlone, McGovern, Nolan, Simmons, Toner, and Zondervan: The East Cambridge Planning Team (ECPT) did not receive the curb cut application for 63 Sixth St from the Clerk’s Office and was made aware of it by Councillor Nolan last week. We thank her for her Charter Right at the last council meeting. As time was of the essence, the ECPT board reviewed the curb cut application and had many concerns detailed as follows: • How many parking spaces will be lost for this one off-street parking space. It appears that 2 maybe lost resulting in a net loss of parking for the neighborhood. • What is the potential damage of excavation to the mature tree adjacent to the driveway? The proposed driveway encroaches on the drip line of the tree and therefore the roots. Many trees in East Cambridge have suffered root damage from excavation making the trees structurally unsound and subsequently had to be razed. • How much ground permeability will be lost due to the driveway and parking place in an extremely hardscaped area? • Is the proximity of the parking place to windows a concern? • This driveway will be across from the Kennedy Longfellow School campus. Will backing out of the driveway be potentially dangerous to children on their way to and from school? In addition to these concerns, the abutter’s letters of support are old, some dating back to 2006 (17 years ago) two abutters are no longer on the deeds. • 154-156 Spring St - Michael Nicoloro dated 2019 (4 years ago) • 153 Spring St - Ava Brown dated 2006 (17 years ago) • 66 Sixth St - Bob Dezengotita dated 2006 (no longer on deed) • 62 Sixth St - Peter Lacamera dated 2006 (sold house in 2009) • 63 Sixth St - Vaalerie Zakszewksi (date unreadable) We cannot support this curb cut application without answers to these questions.
