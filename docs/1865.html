Letter from Hasson Rashid, 820 Massachusetts Avenue, relating to City Manager's Agenda #16 and #17 and Policy Order #7

Peace Be Unto You, Hello Everybody, I'm Mr. Hasson Rashid, 1 reside at 820 Massachusetts Avenue, Cambridge MA. My Public Comment is on CMA Is# 16 and #17, and 'Policy Order and Resolution I# 7 I think that all of these items are additional reflections on how the lower strata's of the trajectory of poor peoples are continuously and statistically, being shot out or excluded from economic and development, opportunities in the City of Cambridge, Ma. Whenever you municipal policy makers choose to ignore the very and extremely, low income populations, you are ignoring the whole community. It's not financial smart, and doesn't make good economic sense to ignore the lower levels of poor peoples in Cambridge, M A ete., in favor of prioritizing the middle income for public funding, and inclusionary housing. I say it is a cock eyed demonstration and form of oppression combined with discrimination and fair housing violations, in the making. Thank you. Yours In Peace, Mr. Hasson Rashid Cambridge, MA May 23, 2006
