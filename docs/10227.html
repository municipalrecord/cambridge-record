An application was received from Eric Grunebaum, for Friends of Jerry's Pond, requesting permission for two (2) temporary banners to be hung on one light pole in the Russell Field parking lot and the second pole left after the bus shelter announcing Historic Jerry's Pond on April 29, 2021 thru April 29, 2023

April 27, 2021 Cambridge City Council Electrical Dept. 795 Massachusetts Ave. Cambridge, MA 02139 Re: Letter of intent re: Jerry's Pond banner Dear Cambridge City Council: "• :· .. r: ;. /' :" // --~~ I am writing this letter to request permission for two banners to be hung on one light pole in the Russell Field parking lot next to Jerry's Pond. The proposed location is the second pole on the left after the bus shelter because that pole is closest to the pond and is clear of trees. (Note that if permission is granted by the OCR, there may be two identical banners hung on a light pole on OCR property at the corner of Rindge Avenue and Alewife Brook Parkway.) Our intention is to show the importance to the community of Jerry's Pond's history and as a natural area. We ask that these banners be installed as soon as possible and stay in place for two years from the date of installation. The designs are below. We thank you and appreciate your time. If you have further questions, please don't hesitate to contact me at the number and email address below.
