New England Base Camp, owned and operated by the Boy Scouts Spirit of Ad- venture Council, provided the program for local youth for Earth Day. The pro- gram is founded on the curriculum of the sustainability merit badge, environ- mental science badge and a dozen other conservation and ecology badges. Dur- ing the recent school vacation week, the boys and girls created long-term inter- active teaching stations and experienc- es. These programs and more were highlighted on Earth Day as kids show- cased their work and their lessons learned. Zoe and Noba Johnson, of Cambridge, were among the dozens of youth who participated. New England Base Camp hosted a full week of activities focused on STEM topics such as robotics and outdoor ac- tivities such as the Earth Day program. Conducted under the theme “Restore our Earth,” boys and girls from across the area engaged in activities aimed at teaching about sustainability and cli- mate change, and why these issues are so important. Youth built stations across the expansive Base Camp area, including the sustainable farm, where kids learn to harvest and grow their own vegetables, honey, maple syrup, eggs and other sustainable ingredients they use in their cooking. Studying climate change during the past 10 years, Scouts and campers have been collecting weather data as part of the weather Merit Badge. Together with local artists, New England Base Camp designed a dramatic and living visualization of that data. After Earth Day, Base Camp visi- tors will be able to add to the station, creating an ongoing display based on each day’s data. Backyard Climate Change was also a focus topic, where campers study the types of plants that ﬂourish, animal migration patterns and ground water to show the evidence of climate change. Base Camp is a network of outdoor adventure parks open to families of all kinds to enjoy the outdoors through programs that introduce youth to ca- reers, hobbies and outdoor pursuits, de- velop valuable life skills, stimulate learning and build strong character. New England Base Camp is open to the public and has strong COVID-19 pro- tocols in place, ensuring any family can spend the entire day or afternoon enjoy- ing a full range of activities.
