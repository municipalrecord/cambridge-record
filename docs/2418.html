Ref: 13452.00 Cambridge Inspectional Services Department Lombardi Building 831 Massachusetts Avenue, 1st Floor - ISD Clerk Cambridge, MA 02139 Re: 35-49-59 Smith Place - Curb Cut Application To whom it may concern, On behalf of The Davis Companies (TDC), Vanasse Hangen Brustlin, Inc. (VHB) is submitting the attached curb cut application package for the proposed improvements at 35-49-59 Smith Place in Cambridge, Massachusetts. The proposed project consists of improvements to an existing surface lot located at 35, 49, and 59 Smith Place, which has completed the Cambridge small project review process. The current use of the existing parcels will remain the same, which is a surface parking lot for employees at the 725 Concord Avenue parcel. The project will consist of the demolition of the existing 2-story Cinderella carriage house located within the 49 Smith Place parcel, and a zoning compliant surface parking lot. Approximately 80 linear feet of the existing frontage along Smith Place has an existing sidewalk. The project is proposing an improved 330 linear feet of public sidewalk along the frontage of Smith Place consisting of one modified curb cut, and one proposed curb cut for surface lot access. The proposed curb cuts will be City of Cambridge standards. Additionally, the project will be closing two existing curb cuts previously utilized by the existing building to be demolished. Please reference the attached existing conditions plan, and proposed layout and materials plan. LOPEZ CITY CLERK DEPUTY CITY CLERK July 21, 2016 Dear Neighborhood Representative: This office is in receipt of a copy of an application from The Davis Companies, requesting a curb cut at the premises numbered 35 Smith Place, Cambridge, Massachusetts. The City Council has directed that all curb cut petitions be submitted to the appropriate Neighborhood Associations for the locality where the curb cut would be made, so that the association may have an opportunity for review, prior to action by the City Council. Please indicate by return mail your approval or disapproval of the petition within twenty-one days from the date of this letter. If the response is "disapproval" please state reasons. Be sure to sign the form and include a daytime phone number. I have enclosed a self-addressed stamped envelope to facilitate your reply. As soon as this office has received both the completed application and your neighborhood association response, I will place the petition on the agenda for the next City Council meeting. If I do not receive a response from your neighborhood organization by twenty-one days from the date of this letter, I will place the petition on the agenda for the next City Council meeting If your neighborhood association cannot complete its review by twenty-one days from today, you may extend the reply time another seven days by requesting an extension by letter to me with a copy to the petitioner. However, I urge you to make every effort to complete your review as soon as possible. Thank you for your cooperation. Sincerely yours, Donna P. Lopez Donna P.
