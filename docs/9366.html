Massachusetts Housing Coalition Pledge Encouraging Tenant & Property Owner Dialogue to Diminish Housing Effects of COVID-19 Crisis We, the undersigned property owners, in the spirit of ensuring the continued safety of residents and lessening the impact of the advancing COVID-19 pandemic, are united in pledging our support for an open, mutually beneficial dialogue with tenants suffering financial hardship. We agree that legal proceedings against tenants in arrearage should be considered only a last resort where the parties fail to productively resolve prevailing issues. We understand that increased vacancies do not benefit tenants, property owners, public health, or the local economy, and recognize that the current public health crisis necessitates collaborative resolutions. Further, we agree that we will, to the largest extent possible, seek open, fair discourse regarding past and/or overdue rent in an attempt to reach a mutually agreeable, mutually beneficial outcome that preserves residents’ ability to remain in their home while minimizing the economic impacts experienced by tenants and property owners.
