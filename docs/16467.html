Dear City Council of Cambridge, On behalf of the MIT List Visual Arts Center, I am writing to apply for new banners on lamp posts: • Ames St., Memorial Drive to Main St. (16 poles): 21, 19, M19, M17, M15, M13, M9, M7, M5, M3, M1, M2, M4, M6, M8, M10 • Ames St., Main St. to Broadway (8 poles): 6, 4, 2, M41, M42, M44, M52, M54 • Broadway, Ames St. to Third St. (8 poles): M30, M26, M22, M18, M16, M12, M8, M4 • Third St., Broadway to Main St. (2 poles): M25, M23 • Main St., Third St. to Broadway (1 pole): M21 to direct students and public audiences to the contemporary art museum at MIT. The banners say: "MIT List Visual Arts Center" and "Contemporary Art" with our logo, "Art Makes You" message campaign with accompanying action words, and renderings of our public art and exhibition artworks. We intend to attach the banners October 2023 and remove them mid-April 2024. Banner attachment/removal will be facilitated by Heritage Flag Co. Thank you for your time, Gwyneth Jackman MIT List Visual Arts Center Marketing Coordinator [phone removed] [email removed] M 20 Ames Street [email removed] • MIT List Visual [phone removed] Building E15-109 listart.mit.edu Cambridge, MA 02139 • Arts Center
