APP 2017 #38 · Agenda item attachment · Jun 12 2017

An application was received from Iram Farooq, Assistant City Manager for Community Development Department, requesting permission for fifteen temporary banners on electrical poles along Huron Avenue, Concord Avenue and Appleton Street to highlight Observatory Hill during construction. The banners would be hung in the Summer of 2017 and are expected to remain in place until the Summer of 2019
