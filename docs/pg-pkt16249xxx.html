CMA 2026-164 IN CITY COUNCIL June 1, 2026 To the Honorable, the City Council: To the Honorable, the City Council: Pursuant to the Surveillance Technology Ordinance, Chapter 2.128 of the Cambridge Municipal Code (the Ordinance), I am submitting a summary report of all requests for approval of Surveillance Technology Impact Reports (STIRs) received by the City Council since the last summary report date. This report is for the Council’s use under Section 2.128.060(D) of the Ordinance, which requires the Council to publicly release such a summary report. No Police Department use of Surveillance Technology in Exigent Circumstances To date, all STIRs have been submitted pursuant to Section 2.128.030 of the Ordinance. No STIRs have been submitted pursuant to Section 2.128.040.
