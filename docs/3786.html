Chapter 55  Provides the Legal Basis for Cross-Agency Collaboration to Study the Alarming Trends in Opioid-related Deaths  Signed into law by Massachusetts Governor Baker in August 2015  Required the MA DPH to examine data related to opioid overdose deaths and to submit report by June 2016  Specifies some contributing data partners  Overcomes legal barriers for use of some data  New Chapter 55 law was extended and expanded to include analysis of nonfatal opioid-related overdoses through June 2017 (Chapter 55 Phase 2)

Why is Linked Data So Important?  Often one dataset does not tell the entire story:  Consider nonfatal overdoses (NFO), we need 3 data sources in order to actually count the number of NFOs:  MATRIS  Case Mix  Deaths  Allows us to control for confounders:  Late stage cancer diagnosis and prescription opioids  Allows us to consider multiple risk factors and conduct more complex analyses
