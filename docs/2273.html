Lopez, Donna ATTACHMENT K From: Kelley, Craig Sent: Tuesday, June 21, 2016 6:42 AM To: Devereux, Jan Cc: Lopez, Donna Subject: Leaf Blower meeting Hey Jan: I can't make today's meeting but I would like for the Council and the City formalize a complaint system around leaf blowers, probably including an 'issue' in Commonwealth Connect, so that the License Commission is collecting useful data about leaf blower complaints and violations, including the company involved, the location the violation occurred, the owner of the property, the type of violation, the date & time, the resulting enforcement investigation, any punishment and whether the complaint was found to be valid. That way we could systematically review complaints and have a better idea of what, if anything, needs to be changed. Right now all we have is a bunch of scattered anecdotes and that is a lousy way to craft policy. I would also like to know if there is any proactive enforcement efforts by the city or is this program entirely complaint driven. If the latter is the case, it seems like it'd be very difficult to actually enforce the decibel limit so I'd appreciate the City explaining how that happens. Right now, all I complain about is multiple leaf blowers where not allowed and folks using them outside the time and dates. These are two very visible violations that CPD could enforce as well so I would like to know how much leaf blower (quality of life issue) enforcement CPD has done and what, if any, it plans to do in the future. Finally, I appreciate Nicole's sending out a note reminding people of the end of leaf blower season. I'm not sure anyone did that before and I think it was a great idea. Leaf blowers seem like such a small problem but when they're being used around the corner from you, especially if you are either trying to work at home or you have a kid trying to take a nap, it seems like a really, really, really unnecessary, noisy, environmentally unsound and polluting way to move vegetative waste around. I would love to further minimize their use, though I understand that our desire to have clear paths and so forth makes it tough to get rid of them completely unless we are willing to spend a lot more money on grounds keeping and have more leaves and vegetative matter underfoot, which would probably mean the occasional bike slipping on wet leaves. So maybe we don't get rid of them completely, but less is better and more formalized enforcement would be lots better! Thanks a lot. Craig
