The Public Health Response to the COVID-19 Pandemic in Cambridge SPECIAL COVID-19 MEETING CAMBRIDGE CITY COUNCIL SEPTEMBER 29, 2020
Page 2 of 20
Where We Are Now • Average number of daily COVID-19 cases: • Now: Month of September = 3.5 cases per day • Then: Month of April = 20.9 cases per day • To our knowledge, Cambridge is the only non-hotspot city in MA to offer free testing for asymptomatic people. • Over 112,000 COVID-19 tests have been given to Cambridge residents, one of the highest test totals in the Commonwealth. • Over 50,800 residents have received at least one molecular COVID-19 test since March. • Only 0.14% of tests administered were COVID+
Page 3 of 20
How We Got Here • Vigorous testing, case monitoring & contact tracing • Supporting residents & visitors to stay safe: • Federal guidance (esp. at beginning of pandemic) • State and City of Cambridge separately declare a Public Health Emergency in early March. • Stay at Home Advisory (March-May) • Emergency orders & phased reopening plan • City actions: Food, housing protection, financial support, testing, information/data sharing, & guidance for public • Strategic Partnerships (e.g., Broad, EAP, City Depts.) • Managing staff fatigue

COVID-19 Testing in Cambridge • Partnership with City, Broad Institute, Pro EMS • Launched April 9 in long-term care facilities • City-funded neighborhood testing sites operating continuously since July. • Free of charge, even for asymptomatic residents • No ID/documentation required • Over 12,600 tests administered through this program • Current Challenges: Inability to secure additional “permanent” testing locations; streamlining appointment logistics

COVID-19 Testing in K-12 Schools • Prior to schools reopening on October 13, all CPS staff will have opportunity to be tested, during the week of October 5. • CPS staff on site will be tested twice a week, on a voluntary basis, as part of a surveillance program. • Testing will be offered to all students and staff who may become symptomatic during the school day. • CPHD has partnered with the Cambridge charter schools to provide technical assistance and test kits so they too can provide COVID-19 testing to their students and staff.
