City Council support of the strike of UNITE HERE Local 26 and Teamsters Local 25 workers at Encore Casino and Resort.

POR 2026-178·Council meeting Sep 28, 2026·2 pages
Page 1 of 2
POR 2026-178 FIRST IN COUNCIL September 28, 2026 City of Cambridge COUNCILLOR MCGOVERN COUNCILLOR SOBRINHO-WHEELER COUNCILLOR SIMMONS MAYOR SIDDIQUI WHEREAS: Encore Casino and Resort workers are ambassadors for Greater Boston area travelers and tourists; and WHEREAS: The over 1,400 members of UNITE HERE Local 26 and Teamsters Local 25 who do the work at the casino reflect the strength and diversity of the City of Cambridge; and WHEREAS: UNITE HERE Local 26 and Teamsters Local 25 workers believe one job should be enough to live in the city they work in, raise a family, and retire with dignity; and WHEREAS: After months of negotiations, UNITE HERE Local 26 and Teamsters Local 25 have gone on strike indefinitely at Encore Boston Harbor; now therefore be it RESOLVED: That the Cambridge City Council go on record expressing support for the strike of UNITE HERE Local 26 and Teamsters Local 25 workers, honor the strike by not eating, meeting, or sleeping at Encore Casino while a labor dispute is in effect, and join all picket lines and encourage all City employees to do the same; and be it further RESOLVED: That the City Clerk be and hereby is requested to forward a suitably engrossed copy of this resolution to the General Manager of Encore Boston Harbor on behalf of the entire City Council. A true copy; ATTEST:- Paula M. Crane, Interim City Clerk
