noted, these questions should appear at the end of your survey. • You should preface the demographic questions with this statement: “The following questions are optional. Answering them will help the City to determine whether the survey respondents are a representative sample of the Cambridge population.” • You should provide information (like a web page link) on how people can view the results of the survey once it is complete. • If you are using survey software such as SurveyMonkey that allows you to anonymize the responses (meaning you will not see the IP address of respondents), you should select that option, and include language like this at the beginning of the survey: “Your responses to this survey are anonymous. No personally identifiable information is captured unless you voluntarily include personal or contact information in your responses. Neighborhood For online or in-person surveys, this question should include a labeled map of Cambridge neighborhoods like that included here. If your survey software allows, you can let respondents click on the map to select their neighborhood. For telephone surveys or other situations where you cannot show the respondent the map, use the alternative version that groups neighborhoods into three areas. This question can appear at the end of the survey for demographic purposes, or you can place it at the beginning if you are using it as a screener question. Question (online/in person) Which neighborhood do you live in?
