To: Louis DePasquale, City Manager From: Lisa Peterson, Chair of Community Preservation Act Committee Date: September 18, 2019 Re: Allocation and Appropriation of FY2020 CPA Funds The purpose of this memo is to summarize the process of the Community Preservation Act Committee (CPAC) and to convey the Committee’s recommendations for FY2020 CPA funding, voted on by the CPAC on September 17, 2019. The Committee met four times: May 16, 2019 (Organizing Meeting), June 20, 2019 (Project Recommendation Hearing), July 31, 2019 (Allocation Percentages Hearing), and September 17, 2019 (Project and Allocation Vote Meeting). The Committee’s recommendations are based on public comments, petitions, and City staff presentations received at the June 20, 2019 and July 31, 2019 hearings, as well as public comment submitted electronically or in person while the record remained open. The Committee received comments as follows: • 16 speakers & 1 petition at the June 20, 2019 hearing; • 13 speakers & 3 petitions at the July 31, 2019 hearing; and • 21 communications received via letter, electronic or in-person submission. The Committee received 42 comments and 1 petition related to CPA projects: • 10 comments supported affordable housing initiatives; • 12 comments supported historic preservation projects; • 17 comments supported open space projects; • 3 comments supported miscellaneous projects; and • 1 petition, with 69 signatures, supported affordability restrictions on the Fresh Pond Apartments. The Committee received 18 comments and 3 petitions on the allocation percentages: • 15 comments supported an 80% allocation to affordable housing; • 2 comments supported a 70% allocation to affordable housing and a 15%, 15% split between historic preservation and open space; • 1 comment supported an increased allocation for open space but did not specify a percentage; and • 3 petitions, with a total of 362 signatures, supported an 80% allocation to affordable housing. Accordingly, on September 17, 2019, the CPAC voted on the following recommendations to the City Council, through the City Manager. The CPAC voted unanimously to recommend that CPA funds in the amount of $13,230,000 be allocated and appropriated as follows: VOTE 1: Fiscal Year 2020 Local Funds ($9,900,000) Vote 1A 80% of FY2020 CPA Local Fund revenues ($7,920,000) allocated to Affordable Housing and appropriated to the Cambridge Affordable Housing Trust Vote 1B 10% of FY2020 CPA Local Fund revenues ($990,000) allocated to Historic Preservation as follows: 1. $609,500 appropriated to the Historic Preservation Grants 2. $150,000 appropriated to the Inman Square Fire Station, façade restoration
