City of Cambridge In the Year Two Thousand and Ten AN ORDINANCE In amendment to the Ordinance entitled "Municipal Code of the City of Cambridge" Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Cambridge that Chapter 10.17 entitled "Vehicle Trip Reduction Ordinance" be amended as follows: 1. Within Section 10.17.070 Strike out the words "eight dollars per permit per household" and replace them with the words "twenty dollars effective with the 2011 permits and twenty-five dollars effective with the 2013 permits and thereafter per permit per household." 2. Add at the end of Section 10.17.070 the following sentence: «The Traffic Director shall provide notice to residents that any person who destres to make a contribution in excess of any amount of the fees required by this section may do so and such contribution shall be appropriated as set forth in Section 10.17.150." 3. Within Section 10.17.060 B delete the first sentence which provides that: "Beginning on the January first following the effective date of this provision, each residential visitor pass issued by the Traffic and Parking Department shall be designed to display a calendar for the year during which it is valid." In City Council October 18, 2010. Passed to be ordained as amended by a yea and nay vote:- Yeas 8; Nays 1; Absent 0; Present 0 Attest:- D. Margaret Drury, City Clerk A true copy; ATTEST:- D. ATTACHMENTC CITY OF CAMBRIDGE Traffic, Parking and Transportation Resident Parking Program The City of Cambridge provides a variety of permits to ensure that residents and their guests can park near their homes. Resident Permit stickers cost $25, which includes one Visitor Permit placard per household. There is no charge for residents over 65, or for those with a disability plate/placard. The Resident Permit allows a vehicle to park anywhere in the City marked Parking by Permit Only, while Visitor Permits may only be used within the designated area contained on the back of the Permit. Households with no registered vehicles may pay $25 to obtain the Visitor Permit, and residents with more than one vehicle may purchase additional Resident Permits for $25 each. All permits expire on January 31 each year.
