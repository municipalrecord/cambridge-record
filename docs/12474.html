To: Yi-An Huang, City Manager From: David Kale, Chair of Community Preservation Act Committee Date: September 7, 2022 Re: Allocation and Appropriation of FY2023 CPA Funds The purpose of this memo is to summarize the process of the Community Preservation Act Committee (CPAC) and to convey the Committee’s recommendations for FY2023 CPA funding, voted on by the CPAC on September 6, 2022. The Committee met four times: May 17, 2022 (Organizing Meeting), June 29, 2022 (Project Recommendation Hearing), July 25, 2022 (Allocation Percentages Hearing), and September 6, 2022 (Project and Allocation Vote Meeting). The Committee’s recommendations are based on public comments and City staff presentations received at the June 29, 2022 and July 25,2022 hearings, public comment submitted electronically while the record remained open, and CPAC discussion at the September 6, 2022 meeting. The Committee received comments as follows: • 0 public speaker at the May 17, 2022 meeting; • 11 public speakers at the June 29, 2022 hearing; • 8 public speakers at the July 25, 2022 hearing; • 4 communications received via electronic submission or the mail; and • 1 emailed petition with a total of 92 signatures. The Committee received 15 comments related to CPA projects: • 4 comments supported affordable housing initiatives; • 1 comments supported historic preservation projects; and • 10 comments supported open space projects. The Committee received 136 comments on the allocation percentages: • 9 individual comments supported an 80% allocation to affordable housing; • 113 petition signatures supported an 80% allocation to affordable housing; • 2 comments supported a 70% allocation to affordable housing and a 15%, 15% split between historic preservation and open space; • 1 comment supported a 70% allocation to affordable housing, 10% to historic preservation, and 20% to open space; and • 1 comment advocated for an unspecific increase to the open space allocation. The attached FY23 Project Recommendations Book provides additional detail on the Committee’s process, funding, and recommended projects for FY23. Accordingly, on September 6, 2022, the CPAC voted on the following recommendations to the City Council, through the City Manager. The CPAC voted unanimously for an allocation of 80% for Affordable Housing, 10% for Historic Preservation projects and 10% for Open Space projects. The Committee then voted to recommend that CPA funds in the amount of $19,710,000 be allocated and appropriated to the Affordable Housing Trust, as well as to certain historic preservation and open space projects. All recommendations were voted unanimously. The Committee votes were as follows:
