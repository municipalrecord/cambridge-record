POR 2025 #86 · Agenda item attachment · Jun 9 2025

Policy Order urging Governor Healey, the MBTA Board of Directors and General Manager Phillip Eng to amend the MBTA Alewife Station Complex redevelopment RFP to include as a priority eliminating untreated Combined Sewer Overflow (CSO) sewage in our neighborhoods by incorporating green and gray infrastructure as central components of the project. The order further calls on the MBTA to collaborate with the MWRA, DCR, DPH, the City of Cambridge, and the community to address this public health threat

Councillor Nolan Amendment by substitution language in Council June 9, 2025 WHEREAS: The MWRA, the City of Cambridge, and the City of Somerville have been collaborating on the Updated Combined Sewer Overﬂow (CSO) Control Plan to reduce CSOs in the Alewife Brook, the Mystic River, and the Charels River. WHEREAS: The draft recommendations for continued control of combined sewer overﬂows are due by the end of 2025, with the ﬁnal recommendations due in January 2027. WHEREAS: The upcoming redevelopment of the Alewife T Station Complex provides an opportunity for the Commonwealth of Massachusetts to continue its Boston Harbor Clean-Up responsibilities to the Alewife Brook, since the planned “world-class public transportation hub” will be located near the most active CSO on the Alewife Brooke, Cambridge’s CAM401A, and the nearby MWRA CSO MWR003. WHEREAS: A critical component of reducing sewage releases into bodies of water is the availability of land in proximity of the regulator structures that control combined sewer overﬂow discharges. The Alewife T Station Complex is a 13-acre site that sits in proximity to the MWR003 regulator and provides an opportunity to build infrastructure that will prevent sewage from overwhelming the system. WHEREAS: The current MBTA Alewife T Complex RFP states that the developer will “evaluate” Cambridge’s request that MBTA provide storage on the site.

“In connection with its long-term planning for the CSO [CAM401A], the City has requested that the MBTA accommodate storage on the site of the Garage. That request will be evaluated as a part of the Project.”; ORDERED: That Community Development Department and the Department of Public Works collaborate with the MBTA and MWRA during the development of the MBTA Alewife Station Complex to facilitate improvements that will support continued and improved CSO control along the Alewife Brook.
