CAMBRIDGE REDEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY 3/15/2017 To the Honorable, the City Council From: Cambridge Redevelopment Authority and Boston Properties RE: Main Street Kendall Square Banners Program Beginning in 1979, Cambridge Center was constructed surrounding the Kendall Red Line MBTA Station, under the guidance of the Kendall Square Urban Renewal Plan (KSURP). At that time the Cambridge Redevelopment Authority (CRA) entered into a contractual agreement with the developer and property owner of Cambridge Center (now Kendall Center), Boston Properties, to maintain and provide programming to the Main Street Plaza and to install and maintain a seasonal banner program on the streetscape of Main Street. The intention of this banner program was to add color, movement and a sense of identity to an urban renewal plan area which at the time had a significant amount of vacant land and parking lots. The CRA installed a series of flag poles in the center median of Main Street when it reconstructed the roadways in Kendall Square in the 1980s, and Boston Properties designed, installed and maintained a series of long rectangular flags that changed color with the seasons (for images of these flags see Attachment 1). The Main Street redesign project was an early action item from the K2 Plan completed by the City in 2013. In the 2014 construction season the flagpoles and the median of Main Street were taken out by DPW contractors and in 2015 and 2016 the reconstruction of Main Street from Broadway to Ames Street was completed. Just prior to the street reconstruction, in 2012-2013 the Main Street Plaza at Kendall Center and surrounding ground floor retail was redesigned and reconstructed by Boston Properties. The street reconstruction included new city-standard light fixtures and banner brackets that would provide hardware to continue the banner program using fixed banners instead of waving flags. Simultaneously, the Kendall Square Association (KSA) started a Kendall Square district-wide identity and wayfinding program that included a new Kendall Square logo for the district. Shortly thereafter the KSA facilitated the design and installation of a wayfinding kiosk that was rolled out across the district in 2016. The KSA was able to bring together a dozen major landowners in this district branding effort who have all committed to utilizing the new district-wide identity in various ways. This has manifested in the orange KSQ landmark in the Plaza on Main Street, the K-in-a-square logo being used on the entryways of both the Broadway and Main Street side of the Marriott hotel, and the top of the wayfinding kiosks, and more. Similar to many other business districts in Massachusetts and across the nation, the KSA membership believes that a unified district identity for the state’s premier innovation district is important for Kendall Square, tying together the disparate components of the district from Tech Square to One Kendall to Kendall Center to Cambridge Research Park on the Broad Canal. The banner program is also seen by the KSA membership as being essential to the streetscape design of Main Street. The DPW design for the Main Street project anticipated this banner program would be introduced at the conclusion of construction. The new banner program will be modified in several ways to reflect the updated design of Main Street. Instead of flags, the banners will be fixed. Instead of being in the center median, there will be two sets of banners – one on the north side light poles and one on the south side light poles. The northern side banners will be created, installed and maintained by Boston Properties. The southern side banners are anticipated to be created, installed and maintained by MIT at the completion of their new buildings south of Main Street. The banner color and design will continue to be changed seasonally with at least one set for spring and summer and a second set for fall and winter.
