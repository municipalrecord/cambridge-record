RES 2026-131 FIRST IN COUNCIL September 28, 2026 City of Cambridge COUNCILLOR SIMMONS WHEREAS: Timothy J. Toomey, Jr. has devoted decades of his life to public service in Cambridge and the Commonwealth, serving on the Cambridge School Committee, for more than three decades, on the Cambridge City Council from 1990 through 2021, and from 1993 through 2016 as State Representative for the 26th Middlesex District; and WHEREAS: Throughout his years in public office, Tim Toomey established a reputation as a tireless advocate for the neighborhoods and residents he represented, with a particular commitment to immigrant families, working people, seniors, and the many diverse communities that have long called Cambridge and Somerville home; and WHEREAS: Tim Toomey has enjoyed a particularly longstanding relationship with the Portuguese-speaking community, earning the respect and affection of generations of residents through his support for local organizations, cultural traditions, community gatherings, and the needs of Portuguese and Portuguese-American families; and WHEREAS: Amigos da Terça was founded in 2004 by a group of friends dedicated to fostering community and supporting the Portuguese-speaking community, with particular emphasis on youth and elders, the preservation of Portuguese heritage and culture, and philanthropic support for local community organizations; and WHEREAS: As Amigos da Terça celebrates its 22nd year, the organization will recognize and honor Timothy J. Toomey, Jr. at its annual Black Tie Dinner/Dance Fundraiser on Saturday, October 3, 2026, at the Holy Ghost Society in Lowell, Massachusetts; now therefore be it RESOLVED: That the City Council go on record congratulating former City Councillor and State Representative Timothy J. Toomey, Jr. upon receiving this well-deserved recognition from Amigos da Terça and the Portuguese community, and expressing its gratitude for his many years of dedicated public service and friendship to the people of Cambridge; and be it further RESOLVED: That the City Clerk be and hereby is requested to forward a suitably engrossed copy of this resolution to Timothy J. Toomey, Jr. on behalf of the entire City Council.
