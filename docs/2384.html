Timing Inclusionary Zoning policies are one of many requirements that affect the rate and scale of development in Cambridge. The Incentive Zoning Ordinance was revised only one yeat ago, and the City has yet to evaluate the effects of that change. The approach with that new zoning policy was a stepped increase from $4.58 per square foot to $12 per square foot, achieved by annual increases. We suggest this model for revising the Inclusionary Zoning Ordinance, while an ongoing evaluation of the Incentive Zoning Ordinance is conducted. We are wary of how quickly this process is moving. We want to be sure that the Council reviews all the components of the analysis, including the Chamber's peer review which will yield valuable examinations of potential impacts. The key function of the City's Inclusionary Zoning Ordinance must be to encourage production of new units. Without this predictable continued production, the number of available units shrinks, and with it, the City's capacity to house those in need. We encourage you and your colleagues on the Council to consider how to best balance the system currently in place with the ever-evolving demands of our unique community. We look forward to engaging further with you and your colleagues on the Council further on this topic, including at the July 11'" Housing Committee hearing. Sincerely, Sarah E.
