Kleimola Franziskus Lepionka Andrea Carrillo-Rhoads First Assistant City Solicitor CITY OF CAMBRIDGE Public Records Access Officer Seah Levy Office of the City Solicitor 795 Massachusetts Avenue Cambridge, Massachusetts 02139 April 14, 2025 Cambridge City Council Cambridge City Hall 795 Massachusetts Ave., 2nd Floor Cambridge, MA 02139 Re: City Charter - Update Regarding Revised Charter Draft To the Honorable, the City Council: Please find enclosed with this correspondence a revised draft of the proposed City Charter. This version of the draft has accepted all revisions and edits approved by prior votes of the Special Committee of the Whole up to the March 28, 2025 meeting. The redlines in the current draft reflect those revisions approved by the Special Committee of the Whole during its March 28, 2025 meeting. Additionally, the redlines contain edits that reflect certain recommendations from the Collins Center. The City Council should vote on whether to accept the edits we made that reflect the recommendations from the Collins Center. As in prior drafts, the redlined edits contain comments explaining the rationale for the changes and referencing applicable City Council votes. A clean copy is also enclosed for your reference. If the Council accepts all the redlined changes that reflect recommendations by the Collins Center, the Council can then vote to petition the Legislature to enact the Home Rule Petition Charter. We have also enclosed the Home Rule Petition of the proposed Charter that will be sent to the state delegation and sponsoring state legislator for presentation to the General Court, should the City Council vote to proceed.
