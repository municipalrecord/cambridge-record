CITY COUNCIL AWAITING REPORT #16-68 CAMBRIDGE ARTS: CREATIVE MARKETPLACE – Attachment 1 1 In 2016/2017, Creative Marketplace will result in the following direct financial support for artists living in Cambridge:  Community Supported Art Participating Artists: 18 (9-artists per learning cohort) - Artists living in Cambridge eligible to self-identify for participation - All Cambridge Open Studios artists eligible for participation Artist Honorariums: $2,000 per artist (Year 1); $2,500 per artist (Year 2) Total Direct Support for Artists: $40,500 (Combined Year 1 & 2) Interested artists are reviewed and selected by Jury and in consultation with Open Studios Advisory Committee  Creative Marketplace: Exhibitions Participating Artists: 160 (4-10 per corporate location) - Artists selected from Cambridge Open Studios roster and database - Artists living and/or working in Cambridge eligible to participate Corporate Partners: 12 Exhibition Locations: 18 Artist Honorariums: $250 per artist (Year 1 & 2) Artist Sales: 100% of sale to artist Total Direct Support for Artists: $40,000 (Combined Year 1 & 2) Artists are selected in direct partnership with Corporate Partners based on the needs and criteria established by the Partner environment where artwork will be exhibited (e.g. hospital vs. corporate lobby or business)
