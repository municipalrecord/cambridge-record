ATTACHMENTA ORDINANCE COMMITTEE MEETING, MAY 11, 2016 RE: STERN, et al PETITION We're here to present the current Stern, et al Petition. For those of you who are not that tamiliar with this Petition, it is the outcome of 18 months of work, expense, and collaboration between the Richard Avenue Neighbors and 2551 Massachusetts Ave. LLC. When this project was first presented to the Planning Board, many of the PB members expressed the idea that the developer was "trying to do too much" The Richard Avenue Neighbors strongly agreed. The PB directed both parties Developer and Neighborhood - to work together to come up with a solution satisfactory to them at. We have done so. It is the current Stern, et al Petition. both. The change the Petition seeks to make is to adjust the northern boundary of the North Massachusetts Avenue subdistrict to terminate at the centerline of Richard Avenue rather than at the western end of the 2551 site. This allows the 2551 site to revert to BA-2 base zoning What the PB meant when they said to the developer 'you're trying to do too much' was that the Developer was forced to fit his project to all the zoning requirements of the site, and there were simply too many zoning hoops to jump through to produce anything which respected the neighborhood. as well as the Mass Avenue guidelines for a pedestrian-friendly area. I will quote the words of Planning Board Chairman Theodore Cohen from his letter following our recent presentation of the Stern, et al Petition to the PB. ": The Board would not typically support a petition to change the zoning of one small area in response to a redevelopment proposal. However, in this case, having seen an awkward development proposal that attempted to adhere to zoning as closely as possible, the Board believes that the current zoning may not be well suited to this specific area" What is lost with passage of the Stern, et al Petition, is the possibility for retail at a site at which it is nearly impossible to accommodate retail. What is gained is four very handsome townhouses which are an enhancement to the neighborhood.
