A communication transmitted from Louis A. DePasquale, City Manager, relative to an update on the Gore Street construction project

January 23, 2018 Dear Neighbor, The City of Cambridge and DivcoWest, the developer for the Cambridge Crossing (formerly North Point) Projects will be undertaking construction on Gore Street between Monsignor O’Brien Highway and Warren Street beginning in March 2018. The Construction will include a new sewer main, full-depth roadway paving, fully ADA complainant sidewalks and additional street trees. While the nature and location of the work should not adversely impact nearby properties, DivcoWest is offering the property owners along Gore Street the opportunity to participate in a pre-construction survey of their homes completed by a third-party contractor. Pre-construction surveys are being conducted by Pre-Seis, Inc, starting January 22, 2018. These pre-construction surveys are consistent with standard protocols for utility projects throughout the City. The pre-construction survey is intended to be of use to both homeowners and DivcoWest in the unexpected event of a claim for damages arising from work during the construction period. The pre-construction survey coupled with a subsequent post-construction survey will provide documentation with which to evaluate any potential claim. Without such pre-construction and post-construction surveys, it may not be possible to ascertain whether damage has been caused by the construction activities that are part of this project. If you choose to participate, a copy of the completed survey will be provided to you. While we encourage you to participate in the pre-construction survey, the decision is entirely up to the individual homeowner. If you choose to participate, please call [phone removed]⁠ or email [email removed] or return the form below before February 1, 2018, and we will schedule a surveyor to visit your home and perform the survey at your earliest convenience. We will take every precaution to avoid unnecessary inconvenience during the process. Our goal is make sure there is no damage to your property as a result of construction activities. For all questions related to this project, or the pre-construction survey, please contact the CSL Consulting at [email removed] or [phone removed]⁠. You can also visit our project website www.cxconstruction.info to sign up for weekly construction updates and to view additional project information.
