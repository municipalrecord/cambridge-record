PETERSON City Manager Deputy City Manager To: Louis DePasquale, City Manager From: Lisa Peterson, Chair of Community Preservation Act Committee Date: September 17,2018 Re: Allocation and Appropriation ofFY2019 CPA Funds The purpose of this memo is to summarize the process of the Community Preservation Act Committee (CPAC) and to convey its recommendations made by the CPAC on September 5, 2018. Recommendations were based on the City's staff presentations and public comments heard at the CPA public hearings held on June 13, 2018 and July 19, 2018, public input from letters, emails, online project requests, petitions received since the beginning of the process, and CPAC discussions at working CPAC meetings on May 23, 2018 and September 5, 2018. There was a total of 27 public speakers, from the public hearing on projects and the public hearing on allocations, along with 34 communications received for CPA project requests. Nineteen public speakers supported the 80% allocation to affordable housing and four petitions were received with a total of 362 signatures supporting affordable housing. Six members of the public supported a 60% allocation to affordable housing and 20%, 20% split between historic preservation and open space projects. A summary of the total comments heard from both hearings are listed below.
