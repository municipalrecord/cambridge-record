5 Research on Short-Term Cash Assistance Short-term interventions can lead to positive, but modest, short-term effects • Short-term cash payments effectively solve some, but not all, economic challenges • No large-scale economic changes • Reduced food insecurity and mental distress • Limited evidence of effects on work • Numerous U.S. evaluations, varied study designs and program features
Page 6 of 45
6 Rise Up Cambridge Rise Up Cambridge built on Cambridge RISE and expanded eligibility to additional families Households with children at or under 21 years of age, earning up to 250 percent of the Federal Poverty Level First payments disbursed between June and August 2023. Final payments disbursed between December 2024 and February 2025. Households received $500 per month for 18 months ($9,000 total).

10 Research Design Limitations ● Cannot make causal claims due to absence of a comparison group ● Quantitative survey data may not represent true early snapshot as responses drew on participant recollection ● Qualitative findings are not generalizable given relatively smaller number Strengths ● Using multiple data sources allows triangulation; verification across multiple data sources and methods ● Comparative approach shows outcomes and experiences of families with different characteristics ● Study period includes first 6 months after payments ● Centers racial equity and community engagement

EVALUATION FINDINGS
Page 12 of 45
Participants were grateful for Rise Up, its features, and the support it provided. Contributed to reduced stress, a sense of empowerment, and more time with family. Families experienced meaningful financial relief during the program and incremental progress that may contribute to future economic mobility, but some hardships remained. Higher income families fare better. Some differences were observed across racial or ethnic groups. However, differences may reflect prior circumstances Extensive outreach and an online application made citywide reach possible. Rise Up did not count as income; participants maintained their other benefits. 12 Findings At A Glance
