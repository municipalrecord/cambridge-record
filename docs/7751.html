City of Cambridge CMA #9 Agenda Item Number 2 IN CITY COUNCIL September 9, 2019 September 18, 2019 ORDERED: That the City Council go on record approving the disposition of a leasehold interest of 420 parking spaces and 9,000 square feet of ground floor retail area in the City’s First Street Garage located at 55 First Street (the “Garage”) to Leggatt McCall Properties (“LMP”) pursuant to the provisions of G.L. Chapter 30B and the City’s Disposition Ordinance, Chapter 2.110 of the Cambridge Municipal Code, subject to all of the terms and conditions set forth in the City’s conditional award of the disposition pursuant to the public bidding process conducted by the City under the provisions of G. L. Chapter 30B and subject to the following additional terms and conditions: 1. LMP shall seek in good faith the following amendments to Planning Board Special Permit No. 288 (“PBSP 288”) relating to the redevelopment of the former Edward J. Sullivan Courthouse located at 40 Thorndike Street (the “Courthouse Project”); a. a reduction of the number of required parking spaces by 125 parking spaces; b. an increase of the total number of required affordable residential units at the Courthouse Project from twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48), thereby converting 24,000 square feet of office space to 24,000 square feet of residential space at the Courthouse Project, resulting in a further reduction of the number of required parking spaces by an additional 25 parking spaces; and c. if the amendments to PBSP 288 are approved by the Planning Board, the total number of parking spaces to be leased by LMP in the Garage shall be 270 parking spaces; 2. LMP shall make an additional 3.5-million-dollar ($3,500,000) payment to the Affordable Housing Trust by no later than 18 months following the issuance of a certificate of occupancy for the Courthouse Project; and 3. LMP shall make every good faith effort to lease the childcare space to be included at the Courthouse Project to a local nonprofit operator.
Page 2 of 2
ORDERED: That the City Council, pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 2.110 of the Cambridge Municipal Code, hereby authorizes the City Manager on behalf of the City to dispose of a long-term leasehold interest in the First Street Garage to LMP subject to the above terms and conditions and to enter into an agreement for the leasehold interest with LMP on such terms and conditions as the City Manager determines are necessary or advisable. In City Council September 18, 2019. Adopted by a yea and nay vote:- Yeas 6; Nays 3; Absent 0. Attest:- Anthony I. Wilson, City Clerk A true copy; ATTEST:- Anthony I. Wilson City Clerk
