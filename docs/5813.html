CAMBRIDGE CITY COUNCIL Craig A. Kelley City Councillor CITY HALL, CAMBRIDGE, MASSACHUSETTS 02139 [phone removed] FAX: [phone removed] TTY/TDD: [phone removed] EMAIL: ckelley@cambridgema.gov MEMORANDUM To: Cambridge City Council From: Craig Kelley, City Councillor Date: December 11, 2018 Subject: Surveillance Ordinance Notes (Amended) Dear Mr. Manager and Fellow Councillors, I am excited about Cambridge’s proposed Surveillance Ordinance and am looking forward to passing it on 10 December. This Ordinance will be a giant step forward in providing the accountability and government oversight needed to help ensure emerging and expanding surveillance technologies do not get misused. I have some suggestions to make our proposed Ordinance even stronger and to further mitigate against the possible chilling effect that improperly supervised government surveillance may have on the general public’s willingness and ability to exercise its constitutional rights. I hope the City Council will review the below suggested amendments based on the important principles of accountability and transparency in governance that the proposed Surveillance Ordinance is trying to achieve. • Section 2.128.010: o Add highlighted language: “…civil rights and liberties of any individuals, communities or groups including communities of color or other marginalized communities in the City…” • Section 2.128.020 (B): o Add highlighted language: “…not infringe upon an individual's right to peacefully protest and exercise other lawful and protected Constitutional rights.”
Page 2 of 2
2 • Section 2.128.020 (G)(1)&(2): o Strike G(2)(c), and insert a new paragraph (r) in G(1) that reads as follows: “Manually-operated, non-wearable, handheld digital cameras, audio recorders, and video recorders that are not designed to be used surreptitiously and whose functionality is used for manually capturing and manually downloading video and/or audio recordings” This change would make hand held cameras, cell phones, etcetera subject to the surveillance ordinance, as it is currently not included as “surveillance technology,” by helping to ensure that the use of those devices does not chill the public’s right to exercise their Constitutional rights. • Section 2.128.030 (C)(4): o Add highlighted language: “The potential impact(s) on privacy in the City; the potential impact on the civil rights and liberties of any individuals, communities or groups…” • Section 2.128.070 (7): o Add highlighted language: “Technology that monitors only City employees or contractors in response to complaints of wrongdoing or in order to prevent waste, fraud, or abuse of City resources provided that contracts for City employees and contractors specifically note that such surveillance is exempted from this ordinance.” Thank you all for your attention to this very important matter and to your commitment in protecting the rights of all of us during these challenging times.
