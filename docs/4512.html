City of Cambridge Department of Public Works Owen O'Riordan, Commissioner 147 Hampshire Street Cambridge, MA 02139 theworks@cambridgema.gov Voice: [phone removed] TDD: [phone removed] February 20, 2018 To: Louis DePasquale City Manager From: Katherine F. Watkins, City Engineer Joseph E. Barr, Director of Traffic, Parking, and Transportation Re: Awaiting Report 18-16, dated February 12, 2018 Inman Square In response to the Council Order O-5, dated February 12th, regarding Inman Square, staff report the following: We recognize that the decision to move forward with the current option for the redesign of Inman Square has raised questions from community members, many of which were too detailed or complex to answer during the public meeting held on January 30th. As a result, we are happy to provide additional opportunities to talk with staff and the consultant team about the analysis that was performed and the decision that we reached, in parallel with our work to move forward the design of the new plaza space. UPCOMING COMMUNITY MEETING AND OPEN HOUSES: As part of the on-going design process for the Inman Square project, there are several upcoming opportunities for the community to learn more about the Inman Square project, review the technical analysis with staff, and provide input into the design of the reconfigured plaza. Community Meeting - New Plaza Designs Wednesday, March 7th Cambridge Rindge and Latin School Auditorium, 459 Broadway 6:30-8:30 pm This meeting will focus on three alternative concepts for the reconfigured Vellucci Plaza. Specific items to be discussed include trees, plantings, seating, public art, lighting and paving materials. Open Houses / Review Previous Presentation Thursday, March 1st Wednesday, March 7th Department of Public Works CRLS Auditorium 147 Hampshire Street 459 Broadway 5:00-8:00 pm 4:30-6:00 pm

As a follow-up to the January 30th Community Meeting, staff will be hosting two Open Houses to review various design options which have been considered for the Square over the past year and a half; the strengths and flaws which we discovered during our analyses of each one; the traffic simulation modeling; and the reasoning behind the decision to move forward with the current option. All of the previous powerpoint presentations and a video recording of the January 30th meeting are available on the project webpage www.cambridgema.gov/theworks/inmansquare . MEETING NOTIFICATIONS The attached meeting notice highlighting these opportunities is being hand delivered to the neighborhood, emailed to the project distribution list (over 450 people), posted on sandwich boards in Vellucci Plaza, and will be advertised on the City’s social media accounts.
