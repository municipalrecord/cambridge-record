That the City Council go on record recognizing September 15 through October 15, 2026 as Hispanic Heritage Month.

POR 2026-180·Council meeting Sep 28, 2026·3 pages
Page 1 of 3
POR 2026-180 FIRST IN COUNCIL September 28, 2026 City of Cambridge COUNCILLOR SIMMONS MAYOR SIDDIQUI WHEREAS: Each year, from September 15 through October 15, communities across the United States observe Hispanic Heritage Month, celebrating the histories, cultures, traditions, achievements, and contributions of Hispanic and Latino Americans; and WHEREAS: The observance began in 1968 as Hispanic Heritage Week and was expanded in 1988 to a month-long celebration, with the September 15 start date reflecting the independence anniversaries of several Latin American nations; and WHEREAS: Hispanic and Latino communities represent a rich diversity of cultures, identities, languages, traditions, and experiences, and generations of Hispanic and Latino Americans have made enduring contributions to government, education, business, science, the arts, public service, civil rights, and countless other areas of American life; and WHEREAS: Cambridge is strengthened by its Hispanic and Latino residents, families, workers, students, educators, entrepreneurs, artists, advocates, and community leaders, whose talents, traditions, perspectives, and civic engagement enrich the life and character of our city; and WHEREAS: Hispanic Heritage Month provides an opportunity to honor these contributions, celebrate the many cultures represented within the Hispanic and Latino communities, and deepen our understanding of the histories and experiences that continue to shape Cambridge and the nation; now therefore be it RESOLVED: That the Cambridge City Council go on record recognizing September 15 through October 15, 2026 as Hispanic Heritage Month and in extending its appreciation to the Hispanic and Latino residents of Cambridge for their many contributions to the life of our community; and be it further RESOLVED: That the City Council encourages all members of the Cambridge community to join in celebrating and learning about the diverse histories, cultures, traditions, and achievements of Hispanic and Latino people in Cambridge and throughout the United States.
