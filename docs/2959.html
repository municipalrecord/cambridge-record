We, the citizens of Cambridge and patrons of Massasoit Elks Lodge #129, petition the city to careful consider all the disruption, loss of revenue, and the long term negative effects of the proposed "Mass and Main" construction project. This project has the potential not only to threaten the integrity of the Port/Central square neighborhood but also threaten viability and sustainability of the Massasoit Elks Lodge a 108 year old community institution and business asset. We the undersigned respectfully demand that the, City Council, Planning and Zoning Boards) fully consider the consequences of a project of this magnitude. We ask that you carefully reconsider this project and suspend all permits and construction plans indefinitely.
