City of Cambridge Agenda Item Number 9 IN CITY COUNCIL November 22, 2021 WHEREAS: WHEREAS: At a meeting held on Friday, November 12, 2021 at 5:30 pm, the Board of Election Commissioners voted 3 in favor, 0 against, and 1 absent to submit a recommendation to the City Council requesting the City Council vote to authorize early in-person voting and the early voting locations, dates, and hours for the December 14, 2021 Special State Primary; and In accordance with the provisions of Chapter 255 of the Acts of 2020, as amended, the Board recommends early in-person voting begin on Monday, December 6, 2021 and end on Friday, December 10, 2021 during the hours and at the locations indicated below: Location Cambridge Election Commission Office, 51 Inman Street Hours Monday, December 6, 2021 from 8:30am – 8pm Tuesday, December 7, 2021 from 8:30am – 5pm Wednesday, December 8, 2021 from 8:30am – 5pm Thursday, December 9, 2021 from 8:30am – 5pm Friday, December 10, 2021 from 8:30am to 12pm Now, therefore be it ORDERED: That in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 255 of the Acts of 2020, as amended by Chapter 5 of the Acts of 2021 and Chapter 29 of the Acts of 2021, upon request of the Board of Election Commissioners, the City Council hereby authorizes early voting in-person for the December 14, 2021 Special State Primary for the hours and locations outlined above. In City Council November 22, 2021. Adopted by a yea and nay vote:- Yeas 9; Nays 0; Absent 0. Attest:- Anthony I. Wilson, City Clerk A true copy; ATTEST:- Anthony I. Wilson City Clerk
