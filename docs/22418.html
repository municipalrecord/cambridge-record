Monday, January 6, 2025 5:30PM Regular City Council Meeting THIS MEETING WILL BE TELEVISED (Sullivan Chamber/Hybrid via Zoom) Tuesday, January 7, 2025 2:30PM The Ordinance Committee will hold a public hearing on two Multifamily Zoning Petitions on Tuesday, January 7, 2025, from 2:30 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. There will be public comment at this meeting. This is a continuation of the public hearing that began on November 19, 2024, that reconvened and recessed again on December 4, 2024 and December 19, 2024. One additional meeting has been scheduled for January 16, 2025. THIS MEETING HAS BEEN CANCELLED Wednesday, January 8, 2025 5:00PM The Ordinance Committee will hold a public hearing on two Multifamily Zoning Petitions on Wednesday, January 8, 2025, from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. There will be public comment at this meeting. This is a continuation of the public hearing that began on November 19, 2024, that reconvened and recessed again on December 4, 2024 and reconvened and adjourned on December 19, 2024. One additional meeting has been scheduled for January 16, 2025. THIS MEETING WILL BE TELEVISED (Sullivan Chamber/Hybrid via Zoom) Monday, January 13, 2025 5:30PM The City Council will conduct a Joint Roundtable of the City Council and School Committee to discuss the City’s universal pre-kindergarten program. THIS MEETING WILL BE TELEVISED (Sullivan Chamber/Hybrid via Zoom) Thursday, January 16, 2025 3:00PM The Ordinance Committee will hold a public hearing on Thursday, January 16, 2025 to continue the discussion on two Multifamily Zoning Petitions. There will be no public comment at this meeting. THIS MEETING WILL BE TELEVISED (Sullivan Chamber/Hybrid via Zoom)
