1. Introduction This introduction briefly summarizes the enclosed application for approval of two (2) relocated curb cuts and three (3) new curb cuts associated with the construction of Building 2 located at 200 Main Street, which is being constructed as part of the Kendall Square Initiative South of Main ("SoMa") project. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (the "Applicant"), as owner of the Building 2 site at 200 Main Street (Map 47, Parcel 85) and immediately adjacent property to the south located at 100 Main Street (Map 47, Parcel 90), is submitting five (5) separate curb cut applications via the Cambridge online permitting portal as part of this filing. The (I) Abutter's Forms, (il) Plot Plan and (ill) Circulation Plan included as part of the MIT Kendall Square SoMa Site 2 Design Submission that was reviewed and approved by the Planning Board (the "Circulation Plan") appear immediately following this introduction. Project Summary Building 2 is a proposed mixed-use building that will include commercial lab and office space with ground floor retail and access to a below-grade parking garage. The existing driveway between the Building 2 site and the Sloan Building located on the 100 Main Street parcel, which runs partially across the property line, will be modified in connection with the development of Building 2. The development program for Building 2 is consistent with the Development Program contained in the SoMa Special Permit as modified. The proposed curb cuts described in these application materials are related to relocation of the entrances to the existing driveway between the Building 2 site and the Sloan Building and new entrances to the proposed underground parking garage and loading areas for Building 2 as shown on the enclosed plans. Description of Proposed New Curb Cut There are two (2) proposed relocated curb cuts associated with modifications to the existing driveway between the Building 2 site and the Sloan Building which provides access to the Hermann Garage located on the 100 Main Street parcel. One curb cut is located on the east side of Wadsworth Street, south of the parking garage. An adjacent existing curb will be discontinued. The other curb cut associated with this driveway is located on the south side of Main Street on the Building 2 site. An adjacent existing curb cut will be discontinued. As noted above, this driveway will serve Building 2, Hermann Garage and the Sloan Building, each of which is owned by the Applicant or a related entity. The three (3) proposed new curb cuts are located on the east side of Wadsworth Street adjacent to proposed Building 2. The southmost proposed curb cut will provide access to the underground parking garage. The two proposed curb cuts to the north will provide access to loading facilities serving Building 2. The locations of the proposed curb cuts were approved by the Planning Board as part of the Final Development Plan consistent with the provisions of Section 13.88.6(b)(ii) of the City of Cambridge Zoning Ordinance, which allows for the Planning Board to approve locations of curb cuts that deviate from the requirements of Section 6.43 of said Zoning Ordinance. See the Circulation Plan. End of Introduction
