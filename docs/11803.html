December 10, 2021 Cambridge Inspectional Services Department 831 Massachusetts Avenue Cambridge MA 02139 RE: Statement of Attempts to Obtain Abutter Signatures for Curb Cut Application To whom it may concern, This curb cut application is being requested as part of the redevelopment of the Cambridge Housing Authorities' Jefferson Park Federal property at 266 Rindge Ave in Cambridge. In accordance with requirements for abutter signatures required for a curb cut application, the attached letter, plan and City of Cambridge Application for Driveway Cuts and Openings Form were mailed to all abutting property owners requesting their signature on November 22, 2021. 123 letters in total were mailed to owners of the abutting properties, according to the address indicated on the Cambridge Assessor's website. This included the 7 properties on Rindge Ave directly across from Jefferson Park Federal (263, 265, 271, 277, 281 and 289 Rindge Ave), the 100 condominium owners at The Brickworks (310, 312, 314, 316, 318, and 320 Rindge Ave, the North Cambridge Catholic Cemetery, and the 15 condominium owners at 147 Sherman St., which abuts the Southeast corner of the property. Signatures were not provided for the adjacent Jefferson Park State property, as it is managed by the CHA and is owned by a CHA affiliate, Returned forms were requested no later than December 10th, 2021. As of the 10" of December, only two forms have been returned, with a total of three individual signatures. These forms are attached, and all three indicate their approval of the curb cut request. Please forward any questions on this curb cut application or on the collection of abutter's signatures to Joe Bednar [phone removed] [email removed]. Sincerely, Joe Bednar Project Manager Cambridge Housing Authority 362 Green Street, Cambridge, MA 02139 • p [phone removed] /TDD [phone removed] x112 f [phone removed] • www.cambridge-housing.org

December 22, 2021 Cambridge Inspectional Services Department 831 Massachusetts Avenue Cambridge MA 02139 RE: Curb Cut Application Summary To whom it may concern, This curb cut application is being requested as part of the redevelopment of the Cambridge Housing Authorities' Jefferson Park Federal property at 266 Rindge Ave in Cambridge. Modification of (2) two existing curb cuts is being requested. Dimensions and other information are provided here that were not compatible with the online form. If there is a discrepancy between a dimension on the form and this narrative, please assume the narrative is correct, as the online form required entering a digit and NA or a reference to this summary was not an option on the form as recommended by ISD staff. The attached certified plot plan shows the existing conditions, which include two existing curb cuts along Rindge Ave. The existing westernmost existing driveway serves as an entrance to the existing Head Start parking lot and the existing easternmost driveway located across from the intersection of Jackson Street and Rindge Ave. serves as the main entrance to the existing Jefferson Park Federal development, AKA Jackson Place. The attached JP Curb Cut Plan shows the (2) two proposed curb cut dimensions and details. The setback of the proposed buildings will be 15 feet and the frontage of the property along Rindge Ave. is 275.9 feet. As you can see from the attached C1.10 Overall Layout Plan drawing, driveway length and area as requested by the online form do not seem applicable to this proposed development. The proposed arrangement of roads (driveways) includes parallel parking for 152 vehicles, of which 17 will be replacing parking required for the adjacent Jefferson Park State development. In total, 135 parking spaces at the Jefferson Park Federal will be provided for this site. Design of the roads (driveways) and parking have been designed in coordination with the CFD, Traffic Parking and Transportation, and DPW departments. 362 Green Street, Cambridge, MA 02139 • p [phone removed] /TDD [phone removed] x112 f [phone removed] www.cambridge-housing.org
