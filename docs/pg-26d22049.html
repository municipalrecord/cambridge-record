How is the Plan reviewed and updated? • Annual reporting on the progress of the NZAP by OOS staff • The Plan requires oversight and annual review by the advisory Climate Committee • The NZAP is required to be updated every 5 years through a full review and community-based process. • The last update process was conducted in 2021, with Council adoption in 2023. New Buildings • As one of the first, Cambridge adopted the Specialized Stretch Code in 2023, requiring all new buildings to be highly energy efficient. • 57 Massachusetts municipalities have now adopted the SSC • Further, Cambridge is one of 10 Fossil Fuel Free communities in Massachusetts allowed to require nearly all new buildings, major renovations, and building additions to only use all-electric energy systems.
