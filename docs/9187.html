From : Ron Witte Sarah Whiting 227 Concord Ave, #4 Cambridge, MA 02138 [phone removed] [email removed] To: Victor Pereira 17 Park Street Stoneham, MA 02180 RE: 33 Bristol Street – Introductions and Curb Cut Modification Dear Mr. Pereira, We are writing to you to introduce ourselves as the new owners of 33 Bristol Street, next door to your building at 8-10 Berkshire Street. As you might imagine. We are very happy to be moving into your neighborhood. Dave and Deb, the previous owners, told us many good things about all of their neighbors and life in your neighborhood. Those stories were a big part of what made us so excited about 33 Bristol Street. There will be two of us living in the building. We are architects and teachers. We recently returned to Cambridge after having been away for about 15 years. We have two cats, one car, and like to cook. We are also writing to let you know a bit about our plans. Here are some of the things that we hope to do: • Relocation of the roll-up door and curb cut (the City has asked us to send you the attached “Abuttor’s Form” as part of their approval process. We would much appreciate your taking a moment to fill this form out, sign it, and return it to us in the enclosed envelope). • Replacement of windows to make them energy efficient. We are also enlarging two windows to get more light into the building and adding a skylight in the upper roof. • A new door into the side yard facing 8-10 Berkshire Street. • Reopening the original 1920s garage doors on Bristol Street to make them windows. • A roof deck on the one-story area of the building along Bristol Street. • A new door onto the roof deck. • A solar energy system on the upper roof.
