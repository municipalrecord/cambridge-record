Trees of Cambridge: How healthy are they? • Over 19,000 City trees • Approximately 130 different species. • The Earthwatch Institute published condition ratings for over half of these trees in 2016. • Of the 2,837 trees planted between 2008 and 2015, all but 105 of these trees have been examined and 78% have been rated as “Good”. • Of the “older” trees that were assessed 89% of those were rated as good or fair.
