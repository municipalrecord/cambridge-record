APP 2019-3

A 1 illuminated projecting sign and 7 awnings at 822 Somerville Avenue

How it started
Jan 28, 2019 · Requested by Citizens Bank: a request that needs the Council’s permission.
What happened
Jan 28, 2019 · Held by charter right exercised by Councillor Devereux; the item returns at the next meeting
Feb 4, 2019 · Placed on unfinished business
Feb 11, 2019 to Apr 1, 2019 · No action taken on the Calendar The minutes of Feb 11, 2019 (p. 5), Feb 25, 2019 (p. 10), Mar 4, 2019 (p. 6), Mar 18, 2019 (p. 5), Mar 25, 2019 (p. 6) and Apr 1, 2019 (p. 8) print “RESULT: NO ACTION TAKEN” under it on the Calendar, Unfinished Business.
Apr 8, 2019 · No action taken on the Calendar; a communication referred to this application The minutes of Apr 8, 2019 print “RESULT: NO ACTION TAKEN” under it on the Calendar, Unfinished Business (p. 14). They print a communication of the Porter Square Neighborhood Association “regarding Citizens Bank sign application located at 822 Somerville Avenue from unfinished business item #4”, COM 189 #2019, “RESULT: REFERRED TO CALENDAR #4” (p. 17). Calendar Item #4 that night is this application.
Apr 22, 2019 to Apr 29, 2019 · No action taken on the Calendar The minutes of Apr 22, 2019 (p. 22) and Apr 29, 2019 (p. 14) print “RESULT: NO ACTION TAKEN” under it on the Calendar, Unfinished Business.
May 6, 2019 · Suspension of the rules to bring forward a late communication on this application carried The minutes of May 6, 2019 print “Vice Mayor Devereux moved suspension of the rules in order to dispense with the regular order of business to bring forward a late Communication and Reports from Other City Officers” and “on a voice vote the rules were - Suspended”; that communication, COF 2019 #27, was “REFERRED TO THE CALENDAR #3” (p. 7).
May 6, 2019 · ✅ Sign request approved. Permission granted. (Order adopted)
View the roll call
The minutes of May 6, 2019 print the result as “ADOPTED BY AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF EIGHT MEMBERS” (p. 7). They print the count only and name no member. · photos: City of Cambridge

The minutes of January 28, 2019 print the request as “a 1 non-illuminated projecting sign and 7 awnings at the premises numbered 822 Somerville Avenue” (p.15); the city’s agenda outline of that night and the minutes of the later nights it was on the Calendar print “a 1 illuminated projecting sign” (May 6, 2019, p.7). The two records differ, and the question is open.

What’s next
🚪 End of the line: permission granted.
