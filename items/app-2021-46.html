To whom it may concern,

Identiti is the signage provider for One Medical and we have been granted the project for the new One Medical site at 822 Sommerville Ave in Cambridge, MA.

Since we are the primary signage vendor for One Medical, we do not have a contract on a site by site basis. This letter is to be used as our contract for the city and permitting process.

If there are any additional questions or information, please feel free to reach out to Back Bay Sign Company (who the subcontractor for this project) or myself.
