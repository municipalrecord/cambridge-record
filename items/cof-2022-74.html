How it started
Jun 13, 2022 · Filed by Paula M. Crane, Interim City Clerk, passing along an update regarding legislative activity. Any city officer can send the Council a communication.
What happened
Jun 13, 2022 · 📁 Placed on file. This was an informative communication; there were no decisions to make.
