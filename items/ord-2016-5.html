AN ORDINANCE

In amendment to the Ordinance entitled "Municipal Code of the City of Cambridge" be amended as follows: That Chapter 12.16 of the Municipal Code entitled "Streets and Sidewalks Use Regulations" be amended in Section 12.16.030 entitled "Trees-Climbing-Signposting-Fastening horses" to add after the word "animal" in the second sentence the words "bicycle, moped, scooter, motorcycle, or any other form of conveyance." Passed to a second reading at the City Council meeting held on May 9, 2016 and on or after May 23, 2016 the question comes on passing to be ordained. ATTEST:- Donna P. Lopez

City Clerk
