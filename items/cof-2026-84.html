CREATING POCKET PARKS

9 Height is used to incentivize pocket parks in the zoning. Any development above 6 stories is required to provide 15% publicly accessible open space. Since the height is limited to 6 stories for most of the corridor, that largely applies to the Lechmere (CAM-10) and Webster/Windsor (CAM-12) sub-districts. There are different ways property owners can contribute towards providing a meaningful public realm – both permanent and flexible temporary spaces.

DESIGN REVIEW FOR NEW DEVELOPMENT OVER 4 STORIES

11 Cambridge Street Districts are considered “Areas of Special Planning Concern”. Most development must go through some kind of advisory review:

Staff Advisory Consultation: 0-2,000 sf (non-residential only)

Public Advisory Consultation: 2,000-50,000 sf

Planning Board Advisory Consultation: 50,000-75,000 sf

Buildings over 4 stories would require, at least, a Public Advisory Consultation. This is a public meeting with public comment and staff comment. Residential uses greater than 75,000 sf need a project review special permit.

Building Use

• This is an allowed use in most districts except residential districts. • There are other building/fire code regulations that would apply differently depending on size of building and type of use.

Building Size

• New lab buildings require building footprints that are too large for the vast majority parcels on Cambridge St. • Most parcels on Cambridge St are only 100 feet deep. • Lab uses are limited to a height of 3 stories or 35 feet. • Non-residential uses are limited to an FAR of 1.0

ENCOURAGING LOCAL RETAIL

13 While zoning cannot regulate businesses by ownership, e.g. chains vs. local, the city does provide a variety of programs that are aimed at local businesses including financial and educational/technical support. Financial:

Storefront Improvement Program

Reimburses funding for exterior façade renovations and ADA accessibility upgrades.

Small Business Enhancement Program

Offers reimbursement grants to retail, restaurant, home-based, and creative businesses for equipment, marketing materials, or interior improvements.

Retail Interior Accessibility Program

Supports interior renovations making businesses more sensory and physically accessible

Small Business E-Commerce Program

Supports online and digital marketing improvements.

Educational/Technical Support:

Small Business Coaching Program

Provides eligible small businesses with up to 4 hours of free, one-on-one sessions with industry experts in financial planning, restaurant operations, marketing, legal setups, succession planning, and more .

Business Development Workshops

Free, city-organized workshops cover topics such as marketing, social media, supplier diversity, e-commerce, accounting, and starting a business. These are open to all Cambridge small businesses.

Small Business Succession Planning Program

Offers resources and events to help business owners transition ownership—via selling, employee buyouts, or succession strategies.

Site Search and Vacant Storefront Program

Track ground floor vacant storefront data, provide site location services to businesses interested in locating or expanding in Cambridge, and connect eligible businesses to the Vacant Storefront Tax Program (state program). THANKS!
