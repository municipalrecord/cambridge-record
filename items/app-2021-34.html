Article 7 limits projecting signs to one per ground floor establishment, and awnings count as projecting signs - so you could have the blade sign or the awning, but not both (unless there is no sign on the awning).

What is the total square footage of signage proposed? Some of the square footage in the application form seems inaccurate.
