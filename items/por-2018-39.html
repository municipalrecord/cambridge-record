POR 2018-39

Asked the City Manager to consult with the appropriate City personnel to determine why there continues to be significant audio and video difficulties during live internet broadcasts of City Council meetings, how these difficulties can be resolved

How it started
Feb 26, 2018 · Proposed by Councillor E. Denise Simmons: a formal request from the Council to the administration.
What happened
Feb 26, 2018 · Councillor Zondervan’s amendments carried The minutes of Feb 26, 2018 print “His proposed amendments were to add three new WHEREAS paragraphs” and “He further amended the aforementioned order by adding a new second ORDERED” and “The question now came on the amendments offered by Councillor Zondervan - and on a voice vote the amendments – Carried.” (p. 34).
Feb 26, 2018 · Held by charter right the city's note: “CHARTER RIGHT EXERCISED BY COUNCILLOR SIMMONS AS AMENDED”
Mar 5, 2018 · Amendment by substitution carried The minutes of Mar 5, 2018 print “Councillor Simmons explained that she exercised her Charter Right to give herself and her colleague to work through some of the language. At this time she moved to amend by substitution.” and “on a voice vote the amendment by substitution – Carried.” (pp. 8-9).
Mar 5, 2018 · Added Councillor Quinton Zondervan as co-sponsor The minutes of Mar 5, 2018 print “He moved to amend to add himself as a co-sponsor.” and “The question now came on the amendment to add Councillor Zondervan as a co-sponsor – and on a voice vote the amendment – Carried.” (pp. 8-9).
Mar 5, 2018 · ✅ Order adopted as amended. The Council's request went to the City Manager.
View the roll call
The minutes of Mar 5, 2018 print the result as “ORDER ADOPTED AS AMENDED BY SUBSTITUTION ON AN AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF NINE MEMBERS” (p. 9). They print the count only and name no member. · photos: City of Cambridge

📨 Answered: the report came back as CMA 2019 #8 (Jan 14, 2019).

City of Cambridge
IN CITY COUNCIL
February 26, 2018

ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to direct the appropriate City personnel to place a contact number on the City Council’s website for the public to call when experiencing difficulties in viewing City Council meetings online; and be it further ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to consult with the appropriate City personnel to determine why there continues to be significant audio and video difficulties during live internet broadcasts of City Council meetings, how these difficulties can be resolved, and to report back to the City Council in a timely manner.

IN CITY COUNCIL · March 5, 2018

ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to direct the appropriate City personnel to place a contact number on the City Council’s website for the public to call when experiencing difficulties in viewing City Council meetings online; and be it further ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to work with 22-Cityview to explore the possibility of simulcasting live streams of City Council meetings on YouTube or an equivalent free-to-use live-streaming platform with sufficiently high bandwidth; and be it further ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to consult with the appropriate City personnel to determine why there continues to be significant audio and video difficulties during live internet broadcasts of City Council meetings, how these difficulties can be resolved, and to report back to the City Council in a timely manner. In City Council March 5, 2018. Adopted as amended by the affirmative vote of nine members. Attest:- Donna P. Lopez, City Clerk A true copy; ATTEST:- Donna P. Lopez, City Clerk
