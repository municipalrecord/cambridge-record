Our Open Space Vision

Cambridge’s parks and open spaces are essential and interconnected spaces that welcome all members of our diverse community. Our parks and open spaces: welcome people to engage in a wide variety of active or passive activities. In our parks and open spaces people can come to be together or spend time alone. are inclusive and accessible places for gathering, relaxing, being active, and playing in well-maintained facilities, landscapes, and nature. foster culture and the arts, providing spaces for spectacle and performance to express and inspire creativity. support our quality of life and provide places and moments where memories are made.
