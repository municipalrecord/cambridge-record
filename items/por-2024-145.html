POR 2024-145

Asked the City Manager to direct the appropriate City staff to prominently incorporate recognition of the Massachusett Tribe during key official events, documents, websites, and communications, establish a living memorial in Cambridge that honors the Massachusett Tribe, with special recognition of Sqa Sachem for her leadership and enduring legacy and place a plaque with this language in a prominent location within City Hall, ensuring that this acknowledgment remains visible and lasting

How it started
Nov 4, 2024 · Proposed by Councillors Simmons, McGovern, Toner, and Wilson: a formal request from the Council to the administration.
What happened
Nov 4, 2024 · Amendment failed Mover not named in the minutes; failed 1–8.The minutes of Nov 4, 2024 record this vote but not the wording of the amendment.
