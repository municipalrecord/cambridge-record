City Hall lighting in observance of Ovarian Cancer Awareness Month

How it started
Sep 9, 2019 · Proposed by Councillors Simmons, Siddiqui, and Mallon: a formal request from the Council to the administration.
What happened
Sep 9, 2019 · Suspension of the rules to take up the late orders and resolutions carried The minutes of Sep 9, 2019 print “THE COUNCIL VOTED UNANIMOUSLY TO SUSPEND IT RULES TO TAKE UP THE FOLLOWING LATE POLICY ORDERS AND RESOLUTIONS” (p. 54).
Sep 9, 2019 · Councillor Simmons moved to add Councillor Siddiqui and Councillor Mallon as sponsors; the motion passed by voice vote The minutes of Sep 9, 2019 print “The motion passed by a voice vote of nine councillors.” (p. 55). A voice vote records the outcome, not individual positions.
Sep 9, 2019 · ✅ Order adopted as amended

City of Cambridge
IN CITY COUNCIL
September 9, 2019

ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to instruct the appropriate City personnel to light the exterior of City Hall in teal from September 17-18, 2019 in observance of Ovarian Cancer Awareness Month.
