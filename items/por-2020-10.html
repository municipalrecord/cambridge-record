City of Cambridge
IN CITY COUNCIL
January 27, 2020

ORDERED: That the City Manager be and is hereby requested to confer with relevant City Departments, and, where relevant, the MBTA and counterparts in other municipalities for routes not wholly in Cambridge, on the feasibility of instituting and funding a fare- free pilot bus program; and be it further ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to report back to the City Council in a timely manner. In City Council January 27, 2020. Adopted by the affirmative vote of nine members. Attest:- Anthony I. Wilson, City Clerk A true copy; ATTEST:- Anthony I. Wilson, City Clerk
