Agenda Items ▸ Policy Orders

POR 2018-6

Asked the City Manager to report back to the City Council regarding efforts to expand the number of electric vehicle charging stations, the feasibility of appropriately placing electric vehicle chargers on residential streets where there is need, the status of possible City fleet replacement to electric vehicles, expanded outreach and education on available rebates and incentive programs, and the feasibility of requiring developers to include a greater number of electric vehicle charging stations in new or substantially renovated multi-unit buildings

How it started
Jan 22, 2018 · Proposed by Vice Mayor Devereux and Councillor Zondervan: a formal request from the Council to the administration.
What happened
Jan 22, 2018 · Held by charter right exercised by Councillor Simmons; the item returns at the next meetingThe minutes of Jan 29, 2018 print “(CHARTER RIGHT EXERCISED BY COUNCILLOR SIMMONS ON JANUARY 22, 2018)” (p. 9).
Jan 29, 2018 · Amendment carried Offered by Councillor Zondervan; carried on a voice vote.
in the seventh paragraph by adding the words “of a new vehicle” after the word “ownership”
The clerk's words, from the minutes of Jan 29, 2018, pp. 9 and 11: “The amendment carried on a voice vote.”
Jan 29, 2018 · Amendment carried Offered by Councillor Zondervan; moved by Mayor McGovern; carried on a voice vote.
adding a new eight paragraph which reads as follows: WHEREAS: The cost of used electric vehicles such as for example the Nissan Leaf is comparable to the cost of same model year used gasoline powered vehicles and therefore used electric vehicles are equally affordable as used gasoline powered vehicles to community members who are looking to buy a used car for commuting to work or for conducting work; and
The clerk's words, from the minutes of Jan 29, 2018, pp. 9 and 11: “Mayor McGovern moved the second amendment which was to add a new eighth paragraph. The amendment carried on a voice vote.”
Jan 29, 2018 · Amendment carried Offered by Councillor Zondervan; carried on a voice vote.
add a new nineteenth paragraph which reads as follows: ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to explore ways to generate revenues from permitting electric vehicle charging stations on public property, such as adding a small surcharge to be collected by the City, and using the resulting funds to subsidize electric vehicle charging stations in low income communities and/or other programs to assist mobility for low income communities.
The clerk's words, from the minutes of Jan 29, 2018, pp. 9 and 11: “The third amendment was to add a new nineteenth paragraph. The amendment carried on a voice vote.”
Jan 29, 2018 · ✅ Order adopted as amended. The Council's request went to the City Manager. (one absent)
