Roll call of Dec 20, 2021 · from the verbatim transcript · photos: City of Cambridge

Dec 20, 2021: Before this roll call Councillor McGovern said he had been advised by the Ethics Commission not to take part: “So I will be abstaining from this vote” (transcript, page 235). The clerk’s roll prints him “Yes” and the count “Yes-9, No-0” (page 236). After the roll call Councillor Toomey said he had thought the two actions were to be voted separately and that he “was gonna vote no on referring”; he moved a suspension of the rules “to vote present on that …”, which passed eight in favor with Councillor Toomey present (page 237). The roll call on the referral was not taken again, and the record gives no new count for it. The Council’s Rules of 2020-2021 (as amended 14 June 2021), the edition in force that night, say nothing about changing a vote after the roll call; Rule 37 sends any question of parliamentary practice the Rules do not provide for to “Roberts’ Rules of Order”. Rule 15 says an application to be excused from voting “must be made before the vote of the City Council has been decided.” The transcript records no request for unanimous consent.
