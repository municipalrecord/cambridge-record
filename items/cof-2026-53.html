Active Uses include:

• All institutional uses including daycares, libraries, community centers. • Some office uses like a dentist’s office, accountant’s office, or insurance agency. This does NOT include general offices, banks, or labs. • All retail and consumer services uses.

Floor Area and Location Standards:

• 60% of interior floor area within 50’ of the street

Mass Ave

Require Active uses for buildings greater than 4 stories From Cambridge Common to Linear Park.

Expand MAS-12A

Active uses required for buildings greater than 4 stories in MAS-18 *General support for this approach at 3/11 Ordinance

Committee Meeting

Existing Cambridge

Street Active Uses required for buildings above a certain height.

Cambridge St:

Greater than 6 stories in Lechmere (CAM-10) and

Webster/Windsor (CAM-12)

*Previous zoning (BA) allowed up to 6 stories with no active use requirement - same as new CAM-6 zoning.

Option 1: Same as Mass Ave

Active uses required above 4 stories in all CAM districts.

Option 2: Stronger

Active uses required above 3 stories in all CAM districts. Option 3…

Option 3: Active Use Nodes

Why these nodes? Existing business activity is fairly consistent throughout the corridor. Future business development should be prioritized in areas closest to existing squares, transformative areas, and transportation infrastructure.

Option 3: Active Use Nodes

Active uses required above 3 stories from Inman to Windsor Street and Fifth Street to Lechmere. Active uses required above 4 stories between Windsor Street and Fifth Street.

General Recommendation:

Rezone Warren Street and

O’Brien Hwy edges where Active

Uses are not a priority. Considerations Option 1 • Some 4-story residential without active use where 6- story mixed-use buildings are infeasible. • More housing, possibly less active use. Option 3 • Stronger active use requirements in nodes could help prioritize business activity in those areas. • More detailed approach may add complexity without much additional benefit. Option 2 • Could be some 3- story residential where 6-story mixed buildings are infeasible, but redevelopment is less likely altogether. • Less housing, possibly more existing active use space is preserved but not guaranteed.
