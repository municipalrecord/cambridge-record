Lexington Ave Speed Observation

On Tuesday, December 5, we recorded the speeds of all vehicles traveling south on Lexington Ave between Huron Ave and Brattle St. The observation was made during morning commuting hours, between 8:00 a.m. and 8:30 a.m. There were 59 speed observations made during the time period, or roughly one every 30 seconds. The posted speed limit on Lexington Ave is 25 m.p.h., but residents have complained that vehicles using the popular cut-through regularly drive at speeds exceeding the legal limit.

Observation of December 5 only offer one data point, but they seem to support the residents' claim. Of the 59 vehicles that were observed, the average speed was 26 m.p.h., as was the median. Thirteen drivers were observed traveling 5 miles or more over the speed limit, about 22 percent. Four drivers were recorded traveling at the highest observed speed, 33 m.p.h.
