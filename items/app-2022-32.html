To whom it may concern, This letter is being submitted to express our interest as the organizers of the Central Square Farmers Market to have two electrical pole banners displayed along Mass Ave. on both sides of the street for the duration of the farmers market season, which lasts from May 16th until November 22nd. This banner will advertise that the market recurs weekly on Mondays from 12 to 6pm and features a $15 SNAP Match that Cambridge residents can use to stretch their food budget each of our 28 market days.

The location of the electrical poles on which these banners would be displayed is right next to where the market takes place, along the entire block of Norfolk St. between Mass Ave. and Bishop Allen Dr. Thank you for considering this proposal, and we look forward to any input you might have. Sincerely,
