Use requirements on Cambridge Street and the Porter

Square PUD subdistricts for sites that redevelop as single parcels. • Adopted Feb. 2025 • Biggest housing reform in 100 years • Multifamily housing allowed as-of- right citywide • Up to 4 stories allowed in all neighborhoods, 6 stories with inclusionary housing • Mixed-use districts allow 6-10 stories of housing or more • No minimum lot size or limits on units • Reduced setback requirements

Background: Multifamily Zoning Amendment

Adopted Dec. 2025 / Jan. 2026 • Implements the recommendations of the Mass

Ave Planning Study (2025) and Our Cambridge

Street Study (2023). • Allows up to 6 stories of housing throughout Cambridge Street and 12 stories on Mass Ave. • Allows up to 10 stories of housing in Lechmere and 12 stories in the Webster/Windsor area. • Allows up to 18 stories of housing in Porter Square in exchange for public open space and retail concentration. • Incentivizes active ground floor uses like retail and restaurants. • Establishes design standards to encourage visually appealing buildings and a vibrant streetscape.

Mass Ave + Cambridge Street Rezoning

buildings above a certain height. Current requirement for part of Mass Ave. 5 stories residential with active ground story use 4 stories residential-only 12 stories residential with active ground story use Why require active uses only above a certain height? • Most new development will want to unlock taller heights (6 stories or more). • Flexibility for more unique sites and cases where height is not the main goal (e.g., rehab/enlargement). • Prevent creating new nonconformities for existing all- residential buildings. • Forcing commercial space where infeasible can lead to undesirable outcomes (e.g., vacancies, unreasonable rents, or making any redevelopment infeasible).

Active Uses include:

• All institutional uses including daycares, libraries, community centers. • Some office uses like a dentist’s office, accountant’s office, or insurance agency. This does NOT include general offices, banks, or labs. • All retail and consumer services uses.

Active Use Floor Area Modifications

Floor Area Standards:

• 60% of interior floor area within 50’ of the street

Proposed Modifications by Special

Permit: • Planning Board can approve modifications if the intent is being met, such as: • Additional non-residential uses • Minor changes in amount and/or location of active use

Cambridge Street

Recommendations

Option 1: Same as Mass Ave

• Active uses required above 4 stories in all CAM districts.

Option 2: Stronger

• Active uses required above 3 stories in all CAM districts. Considerations Option 1 • Some 4-story residential without active use where 6-story mixed-use buildings are infeasible. • More housing, possibly less active use. Option 2 • Could be some 3-story residential where 6-story mixed buildings are infeasible, but redevelopment is less likely altogether. • Less housing, possibly more existing active use space is preserved but not guaranteed. In both scenarios, it is likely that most development will provide active uses in order to build to 6 stories.
