COF 2019-60

A response to Council Order No. 9 of June 6, 2019 that the City Manager is requested to Instruct the City Solicitor to Draft for Discussion Several Ordinances to reduce or Prohibit Campaign Donations from Donors Seeking to Enter into a Contact, Seeking Approval for a Special Permit or Up-Zoning, Seeking to Acquire Real Estate from the City or Seeking Financial Assistance from the City

Officer Communicationfrom the City Solicitor, Nancy E. Glowa
How it started
Filed by the City Solicitor, Nancy E. Glowa, passing along a response to Council Order No. 9 of June 6, 2019 that the City Manager is requested to Instruct the City Solicitor to Draft for Discussion Several Ordinances to reduce or Prohibit Campaign Donations from Donors Seeking to Enter into a Contact, Seeking Approval for a Special Permit or Up-Zoning, Seeking to Acquire Real Estate from the City or Seeking Financial Assistance from the City. Any city officer can send the Council a communication.
What happened
Nov 13, 2019 · Heard in committee: Government Operations, Rules & Claims Committee The committee’s minutes print “Vice Mayor Devereux moved to request that the City Manager have the City Solicitor report back to the City Council about” three questions and “The motion passed unanimously by voice vote.” (T12-2118.pdf p.2).
Nov 13, 2019 · Placed on file (the committee’s minutes print “RESULT: PLACED ON FILE”, T12-2118.pdf p.3)

The city’s agenda outline describes this as a response “to Council Order No. 9 of June 6, 2019”; the Council’s minutes of June 10, 2019 print that order as item 9, POR 2019 #207 (p.31), and the committee’s minutes of November 13, 2019 print “Council Order No. O-9 of June 10, 2019” (T12-2118.pdf p.1). The records differ on the order’s date, and the question is open.
