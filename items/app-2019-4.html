APP 2019-4

A Zoning Petition Has been received from Melissa Grippo and Christian Grippo, et al, requesting the Maximum Ratio of Floor Area to Lot Area.

Zoning petitionfiled by residentsMGL c.40A §5
How it started
Jan 28, 2019 · Filed by Melissa Grippo and Christian Grippo and fellow residents: state law (MGL c.40A §5) lets ten registered voters put a proposed zoning change before the Council, which must process it on statutory clocks.
What happened
Jan 28, 2019 · ↪ Referred to the Ordinance Committee and the Planning Board. Sent to the Ordinance Committee and Planning Board, as §5 requires. (Referred to committee)
Apr 8, 2019 · The committee’s report (CRT 2019 #25) accepted and placed on file The minutes of Apr 8, 2019 print, under Committee Reports, CRT 2019 #25, a report “for a public hearing held on March 27, 2019 to discuss a zoning petition filed by Melissa and Christopher Grippo et al to amend the Zoning Ordinance by adding at the end of section 5.30.11 a sentence”, and “REPORT ACCEPTED AND PLACED ON FILE” (p. 34) and “PASSED TO A SECOND READING AS AMENDED AT THE CITY COUNCIL MEETING OF APRIL 8, 2019 AND ON OR AFTER APRIL 29, 2019 THE QUESTION COMES ON PASSING TO BE ORDAINED” (p. 34).
Apr 22, 2019 · The committee’s report (CRT 2019 #25) on the Calendar: no action taken The minutes of Apr 22, 2019 print “RESULT: NO ACTION TAKEN” under CRT 2019 #25 on the Calendar (p. 22).
Apr 29, 2019 · Ordained, 9-0, with the committee’s report (CRT 2019 #25) on the Calendar The minutes of Apr 29, 2019 print, under CRT 2019 #25 on the Calendar, “Councillor Mallon moved passage to be ordained of the proposed amendment to the Zoning Ordinance”, “(HERE INSERT ORDINANCE NUMBER 1407)” and “PASSED TO BE ORDAINED [9-0-0]” (p. 15).

The minutes of Jan 28, 2019 name the petitioners “Melissa Grippo and Christian Grippo, et al” (p. 15); the committee’s report in the minutes of Apr 8, 2019 names “Melissa and Christopher Grippo et al” (p. 34). The two records name the second petitioner differently, and the question is open.

What’s next
🚪 End of the line: ordained, Apr 29, 2019, as Ordinance 1407 (minutes p. 15).
