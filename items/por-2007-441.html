POR 2007-441

A proposal to designate appropriate staff from the Department of Public Works, Conservation Commission, Public Health Department, and Environmental Planning division of the Community Development Department to form an Integrated Pest Management Citizens Committee for the purpose of devising a public education campaign to educate businesses, institutions, and the public about ways to avoid harmful use of pesticides

How it started
Sep 10, 2007 · Proposed by Councillors Davis, Decker, Kelley, Maher, and Murphy, Mayor Reeves, Councillor Simmons, and Vice Mayor Toomey: a formal request from the Council to the administration.
What happened
Sep 10, 2007 · ✅ 13.withdrawn with unanimous consent on motion of davis 14.that the city manager is requested to designate appropriate staff from the department of public 14.order adopted works, conservation commission, public health department, and environmental planning division of the community development department to form an integrated pest management citizens committee for the purpose of devising a public education campaign to educate businesses, institutions, and the public about ways to avoid harmful use of pesticides. councillor davis, councillor decker, councillor kelley, councillor maher, councillor murphy, mayor reeves, councillor simmons and vice mayor toomey.
