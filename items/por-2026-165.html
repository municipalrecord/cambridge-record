POR 2026-165

Asks the City Manager to direct the appropriate City staff to illuminate City Hall in teal on Monday, November 2, 2026, in honor of Alzheimer’s Awareness Month

How it started
Sep 14, 2026 · Proposed by Mayor Siddiqui and Vice Mayor Azeem: a formal request from the Council to the administration.
What happened

Nothing yet: this is on the agenda for Sep 14, 2026 and the council has not taken it up. What the council does with it will appear here after the meeting.

Alzheimer’s disease is a progressive brain disorder that impacts memory, thinking and language skills, and the ability to carry out the simplest tasks, and is the leading cause of dementia, affecting nearly 7 million Americans today; and

The Alzheimer’s Foundation of America (AFA), founded in 2002, provides nationwide support, services, and education to individuals, families, and caregivers affected by Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias, and funds research aimed at improving treatment and finding a cure; and

AFA operates a National Toll-Free Helpline ([phone removed]), staffed entirely by licensed social workers, offering direct support, guidance, and connections to local resources for anyone in the United States they live; and

Early detection and intervention can significantly improve quality of life for patients and families, yet too many cases go undiagnosed until the disease has progressed beyond the point where treatments and planning are most effective; and

Nearly 1,600 landmarks participated in AFA’s “Light the World in Teal” initiative last year, making it one of the largest awareness campaigns for any cause in the world; and

By participating, the City of Cambridge would demonstrate support for residents, families, and visitors affected by Alzheimer’s, promote important conversations about early detection and care, and help raise visibility for the cause; now therefore be it

RESOLVED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to direct the appropriate City staff to illuminate City Hall in teal on Monday, November 2, 2026, in honor of Alzheimer’s Awareness Month.

A true copy; ATTEST:- Paula M. Crane,

Interim City Clerk

That the City Manager is requested to direct the appropriate City staff to illuminate City Hall in teal on Monday, November 2, 2026, in honor of Alzheimer’s Awareness Month.
