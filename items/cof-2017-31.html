The Final Official Results from the Municipal Election held on Tuesday November 7, 2017 for City Council

Officer Communicationfrom Tanya L. Ford, Executive Director, Cambridge Election Commission
How it started
Dec 4, 2017 · Filed by Tanya L. Ford, Executive Director, Cambridge Election Commission, passing along the Final Official Results from the Municipal Election held on Tuesday November 7, 2017 for City Council. Any city officer can send the Council a communication.
What happened
Dec 4, 2017 · Amendment carried Moved by Councillor Dennis Carlone; carried on a voice vote.
Councillor Carlone stated that an amended certification of the election has been received from the Board of Election Commissioners and he moved to amend by substitution.
The clerk's words, from the minutes of Dec 4, 2017, p. 24.
Dec 4, 2017 · 📁 Placed on file. (The minutes of Dec 4, 2017 print “AMENDED BY SUBSTITUTION OF REVISED CERTIFICATION WAS PLACED ON FILE”, p. 24.)
