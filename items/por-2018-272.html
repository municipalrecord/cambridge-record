POR 2018-272

Asked the City Manager to conduct a comprehensive, independent planning, and parking study of the neighborhood and use of the First Street Garage

How it started
Nov 5, 2018 · Proposed by Councillor Zondervan, Vice Mayor Devereux, and Councillors Siddiqui and Carlone: a formal request from the Council to the administration.
What happened
Nov 5, 2018 · Suspension of the rules to bring this order forward carried The minutes of Nov 5, 2018 print “Mayor McGovern notified the body of his intention to move suspension of the rules to dispense with the regular order of business to bring forward Policy Orders Three and Four” and “on a voice vote the rules were - Suspended” (p. 22).
Nov 5, 2018 · Amendment carried Moved by Councillor Quinton Zondervan; carried on a voice vote.
to amend the aforementioned order by striking out in the second paragraph “RFP” and inserting in place “disposition”
The clerk's words, from the minutes of Nov 5, 2018, p. 22.
Nov 5, 2018 · Amendment carried Moved by Councillor Quinton Zondervan; carried on a voice vote.
That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to conduct a comprehensive independent planning, traffic and parking study of the neighborhood and use of the First Street Garage within six months and include in the disposition report as required by 2.110 Section B.6, so that the City Council can make an informed decision as to whether the proposed disposition is in the best public interest; and be it further
The clerk's words, from the minutes of Nov 5, 2018, p. 25.
Nov 5, 2018 · Held by charter right the city's note: “EXERCISED BY COUNCILLOR SIMMONS ON ORDER AS AMENDED”
Nov 19, 2018 · Suspension of the rules to bring this item forward carried The minutes of Nov 19, 2018 print “Mayor McGovern moved suspension of the rules in order to dispense with the regular order of business to bring forward Calendar Item # 1” (p. 6) and “on a voice vote the rules were – Suspended” (p. 7).
Nov 19, 2018 · Amendment carried Moved by Councillor Quinton Zondervan; carried on a voice vote.
to delete the word “traffic” in the seventh paragraph
The clerk's words, from the minutes of Nov 19, 2018, p. 7.
Nov 19, 2018 · The related communication (COF 2018 #43) brought forward and placed on file The minutes of Nov 19, 2018 print “Vice Mayor Devereux moved suspension of the rules in order to dispense with the regular order of business to bring forward Communication and Report from Other City Officers Item #3”, COF 2018 #43, and “on a voice vote the rules were – Suspended” (p. 6), and that communication was “REFERRED TO THE CALENDAR #1 AND PLACED ON FILE” (p. 11).
Nov 19, 2018 · ✅ Order adopted as amended. The Council's request went to the City Manager. The minutes of Nov 19, 2018 print “ORDER ADOPTED AS AMENDED BY AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF 8 MEMBERS.” and “COUNCILLOR SIMMONS RECORDED AS PRESENT” (p. 11); they do not name the eight. The clerk’s attested copy of the amended order prints “Adopted as amended by the affirmative vote of eight members.” and “COUNCILLOR SIMMONS RECORDED IN THE NEGATIVE” (Amended Calendar Item # 1, p. 2). The two city records differ on Councillor Simmons: the minutes record her as present, the attested copy in the negative. The difference is unexplained, and the question is open.
That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to conduct a comprehensive, independent planning, and parking study of the neighborhood and use of the First Street Garage, within 6 months and include it in the disposition report as required by 2.110, Section B.6, so that the Council can make an informed decision as to whether the proposed disposition is in the best public interest; and be it further ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to report back on this matter as soon as possible.
The order as adopted, from the clerk’s attested copy (Amended Calendar Item # 1, p. 2).
What’s next
🚪 End of the line: the request (AR 2018 #129) left the ledger when the Council’s term ended, with no formal report filed against it.

City of Cambridge
IN CITY COUNCIL
November 5, 2018

ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to conduct a comprehensive, independent planning and traffic study of the neighborhood and use of the First Street Garage, and include it in the disposition report as required by 2.110, Section B.6, so that the Council can make an informed decision as to whether the proposed disposition is in the best public interest; and be it further ORDERED: That the City Manager be and hereby is requested to report back on this matter as soon as possible.
