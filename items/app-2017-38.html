Attachment to Poles:

• Attachments shall be made with proper hardware as prescribed. • The submitted banner design is 2' wide x 3' high, which is a total of 864 square inches. • Banners will be attached to cobra lamp poles and will have more than 16 foot clearance over the roadway. • One location, noted in the attached, may present an obstruction to a traffic signal. We ask the Electrical Department to advise in this instance. • The banner will be made of 18 oz B/O Stock Material, which is designed to withstand the elements.

The base is made from rust-free cast aluminum and the banners include fiberglass arms that flex in strong winds. • AlphaGrraphics will be printing the banners and providing the installation materials; the City of Cambridge Department of Public Works will install and uninstall the banners. AlphaGraphics can be reached at [phone removed]. Full estimate with further details enclosed. babankr 6-8-17
