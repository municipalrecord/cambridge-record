To the Honorable, the City Council:

In accordance with Chapter 603 of the Acts of 1989, the Board of Election Commissioners submits to the voters of Cambridge a voter guide with the text of proposed ballot questions which will appear as questions on the November 4, 2025 Municipal Election ballot. The Special Act requires that each question is followed by a summary of the proposal and the arguments written by the proponents and opponents of the question.

As the party that voted for the home rule petition, the City Council is the proponent of the ballot question and, by extension, presents the proponent argument position in the guide. To assist the City Council, the Law Department has prepared a draft "pro" position for the voter guide for the City Council's consideration. If the City Council wishes to revise or amend the proposed statement, please note that all statements must be limited to 250 words maximum.

The statement must be provided to the Board of Election Commissioners as soon as possible for preparations for printing. We recommend that the Council vote to adopt this "pro" position, or a revised version, and forward it to the Election Commission. Very Truly Yours, Megan B. Bayer
