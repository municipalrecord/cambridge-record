COVID-19 Testing in K-12 Schools

Prior to schools reopening on October 13, all CPS staff will have opportunity to be tested, during the week of October 5. CPS staff on site will be tested twice a week, on a voluntary basis, as part of a surveillance program. Testing will be offered to all students and staff who may become symptomatic during the school day. CPHD has partnered with the Cambridge charter schools to provide technical assistance and test kits so they too can provide COVID-19 testing to their students and staff.
