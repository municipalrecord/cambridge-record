The Federal Grants update is straightforward, the discussion on future budget priorities will require collaboration between the Council and City administration. This portion of the meeting is intended to identify and prioritize initiatives so that the Council can effectively direct the City Manager and staff in aligning resources, planning, and program development with those goals.

This document and process are designed to help us identify 2-3 shared Council priorities for further scoping by City staff. Importantly, narrowing our focus is not meant to exclude, diminish, or eliminate other goals, all of which will continue to be part of conversations between City staff, City Council, and community members. Rather, it reflects the practical need to concentrate staff capacity and financial planning.

As we work to maintain financial stability and moderate budget growth, it is increasingly important to take a disciplined approach to medium- and long-term spending. This requires both prioritization and early direction-setting, so that staff can effectively plan, sequence work, and allocate resources. Some of the ideas we intend to discuss will have implications on both the operating and capital budgets, and even one-time investments can impact our operating budget through debt service and maintenance.

With that in mind, this process has three primary objectives: 1. identify public investment or programming opportunities based on Council goals, 2. identify within those opportunities a list of 2-3 initiatives to prioritize for City staff to scope, and 3. set up a process by which we work on these initiatives collaboratively throughout the scoping so that we can wholly consider feasibility, financial impacts, and implementation.

The Proposed Approach:

Step 1: Confirm the List of Initiatives

The first step is to identify programming or project opportunities based on Council goals. This step will entail reviewing the below list to ensure it is inclusive of major spending priorities. These ideas are not new - the list comes from ideas discussed as a body over the years. Some of the goals in these priorities may be able to be met through other means like state legislation, private partnerships, or federal grants, but we need to prioritize what we can realistically spend municipal funding and staff time on. 1

Step 2: Identify a Focused Set of Priorities

The second step is to work through a collaborative process to identify within the list, 2-3 initiatives, since only a few initiatives can reasonably be scoped by City staff and the City Manager. That work will involve nuanced discussion and could include a ranking of priorities or a consolidation of priorities. In reviewing these priorities collaboratively, we should each consider: ●​ how the priority is reflective of Council goals ●​ how the priority relates to other ongoing initiatives and spending ●​ the timeline for implementation, and ●​ the work that has already been done in this area or on this specific initiative.

Step 3: Define a Working Process

Set up a clear process by which the Council and City staff can work through details and check in on those 2-3 priority initiatives. This will ensure that program development and decision points are transparent and in line with Council priorities. The process may include directing work through a specific committee or an Ad Hoc committee, as well as the Finance Committee, and/or the formation of a working group.
