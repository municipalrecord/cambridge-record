RE: Statement of Attempts to Obtain Abutter Signatures for Curb Cut Application

To whom it may concern, This curb cut application is being requested as part of the redevelopment of the Cambridge Housing Authorities' Jefferson Park Federal property at 266 Rindge Ave in Cambridge. In accordance with requirements for abutter signatures required for a curb cut application, the attached letter, plan and City of Cambridge Application for Driveway Cuts and Openings Form were mailed to all abutting property owners requesting their signature on November 22, 2021.

123 letters in total were mailed to owners of the abutting properties, according to the address indicated on the Cambridge Assessor's website. This included the 7 properties on Rindge Ave directly across from Jefferson Park Federal (263, 265, 271, 277, 281 and 289 Rindge Ave), the 100 condominium owners at The Brickworks (310, 312, 314, 316, 318, and 320 Rindge Ave, the North Cambridge Catholic Cemetery, and the 15 condominium owners at 147 Sherman St., which abuts the Southeast corner of the property.

Signatures were not provided for the adjacent Jefferson Park State property, as it is managed by the CHA and is owned by a CHA affiliate, Returned forms were requested no later than December 10th, 2021. As of the 10" of December, only two forms have been returned, with a total of three individual signatures. These forms are attached, and all three indicate their approval of the curb cut request.

Please forward any questions on this curb cut application or on the collection of abutter's signatures to Joe Bednar [phone removed] [email removed]. Sincerely,

Joe Bednar

This office is in receipt of a copy of an application from: Joseph Bednar for a Curb Cut at the location of 266 Rindge Avenue with a Frontage of 275.9', Setback 15', Distance from surrounding structures 12', Length 7', Width 20' and a total Square Footage of 140.

The City Council has directed that all curb cut petitions be submitted to the appropriate Neighborhood Associations for the locality where the curb cut would be made, so that the association may have an opportunity for review, prior to action by the City Council. Please indicate by return mail your approval or disapproval of the petition within twenty-one days from the date of this letter. If the response is "disapproval" please state reasons. I have enclosed a self-addressed stamped envelope to facilitate your reply. As soon as this office has received both the completed application and your neighborhood association response, I will place the petition on the agenda for the next City Council meeting. If I do not receive a response from your neighborhood organization by twenty-one days from the date of this letter, I will place the petition on the agenda for the next City Council meeting.

If your neighborhood association cannot complete its review by twenty-one days from today, you may extend the reply time another seven days by requesting an extension by letter to me with a copy to the petitioner.
