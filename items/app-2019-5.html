APP 2019-5

A curb cut at 32 Vineyard Street

How it started
Feb 4, 2019 · Requested by Nicholas Faillace: with sign-offs already in hand from Inspectional Services, Traffic, the Historical Commission, and Public Works.
What happened
Feb 4, 2019 · Held by charter right the city's note: “EXERCISED BY VICE MAYOR DEVEREUX”
Feb 11, 2019 · Tabled on motion of Councillor Zondervan. The minutes of Feb 11, 2019 print “TABLED ON AN AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF 8 MEMBERS ON MOTION OF COUNCILLOR ZONDERVAN” (p. 5).
Feb 11, 2019 · A communication referred to this application The minutes of Feb 11, 2019 print COF 2019 #7, the Clerk’s transmittal of “a communication from Vice Mayor Devereux regarding the curb cut application located at 32 Vineyard Street”, “RESULT: REFERRED TO THE CALENDAR ITEM #1” (p. 12). Calendar Item #1 that night is this application.
Feb 25, 2019 · A late communication referred to this application The minutes of Feb 25, 2019 print “Councillor Mallon moved suspension of the rules in order to introduce a late Communication and Reports from Other City Officers” and “on a voice vote the rules were _ Suspended”; that communication, COF 2019 #9, is the certified arborist’s letter (p. 8). Then “Mayor McGovern moved that the late Communication and Reports from Other City Officials be referred to Calendar Item # One” and “RESULT: REFERRED TO THE CALENDAR ITEM #1” (p. 9). Calendar Item #1 that night is this application.
Feb 25, 2019 · The question came on taking from the table the application for the curb cut, and on a voice vote of seven the matter was taken from the table by voice vote The minutes of Feb 25, 2019, p. 9, record it. A voice vote records the outcome, not individual positions.
Feb 25, 2019 · ✅ Curb cut granted. (Order adopted)
