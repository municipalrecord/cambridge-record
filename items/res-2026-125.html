Saint Peter’s School was founded in 1901 by the School Sisters of Notre Dame as a Catholic elementary school associated with Saint Peter’s Parish and dedicated to serving Irish and Italian immigrant families in Cambridge; and For 125 years, Saint Peter’s School has stood as a pillar of the Cambridge community, offering a comprehensive and nurturing education to generations of children, regardless of demographic or economic background; and Saint Peter’s School has maintained an enduring reputation for excellence in education, guided by its founding traditions, its commitment to innovation, and the service of its talented faculty; and The devoted teachers serve students from pre-kindergarten through eighth grade, providing rigorous academics, opportunities for character formation, and an enriching, supportive, and accepting learning environment; and The students at St.

Peter’s School engage both inside and outside the classroom through athletics, student government, theater, social justice ministry, and a wide array of extracurricular activities, developing into capable, globally minded young people prepared for lifelong academic success; and Saint Peter’s School continues to offer an affordable, high-quality educational option in the face of competition from larger private institutions, remaining a valued resource to families in Cambridge and across Greater Boston; and The student body reflects the diversity of Cambridge itself, bringing together children from numerous ethnic, cultural, and socioeconomic backgrounds all united in a shared commitment to learning; and Saint Peter’s School remains dedicated to ensuring that all families seeking a faith- based, academically strong education have access to its programs and community; now therefore be it That the City Council, in recognition of the extraordinary contributions to the City of Cambridge and its residents, hereby celebrates and honors Saint Peter’s School on the joyous occasion of its 125th anniversary; and be it further That the Council extends its gratitude to the Saint Peter’s School faculty, staff, families, students, and alumni for their unwavering dedication, service, and commitment to educational excellence; and be it further That a suitably engrossed copy of this resolution be presented to Saint Peter’s School in commemoration of this milestone and in appreciation of its ongoing role as a cherished and enduring institution in the Cambridge community on behalf of the entire City Council.

A true copy; ATTEST:- Paula M. Crane,

Interim City Clerk

That the City Council go on record celebrating and honoring Saint Peter’s School on the joyous occasion of its 125th anniversary.
