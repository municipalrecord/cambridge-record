Extreme Audio Range & Penetration

Captures normal-volume human conversations uo to 50 feet away, including audio penetrating from inside private building walls (Commonwealth v. Denison). 24/7 Always-On Surveillance Not a passive acoustic system. Mircophones activetly listen, analyze, and record 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

No Democratic Consent

A highly invasive surveillance network operating across Cambridge neighborhoods entirely without community consent, public hearings, or democratic oversight.
