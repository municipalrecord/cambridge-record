POR 2018-45

Asked the City Manager to consult with City staff on the feasibility of initiating a formal transit study and action plan of the Alewife area in response to unanimous concerns of the Envision Alewife Working Group regarding the need for a pedestrian/bicycle/shuttle bridge from the Quadrangle area along Concord Avenue across the railroad tracks to the Triangle area on Cambridgepark Drive and a commuter rail stop which would be critical to create neighborhoods that are safe and healthy for residents and businesses

How it started
Feb 26, 2018 · Proposed by Vice Mayor Devereux, Mayor Siddiqui, and Councillors Kelley and Carlone: a formal request from the Council to the administration.
What happened
Feb 26, 2018 · ✅ Order adopted. The Council's request went to the City Manager.
