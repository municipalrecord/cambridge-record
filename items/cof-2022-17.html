This approach has become the blueprint for addressing climate change through a lens of equity at the scale and pace called for by science, and our nation's young people have risen up through the Sunrise Movement to demand its implementation. Frustrating gridlock has prevented meaningful progress even under President Biden, however, and progressive cities like Cambridge cannot afford to wait for top-down solutions.

In 2021 I worked with Councillor Carlone, Sunrise Cambridge, and others to introduce the Green New Deal Zoning Petition.

This proposal offered one of the first municipal adaptations of the Green New Deal concept by establishing a mechanism for emissions accounting during the special permit process, charging new commercial developments for all of their emissions, and using the money to fund emissions reductions projects and green jobs training programs that would prepare unemployed or underemployed Cambridge residents and young people in particular for good-paying green careers.

This zoning petition was discussed by the Planning Board on June 29, 2021 and by the Ordinance Committee on September 21, 2021 before the previous council let it expire without taking action. The feedback we received during last year's process has substantially informed our resubmission of the proposal. The Planning Board told us that aspects of the proposal related to charging for emissions and promoting green jobs did not belong in zoning.

The Law Department cautioned us to avoid creating legal interdependencies within the municipal code. And CDD reminded us that future emissions reductions should be regulated through the Building Energy Use Ordinance rather than the special permit process. In response to this feedback and with support from several colleagues on the Council, I've retooled the proposal as a three-part package which accomplishes all of the original objectives in a way that is more compatible with our municipal code.

Part 1: Emissions Accounting Zoning Petition.

This petition amends Article 22 of the zoning code (Green Building Requirements) to clarify and standardize what must be provided through the existing emissions accounting requirement, including an embodied emissions calculation for new non-residential buildings.
