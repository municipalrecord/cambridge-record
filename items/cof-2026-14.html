Current Petition

Amend Institutional Use Regulations to permit “Dover-protected” uses

as-of-right in all zoning districts in order to be consistent with state law: • Religious uses • Public, religious, and non-profit educational uses • Child care uses (including home-based child care) Necessary to align current Zoning Ordinance with state law
