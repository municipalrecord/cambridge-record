BACKGROUND The environment for this study was a two-block-square radius centered on the colony. This area is a mixed density urban neighborhood in Chicago, Illinois at approximately 6300 S Cicero and adjacent to Midway airport. The neighborhood included:

Single-family residences

• Multi-family apartments • Alleys, backyards and garages in various stages of maintenance • Small businesses on the ground floors of apartment buildings • Midway International Airport with hangars, storage, open fields and runways • One abandoned and boarded up multi-story residence The area is an ideal habitat for feral cats.
