APP 2019-42

One internal illuminating projecting sign and four awnings extending 4ft. over sidewalk at 1739 Massachusetts Avenue

How it started
Jun 3, 2019 · Requested by East Boston Savings Bank: a request that needs the Council’s permission.
What happened
Jun 3, 2019 · Suspension of the rules to bring this application back before the Council carried The minutes of Jun 3, 2019 print “Councillor Carlone moved suspension of the rules in order to bring Application and Petitions # 1 back before the City Council” and “on a voice vote the rules were - Suspended” (p. 9).
Jun 3, 2019 · Held by charter right exercised by Councillor Carlone; the item returns at the next meeting
Jun 10, 2019 · Placed on Unfinished Business on motion of Councillor Carlone. The minutes of Jun 10, 2019 print “Councillor Carlone moved to place this item on Unfinished Business ... On an affirmative vote the item was - RESULT: PLACED ON UNFINISHED BUSINESS ON MOTION OF COUNCILLOR CARLONE” (p. 19). The minutes then print COF 2019 #34, which Councillor Mallon moved to bring forward as “a response to this item” under Calendar Item # 2, APP 2019 #47, the Inman Square banners, and which they print “REFERRED TO THE CALENDAR #1” (pp. 19-20). Calendar Item # 1 that night is this application. The two lines differ on which item the communication went to; the record does not say why, and the question is open.
Jun 17, 2019 · No action taken on the Calendar The minutes of Jun 17, 2019 print “Councillor Carlone stated that the sign has been installed. He stated that his concern is if the building is vacant the sign has to come down.” and “RESULT: NO ACTION TAKEN” under it on the Calendar, Unfinished Business (pp. 14-15).
Jun 24, 2019 · No action taken on the Calendar The minutes of Jun 24, 2019 print “RESULT: NO ACTION TAKEN” under it on the Calendar, Unfinished Business (p. 6).
Jul 30, 2019 · ✅ Sign request approved as amended. Permission granted. (Order adopted as amended) The minutes of Jul 30, 2019 print “ORDER ADOPTED AS AMENDED” and no count, roll call or voice vote (p. 11).
What’s next
🚪 End of the line: permission granted.
