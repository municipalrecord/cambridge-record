The Manager's report back on using pavement markings that are less dangerous to cyclists and pedestrians

How it started
Submitted by Robert W. Healy, City Manager: his response to the Council’s order about a report on using pavement markings that are less dangerous to cyclists and pedestrians (AR 07-106).
What happened

The clerk's record shows no vote or disposition against this item. Some are received and filed without one, and a roundtable takes no votes at all. If the city's record shows otherwise, that is exactly what an audit should catch.
