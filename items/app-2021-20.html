The Honorable, Cambridge City Council, The Central Square Business Improvement District is working in partnership with Cambridge Rindge and Latin to help celebrate the graduating class of 2021. Last year the council was kind enough to approve the banners posted throughout Central, Inman, Kendall and around the high school. Supporting documents are attached to this letter of support. Please be in touch with any questions or concerns . • Best Regards,

Michael Monestime

Executive Director, CSBID
