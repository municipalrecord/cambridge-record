How it started
Feb 27, 2017 · Requested by Reviewed.com: with sign-offs already in hand from Inspectional Services, Public Works, Community Development, and the abutters.
What happened
Feb 27, 2017 · Tabled set aside by vote
Mar 6, 2017 · Suspension of the rules to bring forward the City Clerk’s communication on these signs carried The minutes of Mar 6, 2017 print “Councillor Devereux moved suspension of the rules to dispense with the regular order of business and bring forward Communication and Reports from City Officers # 2” and “on a voice vote the rules were – Suspended.” (p. 12).
Mar 6, 2017 · Documents substituted The minutes of Mar 6, 2017 print “The question came on substituting the original documents in Communications and Report from City Officers # 2 for the documents in Calendar Item # 6 – and on a voice vote the documents were – Substituted.” (p. 13).
Mar 6, 2017 · ✅ Sign request approved. Permission granted. (Order adopted) The minutes of Mar 6, 2017 print “ORDER ADOPTED FOR 2 SIGNS ON A VOICE VOTE OF EIGHT MEMBERS” (p. 13).
