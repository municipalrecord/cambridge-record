COF 2019-35

Memorandum fro Vice Mayor Devereux regarding a special public meeting for the City Clerk interviews

Officer Communicationfrom Interim City Clerk Paula M. Crane
How it started
Jun 10, 2019 · Filed by Interim City Clerk Paula M. Crane, passing along memorandum fro Vice Mayor Devereux regarding a special public meeting for the City Clerk interviews. Any city officer can send the Council a communication.
What happened
Jun 10, 2019 · Placed on Unfinished Business on motion of Vice Mayor Devereux. The minutes of Jun 10, 2019 print “Vice Mayor Devereux moved to place this communication on Unfinished Business. The item was - RESULT: PLACED ON UNFINISHED BUSINESS ON MOTION OF VICE MAYOR DEVEREUX” (p. 35).
Jun 17, 2019 · A further vote on it carried 8–0. Mayor McGovern moved suspension of the rules in order to bring forward Calendar Item # 5, this communication (minutes p. 7).

Jun 17, 2019 · 📁 Communication placed on file The minutes of Jun 17, 2019 print the election of the City Clerk on this communication, then “The communication was - RESULT: COMMUNICATION PLACED ON FILE” (p. 9), with no count or roll call on the filing. The nine names below are the election’s: “ANTHONY I. WILSON ELECTED CITY CLERK ON BALLOT #1 9 - 0 - 0” (p. 8). On Ballot #1 Councillor Carlone voted for Timothy Phelan and then changed his vote to Anthony Wilson; the final vote lists all nine members for Anthony I. Wilson.
