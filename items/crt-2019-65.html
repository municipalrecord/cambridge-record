September 23, 2019: on Councillor Toomey's motion to suspend the rules to go back to this report, then on the table, Councillor Mallon answers “Yes” and the clerk repeats “No”; the clerk announces “Motion fails. Four in favor and five against.” (transcript pp.63-64). Counted as the members answered, the roll is five in favor and four against; as the clerk counted, four in favor and five against. The roll is shown as the clerk repeated it. The law in force was Plan E as enacted, with the Council's rules of 2018-2019, whose Rule 36A reads: “Any of the foregoing rules with the exception of Rule 20 may be suspended at any meeting by a two-thirds yea and nay vote of the entire membership of the City Council” (minutes of January 29, 2018, p.24). Six votes were needed, so the motion fails on either count. The Mayor then says “it's been disposed of since we did not suspend the rules.” The city's meeting video has not yet been checked.
