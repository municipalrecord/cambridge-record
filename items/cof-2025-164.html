A memorandum regarding COF 2025 #154 in Council December 8, 2025 regarding pending Awaiting Reports that it wished to be carried forward to the newly elected City Council for their consideration in the next legislative terms

What happened

The clerk's record shows no vote or disposition against this item. Some are received and filed without one, and a roundtable takes no votes at all. If the city's record shows otherwise, that is exactly what an audit should catch.

INTERIM CITY CLERK

Dear Honorable Mayor E. Dennis Simmons, In accordance with COF 2025 #154, adopted on December 8, 2025, I recommend that the City Council take an affirmative vote to carry over pending Awaiting Reports to the next City Council. Attached is a list of Awaiting Reports that Councillors would like sent to the next City Council for its consideration. The recommended action would be a motion to forward the Awaiting Reports that appear on the list compiled by the City Clerk, to the next City Council. This motion would be subject to amendments made on the Council floor. Best, Dancrane Paula M. Crane

Interim City Clerk

CITY HALL, 795 MASSACHUSETTS AVENUE, CAMBRIDGE, MASSACHUSETTS 02139
