Agenda Items ▸ Awaiting Reports

AR 19-106

Asked the City Manager to report on conducting City directed environmental testing on the Sullivan Courthouse building and water in basement, to determine the risk posed to the public, and provide a timeline of completion and to establish an operational understanding directly with DCAMM officials and ask for a state designee for communication/coordination on how the building will be secured and monitored

How it started
The Council asked this of the City Manager via POR 2019 #277, adopted Sep 9, 2019, sponsored by Councillors Toomey and Mallon.
What happened
Sep 16, 2019 · 📨 Answered: the report came back and the Council accepted it. (Report received and accepted) The clerk’s Awaiting Report list of Sep 16, 2019 prints “REPORT RECEIVED AND ACCEPTED RESPONSE RECEIVED AT HEARING ON 9.18.2019” (minutes p. 33). The clerk’s Awaiting Report lists of October 7, 2019 (p.33) and October 21, 2019 (p.28) still print 19-106 “(O-5) from 9/9/2019”, with no result, and on December 16, 2019 Councillor Toomey named it among the requests to carry over to the next Council: “I have them by name. 19-106, 19-128, and 19-130.” (transcript p.66). The records differ, and the question is open.
What’s next
🚪 End of the line: request closed.
