SoundThinking’s Acoustic Gunshot Detection Technology (“ShotSpotter”) pursuant

to the Surveillance Technology Ordinance, Section 2.128.060; and Section 2.128.060 sets forth clear and unambiguous procedures that the City Council must follow before voting to approve or disapprove the use of surveillance technology; and The City Council did not follow the required procedures outlined in Section 2.128.060(c) before voting to disapprove ShotSpotter; and Section 2.128.060(c) requires, as a condition precedent to any such vote, that the City Council examine the information contained in the Annual Surveillance Report; and The City Council did not conduct such an examination; and Section 2.128.060(c) further requires the City Council to determine, based on the Annual Surveillance Report, whether the benefits of continued use of the technology outweigh its financial and operational costs; and The City Council did not make such a determination; and Section 2.128.060(c) also requires the City Council to determine whether reasonable safeguards exist to address concerns regarding privacy, civil liberties, and civil rights; and The City Council did not make such a determination; and Section 2.128.060(c) provides that only after making these determinations may the City Council consider one of the following actions: 1.

Recommend modifications to the Surveillance Use Policy to the City

Manager; 2. Request a report from the City Manager on steps taken to address concerns; or 3. Disapprove further use of the technology; and The City Council did not make the required threshold determinations and did not consider these options in the manner prescribed; and

On May 11, 2026, the City Council adopted Policy Order 2026-98, which states that

the Annual Report was reviewed by the Public Safety Committee rather than the full City Council, and identifies concerns about the technology but does not make the determinations required by Section 2.128.060(c); and These actions are inconsistent with the requirements of the Surveillance Technology Ordinance; now therefore be it That the City Council shall comply with the procedures outlined in Section 2.128.060 of the Surveillance Technology Ordinance; and be it further That the June 1, 2026, vote to disapprove ShotSpotter be declared null and void; and be it further That the City Council shall complete the required review and determinations prescribed by Section 2.128.060(c) before taking any future vote regarding the continued use of ShotSpotter or any other surveillance technology.

A true copy; ATTEST:- Paula M. Crane,

Interim City Clerk
