City Council · Inaugural Meeting, January 1, 2024

The inaugural meeting has no agenda. The City Clerk presides until the Mayor is elected; the council elects a Mayor and a Vice Chair, then adopts its rules. In 2024 the rules were adopted provisionally and the meeting adjourned, each on a voice vote.

Elections of the Mayor and the Vice Chair

Each member names a choice as the clerk calls the roll, and may change it before the chair declares the ballot final.

  1. Mayor, ballot 1. First count: Councillor Simmons 3; Councillor Siddiqui 3; Councillor McGovern 2; Councillor Nolan 1. After changes: Councillor Simmons 4; Councillor Siddiqui 3; Councillor Azeem 1; Councillor Nolan 1. No one elected on this ballot. Minutes p. 5.

    • Councillor McGovern: McGovern, changed to Simmons
