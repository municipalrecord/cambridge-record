Every councillor since 1942

54 people have sat on the Cambridge City Council under Plan E. Nine are elected by proportional representation every odd November and seat themselves the following January, and they choose the mayor from among themselves.

Sorted by papers filed. The index measures each councillor against the others on their own council, so a career in the sixties and one in the twenties can be read in the same column: 100 is the median member of that council. Raw counts reflect how much of each era's record survives; the index largely does not. Where this comes from.
