    The clerk first recorded Councillor Carlone as present ("eight in favor, one present"). Councillor Carlone then said: "When I said 'present', I thought we were coming into the meeting again. I- I- I'm- I am for it." The clerk said: "Councillor Carlone, you've been recorded as a yes" (transcript, page 77). The clerk gave no new count.
