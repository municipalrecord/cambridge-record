Votes on the council’s own business

Votes the council took that night that were not on any agenda item: accepting the minutes of earlier meetings, suspending the rules, closing public comment, adopting its rules, adjourning. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them, except that a member of the public named in a motion is not named here.

  1. Mayor McGovern made a motion to move to executive session and the roll was called and resulted as follows: · 5–0

    Voted yes (5): Dennis J. Carlone, Jan Devereux, Craig A. Kelley, E. Denise Simmons, Marc C. McGovern

    Absent: Alanna Mallon, Sumbul Siddiqui, Timothy J. Toomey, Quinton Zondervan

    The minutes list Councillor Toomey among those present at the meeting (p. 2), print Councillor Toomey absent on the roll to move to executive session (p. 2), and record that the meeting adjourned on Councillor Toomey’s motion (p. 3). The records differ; Councillor Toomey may have arrived after the roll. The question is open.

  2. Mayor McGovern opened public comment at 1:34 PM. No one appeared. Public comment was closed on a motion by Vice Mayor Devereux.

    The minutes of Jun 12, 2019, p. 2.

  3. The Special City Council meeting adjourned at 1:35 PM on motion of Councillor Toomey.

    The minutes of Jun 12, 2019, p. 3.
