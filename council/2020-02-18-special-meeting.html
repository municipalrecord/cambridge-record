What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them. Source: the verbatim transcript of the special meeting (the city’s copy).

  1. No roll call was taken at this meeting; every vote was a voice vote. Councillor Simmons was not yet there at the call to order and joined shortly; the attendance table marks her present with no arrival time. Vice Mayor Mallon and Councillor McGovern were absent. The Council accepted two Ordinance Committee reports and placed them on file, heard public comment, and amended the petition by substituting the Ordinance Committee's January 30, 2020 language. A motion by Councillor Nolan to add the Community Development Department's suggested language to Section 20.5(4.3) failed. Councillor Zondervan's amendment to insert "As of Right," was adopted, Councillor Toomey recorded as no. Councillor Carlone's further written amendments were not voted on; the Clerk said the petition would expire on March 10 and the notice had already gone to the newspaper. The petition was passed to a Second Reading on a motion by Councillor Simmons, Councillor Toomey recorded as no. The council adjourned on a motion by Councillor Toomey.
