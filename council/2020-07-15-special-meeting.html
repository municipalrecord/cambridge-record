What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them. Source: the verbatim transcript of the special meeting (the city’s copy).

  1. The Mayor said the meeting was held under the Governor's March 12, 2020 order allowing remote participation, with a Zoom link for public comment. At the opening roll five councillors were present (Mallon, Nolan, Toomey, Zondervan, Siddiqui); Councillors Simmons and Sobrinho-Wheeler were brought on shortly after and marked present. Councillors Carlone and McGovern were absent. One member of the public spoke on shared streets. The City Manager and staff reported on summer programs for young people, Shared Streets and outdoor dining, testing and public health, then answered questions. The council closed public comment on a roll call, 7-0 with two absent, on a motion by Councillor Zondervan, and adjourned on a roll call, 6-0 with three absent, on a motion by Councillor Toomey. Councillor Sobrinho-Wheeler was called absent at the adjournment roll.

  2. On a motion by Councillor Zondervan to close Public Comment · 7–0, 2 absent

    Voted yes (7): Mallon, Nolan, Simmons, Sobrinho-Wheeler, Toomey, Zondervan, Siddiqui

    Absent: Carlone, McGovern

  3. On Councillor Toomey's motion to adjourn · 6–0, 3 absent

    Voted yes (6): Mallon, Nolan, Simmons, Toomey, Zondervan, Siddiqui

    Absent: Carlone, McGovern, Sobrinho-Wheeler
