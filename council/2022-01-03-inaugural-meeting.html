Transcript of the city’s video (unofficial)

Unofficial: our best attempt to fill the gap the city’s missing minutes leave. We listened to the city’s video of this meeting and wrote down each vote the council took, in the speakers’ own words where we could, with the time in the video. A name we could not hear is marked unheard. These votes are not the city’s record: they are counted in none of this site’s tallies, councillor pages or statistics. Source: the city’s video of the meeting.

  1. Election of Mayor for 2022 and 2023: 'please state the name of the councillor that you vote to elect as the mayor' · Siddiqui elected Mayor, 9 votes ('Currently I have nine votes for Councillor Sumbul Siddiqui'); no member changed a vote

    Taken by ballot by roll call (each member states a name). At 0:50:46 in the city’s video.

    Azeem: Siddiqui; Carlone: Siddiqui; Mallon: Siddiqui; McGovern: Siddiqui; Nolan: Siddiqui (Nolan is called after Toner, out of alphabetical order, as heard); Siddiqui: Siddiqui (Call and answer overlap; heard once clearly; the spoken total of nine confirms it); Simmons: Siddiqui; Toner: Siddiqui; Zondervan: Siddiqui.

    The presiding voice is not named on the audio here; a member addresses it as 'Mr. Clerk' at 1:04:55. The spoken total of nine at 0:51:56.

  2. Election of Vice Mayor: 'when I say your name, please state the name of the councillor that you would like to elect to the position of vice mayor' · First call of the roll: Mallon 2, Simmons 3, Nolan 4 (the presiding voice at 1:02:09); no one elected yet

    Taken by ballot by roll call (each member states a name). At 1:00:39 in the city’s video.

    Azeem: Mallon; Carlone: Nolan; Mallon: Mallon; McGovern: Simmons; Nolan: Nolan; Siddiqui: Nolan; Simmons: Simmons; Toner: Simmons; Zondervan: Nolan.

    Each answer repeated by the presiding voice; the counts heard match these nine answers.

  3. Vice Mayor ballot, changes of vote after the roll ('Would any councillor at this time like to make a correction or change to their vote?') · Mallon elected Vice Mayor with 5 votes; Simmons 2, Nolan 2 (the presiding voice at 1:05:08 and 1:05:23)

    Taken by ballot by roll call, changes of vote. At 1:02:32 in the city’s video.

    Siddiqui: Mallon (changed from Nolan) (After this change the presiding voice announced 'three votes for Alanna Mallon, two votes for Councilor Simmons, and four votes for Councilor Nolan' (1:02:44); by the answers heard it was 3-3-3. After Zondervan's change the spoken count wavered (1:03:14 to 1:04:09) and settled on Mallon 4, Simmons 3, Nolan 2 at 1:04:43, which matches the answers heard); Zondervan: Mallon (changed from Nolan); McGovern: Mallon (changed from Simmons).

    Final votes by name, from the roll and the three changes: Mallon (Azeem, Mallon, McGovern, Siddiqui, Zondervan), Simmons (Simmons, Toner), Nolan (Carlone, Nolan).

  4. 'I'd like to move that the city council provisionally adopt the rules of order for the 2022-2023 year' · Carried, 9-0: 'Rules are provisionally adopted unanimously' (1:09:40)

    Taken by roll call. At 1:09:04 in the city’s video.

    Azeem: Yes (First answer heard as 'I agree'; the presiding voice explains the choices (yes, nay, present) and Azeem answers 'Yes'); Carlone: Yes; Mallon: Yes; McGovern: Yes; Nolan: Yes; Siddiqui: Yes; Simmons: Yes; Toner: Yes; Zondervan: Yes.

    Mover not named on the audio (unheard).

  5. Motion to adjourn ('So moved') until 5:30 p.m. on 10 January 2022 · Adjourned: 'All those in favor say aye. Aye!'

    Taken by voice vote. At 1:16:14 in the city’s video.

    A roll call on adjournment was started at 1:11:02 and stopped for a musical selection; the voice vote follows at 1:16:14 to 1:16:24. Mover unheard. No call for opposed is clearly heard.
