What the council voted on

  1. The Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Toner who made a motion to close public comment. · 6–0, 3 absent

    Voted yes (6): Patricia Nolan, Sumbul Siddiqui, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Paul F. Toner, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons

    Absent: Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Ayesha M. Wilson

  2. The Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Siddiqui who made a motion to go into Executive Session for the purpose of conducting a strategy session in preparation for negotiations with nonunion personnel, specifically the City Manager. · 6–0, 3 absent

    Voted yes (6): Patricia Nolan, Sumbul Siddiqui, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Paul F. Toner, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons

    Absent: Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Ayesha M. Wilson

  3. The chair noted that the council would not reconvene in open session. In executive session from about 3:07 p.m.; all nine answered its roll. The two votes below were taken in executive session, which adjourned at about 3:24 p.m.

  4. The vote of the meetingThe Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Toner who made a motion to offer the City Manager a 4-year contract with the salary as stipulated in prior meetings and to ask [the council’s outside counsel] to present the terms to the City Manager and if he is in agreement, to draft a final contract for discussion in Executive Session as soon as possible. · 5–4

    Voted yes (5): Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Paul F. Toner, Catherine Zusy

    Voted no (4): Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Sumbul Siddiqui, Ayesha M. Wilson, E. Denise Simmons

  5. The Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Toner who made a motion to adjourn. · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Sumbul Siddiqui, Paul F. Toner, Ayesha M. Wilson, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons
