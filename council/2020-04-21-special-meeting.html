The clerk’s record lists no agenda items for this meeting; the call is the agenda.Special Meeting · 4:00 PM · Sullivan Chamber, with most councillors taking part remotely

Who answered the roll

Present (7): Dennis J. Carlone, Alanna Mallon, Patricia Nolan, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Timothy J. Toomey, Quinton Zondervan, Sumbul Siddiqui

Arrived late (2): Marc C. McGovern, E. Denise Simmons

What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them. Source: the verbatim transcript of the special meeting (the city’s copy).

  1. At the opening roll Councillors McGovern and Simmons were absent; Councillor Carlone and Councillor Sobrinho-Wheeler were first called absent, then answered present. The Mayor said a quorum was present and most councillors were taking part remotely. Councillor McGovern arrived at 4:16 p.m. and Councillor Simmons at 6:01 p.m. The City Manager, the Police Commissioner, the Public Health Commissioner and officials from the City, the Cambridge Health Alliance and a shelter operator reported on the shelter and quarantine sites at the War Memorial, the Mayor's Disaster Relief Fund, housing, testing and hospital capacity, then answered councillors' questions. There was no public comment. At about 6:00 p.m. the council extended the meeting 15 minutes on a roll call, 8-0 with Councillor Simmons absent, on a motion by Councillor Toomey.

  2. A motion by Councillor Toomey to extend the meeting by, let's say, 15 minutes · 8–0, 1 absent

    Voted yes (8): Carlone, Mallon, McGovern, Nolan, Sobrinho-Wheeler, Toomey, Zondervan, Siddiqui

    Absent: Simmons

  3. Councillor McGovern: “We'll move to extend for 15 minutes?” The Clerk: “On the Motion to extend the meeting till 6:30.” · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Carlone, Mallon, McGovern, Nolan, Simmons, Sobrinho-Wheeler, Toomey, Zondervan, Siddiqui

  4. The council adjourned at about 6:30 p.m. on a roll call, 8-0 with Councillor McGovern absent, on a motion by Councillor Toomey.

  5. On a Motion by Councillor Toomey to adjourn · 8–0, 1 absent

    Voted yes (8): Carlone, Mallon, Nolan, Simmons, Sobrinho-Wheeler, Toomey, Zondervan, Siddiqui

    Absent: McGovern
