What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them. Source: the clerk’s record of the roundtable, a timed account of what was said (the city’s copy).

  1. A joint roundtable of the City Council and the School Committee. The City Manager, the School Committee Vice-Chair and the Superintendent presented the FY21 school budget and the district's equity work, and two presenters from a school equity project described it. Councillors then gave comments and questions, and School Committee and student members responded. The Mayor said no votes would be taken and there was no public comment. The Mayor named Councillor McGovern among those present at the opening (p. 1); later, setting the speaking order, she said she hoped he would join by then (p. 13). Councillor Toomey apologized for his “unexpected delay in getting here” (p. 20). The attendance table shows no late times. Councillor Simmons said she had to leave (p. 21). The Mayor extended the meeting 15 minutes by consent. The meeting ended on a motion to adjourn by Councillor McGovern; no vote was recorded.
