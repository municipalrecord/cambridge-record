Votes on the council’s own business

Votes the council took that night that were not on any agenda item: accepting the minutes of earlier meetings, suspending the rules, closing public comment, adopting its rules, adjourning. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them, except that a member of the public named in a motion is not named here.

  2. Councillor McGovern's motion to adjourn at 11:10 a.m. · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Ayah A. Al-Zubi, Burhan Azeem, Timothy R. Flaherty, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Sumbul Siddiqui, E. Denise Simmons, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Catherine Zusy
