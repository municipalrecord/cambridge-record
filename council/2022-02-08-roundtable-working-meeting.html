What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them. Source: the verbatim transcript of the roundtable (the city’s copy).

  1. The City Council and the School Committee met jointly. At the opening roll six councillors were present (Councillors Azeem and McGovern and Vice Mayor Mallon were marked absent); Councillors Azeem and McGovern were announced as joined right after the roll, and Vice Mayor Mallon joined later. Four School Committee members were present at the roll and the record notes quorum for the School Committee. The Superintendent and the school chief financial officer presented the school budget overview, enrollment projections, the Superintendent's entry plan findings, the budget guidelines and the FY23 budget timeline, and councillors and School Committee members asked questions. The meeting adjourned at about 7:00 p.m.

  2. Mayor Siddiqui: on a motion by Councillor Simmons to adjourn, we'll do a roll call. · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Burhan Azeem, Dennis J. Carlone, Alanna Mallon, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, E. Denise Simmons, Paul F. Toner, Quinton Zondervan, Sumbul Siddiqui
