A year from 2002 on opens on its meetings, with agendas, minutes and roll calls: 2016 onward from the city's own portals, earlier years rebuilt from what the Internet Archive captured of the city's first database before it went offline. Before 2002 the record is the City Clerk's annual index, which lists the papers the council acted on (actions, not meetings), most linking to the city's scan of the document. Where two systems cover the same year, the year goes to whichever holds more of it. Where this comes from sets out all three.
