What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them. Source: the verbatim transcript of the roundtable (the city’s copy).

  1. The Mayor called the meeting to order, saying it was a roundtable held in a virtual format with no public comment. At the opening roll Councillors Azeem, Carlone, Zondervan, Vice Mayor Mallon and Mayor Siddiqui were present; Councillors McGovern, Nolan and Simmons were marked absent; Councillor Toner was in the meeting as an attendee waiting to be moved over, “he has his hand up” (p. 4). Councillors McGovern and Nolan then joined, and all three confirmed they were present and audible (p. 6); the record does not say when Councillor Simmons joined. A professional registered parliamentarian led the training. The council took a practice vote on a made-up motion as part of the exercise, and a five-minute stand at ease ran until 7:05. The Mayor declared the meeting adjourned without a motion or vote, at about 8:00 p.m.
