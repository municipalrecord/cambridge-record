What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them. Source: the clerk’s minutes of the executive session (the city’s copy).

  1. Called to order at about 9:15 p.m. The City Manager and his team briefed the council on a possible purchase of a building on Massachusetts Avenue to house City departments. All councillors expressed support for the City Manager to pursue the purchase. The session adjourned at about 10:15 p.m.

  2. A motion was made to adjourn the Executive Session and the roll was called. · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Burhan Azeem, Dennis J. Carlone, Alanna Mallon, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, E. Denise Simmons, Paul F. Toner, Quinton Zondervan, Sumbul Siddiqui
