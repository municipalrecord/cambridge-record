City Council · Inaugural Meeting, January 6, 2020

The city has not published minutes for this meeting. The city’s meeting portal lists this meeting and no agenda, and holds no minutes for it. This page shows what the city published, and adds our own reading of the city’s video, marked unofficial.

Transcript of the city’s video (unofficial)

The city’s portal holds no agenda for this meeting. It lists the meeting as the inaugural meeting, 10:00 AM, Sullivan Chamber.

Transcript of the city’s video (unofficial)

Unofficial: our best attempt to fill the gap the city’s missing minutes leave. We listened to the city’s video of this meeting and wrote down each vote the council took, in the speakers’ own words where we could, with the time in the video. A name we could not hear is marked unheard. These votes are not the city’s record: they are counted in none of this site’s tallies, councillor pages or statistics. Source: the city’s video of the meeting.

  1. Election of Mayor, ballot number one: 'each member, as his or her name is called, will indicate his or her choice for the office of mayor' · Siddiqui elected Mayor on the first ballot, 9 votes; no member changed a vote

    Taken by ballot by roll call (each member states a name). At 0:51:09 in the city’s video.

    Carlone: Siddiqui; Mallon: Siddiqui; McGovern: Siddiqui; Nolan: Siddiqui; Siddiqui: Siddiqui; Simmons: Siddiqui; Sobrinho-Wheeler: Siddiqui; Toomey: Siddiqui; Zondervan: Siddiqui.

    The presiding voice is not named on the audio here; a member addresses the chair as 'Mr. Clerk' at 1:13:59. Each answer is repeated as 'recorded for Sumbul Siddiqui'; the presiding voice declares the ballot closed at 0:53:05 without speaking a total.

  2. Election of Vice Mayor, ballot number one · Mallon elected Vice Mayor on the first ballot, 9 votes; no member changed a vote

    Taken by ballot by roll call (each member states a name). At 1:04:03 in the city’s video.

    Carlone: Mallon; Mallon: Mallon; McGovern: Mallon (McGovern's first answer is cut off ('Come... sorry'); it is repeated as Mallon at 1:04:33); Nolan: Mallon; Siddiqui: Mallon; Simmons: Mallon; Sobrinho-Wheeler: Mallon; Toomey: Mallon; Zondervan: Mallon.

    The presiding voice repeats each answer as 'casts (his/her) vote for Alanna Mallon'; result declared at 1:05:36 without a spoken total.

  3. 'I would move that we would provisionally adopt the city council rules in the hope that the mayor, as time permits, will appoint an ad hoc committee to review the rules of the city council for permanent adoption' · Carried on the motion as quoted: 'The ayes have it'

    Taken by voice vote. At 1:14:00 in the city’s video.

    'On the motion of Councillor Simmons, all those in favor say aye' is heard at 1:14:23, and the speaker is not named; at 1:13:50: 'The chair would entertain a motion to adopt the 2018-2019 rules and orders of the city council for the 2020-2021 legislative year'; ayes heard, no nays heard.

  4. Motion to adjourn ('So moved') · Adjourned: 'The ayes have it'

    Taken by voice vote. At 1:21:51 in the city’s video.

    Mover not named on the audio (unheard); no nays heard.
