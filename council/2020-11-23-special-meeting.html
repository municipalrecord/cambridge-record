Who answered the roll

Present (9): Dennis J. Carlone, Alanna Mallon, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, E. Denise Simmons, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Timothy J. Toomey, Quinton Zondervan, Sumbul Siddiqui

What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them. Source: the verbatim transcript of the special meeting (the city’s copy).

  1. The Mayor called the meeting to order with a quorum, noting that the Governor's order allows remote participation. The council questioned a panel of epidemiologists and other health and science experts about the winter outlook, indoor dining and schools. Members of the public spoke in public comment. The Vice Mayor moved to adjourn; the roll was Yes-7, with Councillors McGovern and Toomey absent. The Mayor ended by saying the council would return at 5:30, for the regular meeting.

  2. On a Motion to adjourn · 7–0, 2 absent

    Voted yes (7): Carlone, Mallon, Nolan, Simmons, Sobrinho-Wheeler, Zondervan, Siddiqui

    Absent: McGovern, Toomey
