    The clerk did not call Councillor McGovern on the roll call to adjourn and began to announce “Motion passes. Nine…”; Councillor McGovern asked: “What about me?” The clerk finished “… in favor, zero against”, then called Councillor McGovern, who answered yes (transcript, pages 47 and 48). The Council’s Rules of 2020-2021 as amended October 26, 2020, the edition in force that night, say nothing about changing a vote after the roll call; Rule 37 sends any question of parliamentary practice the Rules do not provide for to “Roberts’ Rules of Order”. The transcript records no request for unanimous consent.
