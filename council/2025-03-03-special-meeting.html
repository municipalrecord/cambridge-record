What the council voted on

Every roll call of the meeting, in the clerk’s order. The words are the record’s, as near the roll as it puts them; the council’s outside counsel is not named. Source: the clerk’s minutes of the special meeting (the city’s copy).

  1. The Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Toner who made a motion to close public comment. · 7–0, 2 absent

    Voted yes (7): Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Sumbul Siddiqui, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Paul F. Toner, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons

    Absent: Burhan Azeem, Ayesha M. Wilson

    The clerk’s note: Councillor Azeem was present and asked that his vote be recorded as Yes. The final vote was Yes-8, Absent-1.

  2. The Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Nolan who made a motion to go into Executive Session for the purpose of reviewing and deliberating on the City Manager’s contract. · 6–0, 3 absent

    Voted yes (6): Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Paul F. Toner, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons

    Absent: Burhan Azeem, Sumbul Siddiqui, Ayesha M. Wilson

    The clerk’s note: Councillor Azeem was present and asked that his vote be recorded as Yes. The final vote was Yes-7, Absent-2.

  3. In executive session from about 10:08 a.m.; all nine answered its roll. The vote below adjourned the executive session, at about 10:27 a.m.

  4. The Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Toner who made a motion to adjourn. · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Sumbul Siddiqui, Paul F. Toner, Ayesha M. Wilson, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons

  5. The Chair, Mayor Simmons called the special meeting back to order; all nine answered the roll.

  6. The vote of the meetingThe Chair, Mayor Simmons instructed the City Clerk to call the vote on Councillor Toner’s motion to approve the 4-year employment contract with the City Manager covering the period from September 6, 2025 through September 5, 2029 as presented. · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Sumbul Siddiqui, Paul F. Toner, Ayesha M. Wilson, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons

  7. The Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Toner who made a motion to suspend the rules to allow reconsideration. · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Sumbul Siddiqui, Paul F. Toner, Ayesha M. Wilson, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons

  8. Failed, as intendedThe Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Toner who made a motion to reconsider the vote to approve the 4-year employment contract with the City Manager covering the period from September 6, 2025 through September 5, 2029 as presented. · 0–9

    Voted yes (0): None

    Voted no (9): Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Sumbul Siddiqui, Paul F. Toner, Ayesha M. Wilson, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons

    A motion to reconsider that every member votes down closes the question: the approval cannot be reopened at a later meeting.

  9. The Chair, Mayor Simmons recognized Councillor Toner who made a motion to adjourn. · 9–0

    Voted yes (9): Burhan Azeem, Marc C. McGovern, Patricia Nolan, Jivan Sobrinho-Wheeler, Sumbul Siddiqui, Paul F. Toner, Ayesha M. Wilson, Catherine Zusy, E. Denise Simmons
