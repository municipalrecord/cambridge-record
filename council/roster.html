Roster history

Every Cambridge city council since the 1942 term: 43 terms, 100 names. Cambridge has elected nine councillors by proportional representation at every odd-year election since 1941, seated the following January for two years, and they choose the mayor from among themselves.

The mayor and vice mayor are marked. Every name links to that person's own terms, and every term back to its council. Each term says which of the council's own documents seats it: the inaugural minutes and roll calls from 2002, the clerk's roll-call tally sheets from 1980 to 2001, and the council's index volumes before that. Before 1980 the index mostly prints surnames, and a surname alone is one term's record, not a career: the same surname in two terms may be two people. A name marked disputed is one the record seats and a cross-check does not; it is shown as a question until the primary is re-read.
