Roster history

Every Cambridge city council since the 1942 term: 43 terms, 53 people. Cambridge has elected nine councillors by proportional representation at every odd-year election since 1941, seated the following January for two years, and they choose the mayor from among themselves.

The mayor and vice mayor are marked. Every name links to that person's own terms, and every term back to its council.
