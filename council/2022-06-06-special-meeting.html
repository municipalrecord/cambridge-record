To appoint the City Manager, each member named a finalist as the clerk called the roll. The first count was "Yi-An Huang-6, Cheryl Watson Fisher-3". The clerk asked whether any councillor wished to correct or change a vote; Councillor Toner, and then Councillor McGovern, changed to Yi-An Huang. The clerk: "the current vote is eight votes for Mr. Yi-An Huang and one vote for Cheryl Watson Fisher. Mr. Huang has more than five votes. He's currently appointed as the next city manager" (transcript, pages 102-103). The meeting's own minutes print the final choices only (minutes, page 3).
